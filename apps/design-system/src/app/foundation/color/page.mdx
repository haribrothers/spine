# Color

The palette is deliberately restrained: one accent color (primary) for interactive elements, a warm neutral scale for everything structural, and a reserved four-color semantic palette for financial document status that is never repurposed for anything else. Toggle the theme switcher in the top bar to see the dark-mode values below.

## Surfaces

`background` is the page behind the content; `surface` is everything raised on it — cards, tables, dialogs, drawers, menus, the sidebar and form controls. The two differ in both themes (light: warm grey page, white surface; dark: near-black page, a step lighter surface), so content lifts off the page without shadows (D-131).

## Theme presets

An Owner can switch the whole organisation to Harbour Teal, Plum or Graphite in Settings › Branding (US-SET-09). A preset replaces only Primary and Primary hover, through `[data-brand]` rules in the preset CSS; neutrals and the financial status colours below never change. Values for both modes are in `docs/DESIGN.md` and `tokens.json` (`themePresets`).

## Brand (logo and icons only)

The colours of the Spine logo (D-206): its gradient, the layer colours and the wordmark (Spine blue in light mode, light text in dark). They're outside the theme rules, so a business's theme never recolours Spine's own logo. They appear only in `SpineLogo` and the app icons — never on text, buttons or status.
