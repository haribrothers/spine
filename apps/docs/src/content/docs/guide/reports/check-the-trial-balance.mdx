The Trial Balance lists every account with its debit or credit balance on a date. Total debits always equal total credits, which proves your books balance. Accountants start here at year end.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **View financial statements**.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the report** — Go to **Reports › Trial Balance**, or use the tabs from Profit & Loss.
2. **Choose the date** — Set **As of**.
3. **Read the table** — **Code**, **Account**, **Debit** and **Credit** for every account with a balance, and a **Total** row.
4. **Check the Difference card** — It reads "✓ balanced" when debits equal credits, and the footnote says "Balanced — total debits equal total credits."
5. **Show every account** — Untick **Hide accounts with no balance** to list zero-balance accounts too.
6. **Click an account** — See every journal entry behind its balance.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="reports-trial-balance" alt="The Trial Balance with the Difference card reading balanced and the table of accounts with debit and credit balances" />

Use **Search accounts** to find one account in a long list.

## If the books ever stop balancing

Spine won't post an entry that doesn't balance, so this should never happen. As a safeguard it checks every night. If total debits and credits ever differ:

- The Trial Balance shows a red alert: "Out of balance by *amount*. This has been logged; tell your administrator."
- Owners see a banner on the dashboard, "Your books are out of balance by *amount*. Contact the person who runs Spine.", and get a notification.

The banner goes away after the next check passes.

<Aside type="danger">Don't try to fix an out-of-balance difference with a journal. Tell the person who runs your Spine install: it usually means data was changed outside the app.</Aside>

To export, press **Export** (see [Export or print a report](../export-or-print-a-report/)).
