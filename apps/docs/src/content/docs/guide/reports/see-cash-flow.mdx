You can make a profit and still have less cash at the end of the month: customers haven't paid yet, you bought equipment, or you paid back a loan. The Cash Flow report starts from your cash at the beginning of the period and shows what moved it.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **View financial statements**.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the report** — Go to **Reports › Cash Flow**, or use the tabs from Profit & Loss.
2. **Set the range** — Choose **From** and **To**.
3. **Read the cards** — **Cash at start**, **Net change** (with "▲ more cash than at the start" or "▼ less cash than at the start") and **Cash at end**.
4. **Read the sections** — **Operating activities**: net profit adjusted for changes in what customers owe you, what you owe vendors, tax and advances. **Investing activities**: fixed assets. **Financing activities**: capital, drawings and loans. Then **Net change in cash** and **Cash at end**.
5. **Click a line** — See the accounts behind it. A positive amount brought cash in.

</Steps>

## It ties to your accounts

**Cash at end** equals the total of every bank and cash account on the **To** date. The footnote confirms it: "Cash at end equals the total of your bank and cash accounts". Card accounts are liabilities, not cash, so they're not included.

<Aside type="caution">If Cash at end doesn't match your bank and cash accounts, the report says so and Spine logs it. Check the [Trial Balance](../check-the-trial-balance/) and tell the person who runs Spine.</Aside>

To export, press **Export** (see [Export or print a report](../export-or-print-a-report/)).
