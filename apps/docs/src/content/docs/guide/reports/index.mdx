Every report in Spine is worked out from your books, the journal entries that each invoice, payment, bill, expense and journal posts. Nobody types figures into a report, so Profit & Loss, the Balance Sheet, the Tax Summary and job profitability always agree with each other and with the documents behind them. Click a figure to see the entries that make it up.

## The main ideas

- **The ledger.** Every business event posts a balanced journal entry: debits equal credits. Reports read those entries.
- **Accrual and cash basis.** Accrual counts income when you invoice and costs when you're billed. Cash counts them when money moves. Profit & Loss offers both; other statements are accrual.
- **Periods.** Most reports cover a range from **From** to **To**; the Balance Sheet and Trial Balance are **As of** one date. The default range is your financial year to date.
- **Drill-down.** Clicking an account opens the entries behind its figure, and each entry opens its document.
- **Journals.** Adjustments no document covers, such as depreciation, posted by an Owner and never edited, only reversed.
- **Closed periods.** Once a period is closed, nothing can be posted with a date inside it. Closing periods, adding accounts and opening balances are covered in [Chart of accounts and opening balances](../accounts/).
