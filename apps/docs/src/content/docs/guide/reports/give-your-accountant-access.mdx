Owners open every report. Everyone else sees only the reports their Teams allow, and each report right is granted on its own. Your accountant can read the financial statements without being able to change anything, and your office staff can chase receivables without seeing profit.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners only. Managing Teams is always Owner-only.</Aside>

## The report rights

They're in the **Reports & Ledger** group of a Team's permissions:

| Right | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **View dashboard summary** | The dashboard's key figures and the money in and out chart. |
| **View receivables** | Receivables, Payments received, Credit notes and refunds, and the dashboard's receivables cards. |
| **View payables** | Payables, Payments made, Vendor credits and the dashboard's Payables card. |
| **View job profitability** | Job profitability and the dashboard's job cards. |
| **View financial statements** | Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Cash Flow, Tax Summary, the sales and purchase reports, GSTR-1, Export to Tally, and the dashboard's Tax this period and Top expenses cards. It grants nothing that writes. |
| **View ledger / journals** | The entries behind a figure, the General ledger, the Day book and Journals (read only). It doesn't open Profit & Loss. |

A new business's General Staff Team starts with the first four. **View financial statements** and **View ledger / journals** start off.

## Give a Team a report right

<Steps>

1. **Open the Team** — Go to **Teams** in the sidebar and open the Team, for example one you've made for your accountant.
2. **Open Permissions** — Choose the **Permissions** tab and expand **Reports & Ledger**.
3. **Switch on the rights** — Turn on the reports this Team needs.
4. **Save** — Press **Save changes**. You see how many permissions changed, and members get them at their next request.

</Steps>

## What people without a right see

A report someone can't open isn't listed on their Reports page. If they follow a link to it, they see "You don't have access to financial statements. Ask an owner." or "You don't have access to this report. Ask an owner." Dashboard cards they can't see are left out, never shown locked.

<Aside type="caution">Even with **View financial statements**, a member can't post a journal, edit opening balances, close a period or change Settings. Those stay with Owners.</Aside>

Field crew never see financial reports, on the web or on a phone, whatever their Teams. For more on Teams, see [Team and permissions](../../team/).
