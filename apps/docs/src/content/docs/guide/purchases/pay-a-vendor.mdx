When you pay a vendor, record the payment in Spine and tick the bills it covers. Each bill's balance drops by what the payment put on it, and the payment is posted to your books: Accounts Payable goes down and so does the account you paid from. One payment can pay several bills from the same vendor.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create own vendor payments**. Field users can't record vendor payments.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the payment dialog** — Go to **Vendor Payments** and press **Record payment**. Or, on a bill's page, press **Pay bill**: the vendor is chosen and that bill is ticked for its balance.

2. **Choose the vendor** — Their recorded bills with a balance are listed, oldest due first, with the bill number, bill date, due date (and how late), balance and a tick box.

3. **Tick what it pays** — Tick each bill the payment covers, or press **Pay all**. The **Amount** becomes their total. If you type the amount first instead, it's spread over the bills oldest first, and you can change any line up to its balance.

4. **Fill in the details** — Set the **Date**, the **Method** (Cash, Bank transfer, Cheque, Card, UPI or Other) and **Paid from** — the account the money left. **Reference** is optional: a cheque number or transaction ID.

5. **Check the line above the button** — When the amount and the ticked lines match, it reads, for example, "$48,000.00 pays RTE-3302 in full and $8,000.00 of RTE-3340.". If they don't match, it says by how much, and **Record payment** stays off.

6. **Record it** — Press **Record payment**. Spine asks "Record this payment?" with the same summary. Confirm with **Record payment**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="purchases-record-payment" alt="The Record a vendor payment dialog with Vendor, Amount, Date, Method, Paid from, Reference and the list of bills to tick" />

The toast names the payment, for example "VPY-0012 recorded". From the list, the payment's page opens; from a bill, the bill refreshes with its new balance.

## Before you record

- **Open credit.** If the vendor has open vendor credit, the dialog says "&lt;Vendor&gt; has … of credit. Apply it first?" with a link. Using the credit first means you pay less — see [Apply a vendor credit to bills](../apply-a-vendor-credit/).
- **A payment that looks recorded already.** If there's a payment to the same vendor for the same amount within 3 days, or with the same reference, the dialog warns "This looks like VPY-0011 …" with a link. Record it anyway if it's really a second payment.
- **No bills to pay.** If the vendor has no recorded bills with a balance, the dialog says so. Record the bill first.

<Aside type="tip">
  You can also pay from [Payables](../pay-bills-from-payables/), or record a payment from a bank statement line while reconciling. It's the same dialog everywhere.
</Aside>

Every payment gets a VPY- number. To tell the vendor what you paid, [send a remittance advice](../send-a-remittance-advice/).
