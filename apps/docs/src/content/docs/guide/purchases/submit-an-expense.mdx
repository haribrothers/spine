Submit an expense whenever you spend the business's money, or your own on its behalf — fuel, materials, a site meal. Add the receipt, and it waits for approval. A submitted expense posts nothing to your books until an Owner or approver approves it.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Everyone. Owners, Staff whose Team has **Submit/edit own expenses**, and Field users, who can always submit their own. Field crew usually submit from the phone in the field app.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the form** — Go to **Expenses** and press **Submit expense**.

2. **Add the receipt** — Drop the receipt photo or PDF on the form, or press **Add files**. The first file is the receipt and shows a preview, with **Replace** and **Remove**. You can add more files at once; they're attached to the expense when you save.

3. **Choose the category** — Pick a **Category**. Each one shows what it's for under its name, and typing searches both. A category's usual tax rate fills in for you.

4. **Enter the details** — Choose a **Job** if it's for one (it flows into job costing once approved; the field shows while the **Jobs & field** module is on). Set the **Date** and the **Amount**. Choose the **Tax rate**: the **Tax amount** is worked out as included in the amount, not added on top. You can still change it.

5. **Say who paid** — Under **Paid by**, choose **Company** or **Employee (reimbursable)**. Owners and Staff also choose **Paid from**, the account the money left.

6. **Describe it** — Say what it was for in **Description**. Tick **Billable to a customer** if the job's customer should pay for it.

7. **Submit** — Press **Submit expense**, or **Submit and add another** to start a fresh form with the same date, currency, paid-by and job.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="purchases-expense-form" alt="The Submit an expense form with the receipt pane, Category, Job, Date, Amount, Tax rate, Tax amount, Paid by and Description" />

The toast says "Expense submitted", and it's listed as **Waiting for approval**. When it's approved, you're told in Spine and by email.

## Checks before you submit

- No category: "Select a category before saving."
- A zero amount: "Enter an amount greater than zero."
- Receipts can be up to 10 MB. If one doesn't upload, you see "Could not upload the receipt."
- If you already submitted the same amount on the same day, a warning names it with a link. Check it isn't the same receipt; you can still submit.

## Change or delete it while it waits

Open the expense and press **Edit** (on the page, or under **More**), change it, and press **Save changes** — it stays waiting for approval. To withdraw it, open **More**, choose **Delete expense** and confirm "Delete this expense?". It's removed for good.

<Aside type="caution">
  Once an expense is approved it can't be edited or deleted — it has already posted to your books. A rejected one can be [fixed and resubmitted](../fix-and-resubmit-a-rejected-expense/).
</Aside>

To enter a day's labour as workers × day rate instead, see [Enter a day of site labour](../enter-a-day-of-site-labour/). To fill the form in from the receipt, see [Fill in a bill or expense with AI](../fill-in-a-bill-or-expense-with-ai/).
