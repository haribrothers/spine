Field crew can log fuel, materials or a site meal on the spot from the field app, with a photo of the receipt, so it isn't lost and you're paid back. Your office approves it before it counts. If there's no signal, the phone keeps it and sends it when you're back online.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Field users, for their own expenses. The phone only ever shows your own expenses — never anyone else's.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start a new expense** — Open the **Expenses** tab and tap the add button: **+** at the top on iPhone, or **Log expense** at the bottom on Android. On **Today**, **Log expense** and **Scan receipt** do the same; **Scan receipt** opens straight into the camera.

2. **Photograph the receipt** — Under **Receipt**, tap **Take a photo** and fit the whole receipt inside the frame, or tap **Choose a photo** to pick one you already took. **Retake** replaces it.

3. **Fill it in** — Enter the **Amount**, choose a **Category** and a **Job** if it's for one (Job shows while the Jobs & field module is on), set the **Date**, and say who paid under **Paid by**: **Me — pay me back** or **Company**. Add a **Description**.

4. **Submit** — Tap **Submit**. The note on the form reminds you: "Your office approves it before it's paid back. No signal? It's saved and sent later."

</Steps>

You don't choose which account paid — your office sets that when they approve it.

If your business uses the AI assistant, **Read receipt** fills in the amount, date and category from the photo. Check them before you submit. Reading needs a connection.

## Labour for the day

To log a day's labour instead, switch from **Amount** to **Headcount × day rate**. Add each trade with its **Workers** and **Day rate**; **Total for the day** adds them up. **Job** is required: labour is always for a job. See [Enter a day of site labour](../enter-a-day-of-site-labour/).

## See where your expenses stand

The **Expenses** tab lists **Waiting to sync** (saved on the phone, not sent yet) and **Submitted by you**, each with its category, date, job, amount and status: **Submitted**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. A rejected expense shows the reviewer's reason in red under it. If the Approvals module is off, expenses show as **Approved** as soon as they reach the office.

<Aside type="caution">
  A receipt photo over 10 MB can't be sent. The phone says so — retake it closer, or choose a smaller one.
</Aside>

If you leave out the category or the amount, you see "Choose a category." or "Enter an amount above zero." under the field.

<Aside type="tip">
  Tap an expense to see its details and, if it was rejected, the reason. The phone shows them read-only: to change, fix or delete an expense, sign in to Spine in a browser — see [Fix and resubmit a rejected expense](../fix-and-resubmit-a-rejected-expense/).
</Aside>
