When a supplier or subcontractor sends you a bill, enter it in Spine so what you owe is visible. A new bill saves as a draft. A draft posts nothing to your books until you record it, so you can check it first.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Vendor Bills** and **Create/edit own vendor bills**. Changing a draft someone else entered needs **Edit others' vendor bills**. Field users can't enter bills.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start a new bill** — Go to **Vendor Bills** and press **New vendor bill**.

2. **Fill in the header** — Choose the **Vendor**. Enter the **Bill number** — the vendor's own number, for example INV-1042. Spine doesn't number vendor bills. Choose a **Job** if the bill is for one: it flows into job costing once recorded. Set the **Bill date** and the **Due date**, or press **On receipt**, **+15 days**, **+30 days** or **+60 days**.

3. **Add the lines** — Each line has an **Item**, **Qty**, **Rate**, **Tax** and **Account** (the expense account it posts to), with a description under it. Press **Add item** for another line. Drag a line, use its menu, or press Alt + ↑ / ↓ to move it.

4. **Add notes and files** — **Notes** are for your team; the vendor never sees them. Under **Attachments**, press **Add files** or drop the vendor's PDF or photo, so it stays with the bill.

5. **Save** — Press **Save as draft**. To post it straight away, press **Save and record** instead — see [Record a vendor bill](../record-a-vendor-bill/).

</Steps>

## Help while you type

- **Due date from the last bill.** When you choose a vendor and the due date is empty, Spine suggests one from that vendor's last bill — "Suggested: 30 days, as on this vendor's last bill".
- **Duplicate warning.** If this vendor already has a bill with the same number (ignoring case and spaces, and not counting void bills), a warning names it with its date and amount, with a link to open it. You can still save if it's really a different bill.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes.** Spine asks "Leave without saving?" before you lose your work.

If you save without a vendor, you see "Select a vendor before saving.". Without a bill number, you see "Enter the vendor's bill number before saving.".

## Copy a bill

On a bill's page, open **More** and choose **Duplicate**. A new bill opens with the same vendor, job, currency and lines, today's date and an empty bill number.

## Change or delete a draft

Open the draft and press **Edit**, then **Save draft**. To remove a draft, open **More** and choose **Delete draft**. Spine asks "Delete this draft bill?" first.

<Aside type="danger">
  Deleting a draft removes it for good. A recorded bill can't be deleted — it can only be voided.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  Attachments take PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files, up to 10 MB each and 10 per record. Once a bill is recorded, only an Owner can remove its files, with a reason.
</Aside>
