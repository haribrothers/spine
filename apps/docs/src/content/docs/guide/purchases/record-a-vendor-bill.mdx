Recording a bill posts it to your books: its lines go to their expense accounts, its tax to input tax, and the total to Accounts Payable as owed to the vendor. A bill tagged to a job counts as that job's cost from then on. A recorded bill is locked — it can't be edited, only voided.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Record a vendor bill**. Entering the draft needs **Create/edit own vendor bills**, which is a separate right.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the draft** — Go to **Vendor Bills** and open the draft. Its state reads **Draft**.

2. **Check it** — Look over the vendor, bill number, dates, job and lines. Press **Edit** if anything needs changing.

3. **Record it** — Press **Record bill**. Spine asks "Record this bill?" and says what will be posted. Press **Record bill** again to confirm.

</Steps>

A toast says the bill is recorded and how much is posted as owed to the vendor. The bill's state changes to **Unpaid** (or **Overdue** once its due date has passed).

On the bill builder, **Save and record** saves the draft and records it in one go, after the same question.

## Read the bill's page

Once a bill is recorded, its page shows:

- **The state in words** — Unpaid, Partly paid, Paid, Overdue or Void — with how late it is, for example "12 days late" or "Due in 5 days".
- **A strip** with **Total**, **Paid**, **Credits applied** and **Balance due**.
- **Payments** against the bill, each opening its payment.
- **Activity**, newest first: created, recorded, each payment and credit applied, and voided, with who and when.

The main action is **Pay bill** while money is owed — see [Pay a vendor](../pay-a-vendor/). The rest sit in **More**.

## Record several drafts at once

<Steps>

1. **Tick the drafts** — On the **Vendor Bills** list, tick the bills you want to record. Only drafts count.

2. **Record them** — Press **Record (n)** in the bar. Spine asks "Record n bills?" with their total. Confirm with **Record bills**.

</Steps>

The toast says how many were recorded. Any that couldn't be recorded are named, with the reason — for example "It's already recorded.".

<Aside type="caution">
  You can't edit a bill after you record it. If it's wrong, [void it](../void-a-vendor-bill/) and enter it again, or record a [vendor credit](../record-a-vendor-credit/) for the difference.
</Aside>

If recording fails, the page says so and nothing is posted. Try again, or check the bill for a missing account.
