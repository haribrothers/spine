Spine reminds you shortly before a meeting starts, so you're never caught out. The business has a default reminder time, and each person can choose their own.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone can set their own reminder time. Only Owners set the business default and choose which meetings the dashboard shows.</Aside>

## Who gets a reminder

You get one "starting soon" reminder, by every channel you have turned on, when:

- you organise the meeting, or
- you're invited and answered **Accepted** or **Tentative**.

If you declined, you get no reminder. A recurring meeting reminds you once for each occurrence, and not for a cancelled one. If a meeting's time changes after you were reminded, you're reminded again before the new time.

## Choose your own reminder time

<Steps>
1. **Open your notifications** — Open **Settings** and choose **Notifications** under **Your account**.
2. **Pick a time** — Under **Meeting reminders**, choose **5 min**, **15 min**, **30 min** or **60 min**, or the business default (shown as "Business default · 15 min").
3. **Check it** — Spine says, for example, "You'll be reminded 30 minutes before." and "Meeting reminders saved."
</Steps>

## Set the business default

<Steps>
1. **Open the setting** — Open **Settings** and choose **Meeting reminders** under **Sales & meetings**.
2. **Pick a time** — Choose **5 minutes before**, **15 minutes before**, **30 minutes before** or **60 minutes before**. It starts at 15 minutes.
3. **Save** — Press **Save**. Everyone who hasn't chosen their own time uses it.
</Steps>

## Choose which meetings the dashboard shows

The dashboard's upcoming meetings can show each person's own meetings, or every meeting in the business.

<Steps>
1. **Open the setting** — Open **Settings** and choose **Upcoming meetings** under **Sales & meetings**.
2. **Choose** — Choose **Only my meetings** ("Each person's dashboard shows only the meetings they organize.") or **Every org meeting**, which suits a small team that plans as one calendar.
3. **Save** — Press **Save**. Every Staff dashboard follows your choice.
</Steps>

Field users always see only their own meetings, whatever you choose. See [Dashboard](../../dashboard/).

<Aside type="tip">Reminders by email and browser need those channels to be working. See [Notifications](../../notifications/) and, for your server's email, [Email](../../../self-hosting/email/).</Aside>
