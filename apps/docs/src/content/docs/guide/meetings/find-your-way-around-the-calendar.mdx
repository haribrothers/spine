The calendar shows your meetings the way you need them: a single day, a working week, the whole month, or a list of what's coming up. The mini calendar and the sidebar lists help you find a free day or the next meeting.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone who can open **Meetings**: Owners, Staff whose Team has **Open Meetings**, and Field crew. You see only the meetings you're allowed to see.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Choose a view** — In the toolbar, choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Agenda**. The heading shows the range, such as "October 4 – 10, 2026" or "Upcoming from Oct 4, 2026".
2. **Move through time** — Press **Previous** or **Next** to move a range back or forward, or **Today** to come back to today.
3. **Jump to a date** — Click a date in the mini calendar. The main view moves to it.
</Steps>

## What the calendar shows

- **Day** and **Week** open at 8 AM, or at the hour before now when you're looking at today and it's later than 9 AM. A line marks the current time on today's column.
- In **Month**, a busy day shows "+3 more" when its meetings don't fit. Press a date to open it in **Day** view.
- **Agenda** lists each meeting with its time (or "All day"), title, location and contact. With nothing coming up, it says so.

## The mini calendar

A dot under a date means there's at least one meeting that day. Hover over it to see how many, such as "2 meetings". In **Day** or **Week** view, the days you're looking at are shaded. Use the arrows to page to another month.

## The sidebar lists

- **Upcoming** lists your next 8 meetings in the next 60 days, such as "Today · 2:00 PM" or "Tue 13 Oct · 9:00 AM". Press a title to go to that day and open the meeting. With none, it says "Nothing in the next 60 days."
- **Meetings by contact** counts the linked meetings in the range you're looking at. Press a contact to open their page.

Press **Hide calendar sidebar** to give the calendar more room, and **Show calendar sidebar** to bring it back.

## Come back to the same place

The address keeps your view and date (for example `/meetings?view=month&date=2026-10-12`), so reloading, Back and bookmarks return you to the same place. Opening plain **Meetings** shows the view you last used on this device, on today.

## On a small screen

On a window narrower than a tablet, the calendar opens in **Agenda**, and **Week** isn't offered. Press **Mini calendar** above the calendar to open the mini calendar and the **Calendars** list.

<Aside type="tip">The dashboard's upcoming meetings link to the calendar too. See [Dashboard](../../dashboard/).</Aside>
