When a colleague invites you to a meeting, let them know whether you'll be there. Your answer shows next to your name for everyone who opens the meeting, and it decides whether Spine reminds you before it starts.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone invited to the meeting, including Field crew. Answering your own invitation needs no permission.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Find the meeting** — Open the invitation from the bell, or go to **Meetings** and find it on the calendar or in **Upcoming**.
2. **Open it** — Press the meeting. Its quick view opens.
3. **Answer** — Under **Your response**, press **Accepted**, **Tentative** or **Declined**. The button you chose stays highlighted.
</Steps>

You can also answer from **Manage attendees**: press the attendees button beside the meeting in **Upcoming**. It lists everyone invited with their response, such as **Invited** or **Accepted**.

## What happens next

- The organiser sees your response next to your name in the quick view.
- If you change an earlier answer, for example from **Accepted** to **Declined**, the organiser is notified. Your first answer doesn't notify them.
- If you accepted or answered **Tentative**, Spine reminds you before the meeting starts. If you declined, it doesn't. See [Set meeting reminders](../set-meeting-reminders/).

If your response can't be saved, you see "Could not save your response." Try again.

## Invitations and changes

You're told by email ("You've been invited: …") and in Spine when you're invited. You're told again if you're removed from the meeting or it's cancelled. For how notifications reach you, see [Notifications](../../notifications/).

## In the field app

Field crew see their next meeting in the next 7 days on the field app's **Today** screen, under **Next meeting**. Tapping it, or a meeting notification, opens the meeting's title, time, location and notes. The field app shows meetings read-only, so answer invitations in Spine on the web. If the meeting has been cancelled, or you're no longer invited, the app says "This meeting isn't available".

<Aside type="tip">No response buttons? You're not on the meeting's invite list, or you're its organiser. If you can edit the meeting, the quick view shows its edit buttons instead: answer from **Manage attendees**.</Aside>
