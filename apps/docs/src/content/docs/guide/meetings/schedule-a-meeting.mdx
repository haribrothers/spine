Put every site visit, call and catch-up on the calendar, so you can plan your week and your colleagues know where you are. Link a meeting to a customer and it shows on their timeline too.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own meetings**. Without it, there's no **New meeting** button and dragging on the calendar does nothing. Field crew can't create meetings.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Start the meeting** — Go to **Meetings** and press **New meeting**. It starts at the next half hour today, for 30 minutes, or at 9:00 AM on the day you're looking at. In **Day** or **Week** view you can instead drag across empty time to choose the start and end.
2. **Name it** — In the **New event** dialog's **Details** tab, fill in **Title**, such as "Site visit — Acme Contracting". It's the only required field.
3. **Link a contact** — In **Contact**, choose the customer or vendor it's with. Leave it as **None** for an internal meeting.
4. **Set the time** — Change **Start** and **End**, or turn on **All day**.
5. **Add the details** — Choose a **Category** colour, and add a **Location** and **Notes** if you like.
6. **Invite people** — Open the **People** tab to invite colleagues and choose who can see it. See [Invite people and choose who sees a meeting](../invite-people-and-choose-who-sees-a-meeting/).
7. **Save** — Press **Create event**. The meeting appears on the calendar in its colour.
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="meetings-new" alt="The New event dialog on its Details tab with Title, Contact, All day, Start, End, Category, Location and Notes" />

If the end is before the start, the **End** field shows an error and nothing is saved.

To make the meeting repeat, turn on **Repeats** in the same dialog. See [Set up a recurring meeting](../set-up-a-recurring-meeting/).

## Move or resize a meeting

- **Drag** a meeting to another time or day to move it.
- **Drag its bottom edge** to change when it ends.
- **With the keyboard**, focus the meeting and press Shift and the Up or Down arrow to change its start, or Shift, Alt and the Up or Down arrow to change its end.

The change is saved straight away. You can move only meetings you're allowed to edit. For a recurring meeting, Spine asks whether to move just this event or all events.

## Linked meetings and contacts

A meeting linked to a contact shows on that contact's **Timeline**, at the time it takes place. In the calendar's sidebar, **Meetings by contact** counts the linked meetings in the range you're looking at. In **Agenda**, each meeting shows its contact, or "Internal".

<Aside type="tip">You can also start a new meeting from **Search…** at the top of any page.</Aside>
