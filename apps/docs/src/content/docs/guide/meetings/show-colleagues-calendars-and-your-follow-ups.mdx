When you're finding a time that suits everyone, show just the people involved. Tick **My Activities** to see the calls and tasks you've planned with contacts and leads on the same calendar as your meetings.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone who can open **Meetings**. Hiding or showing calendars changes only what you see; it never shows you a meeting you aren't allowed to see.</Aside>

## Choose whose calendars show

<Steps>
1. **Open the list** — Go to **Meetings**. In the sidebar, the **Calendars** list has a row for each person in your organisation.
2. **Hide someone** — Untick a person. Meetings they organise disappear from the calendar, the mini calendar's dots and **Upcoming**.
3. **Show everyone again** — Tick the box beside **Calendars** (**Show every calendar**).
</Steps>

Spine remembers which calendars you've hidden on this device, so they stay hidden next time. If you're the only person in the organisation, the list says "No team members yet."

## Show your follow-ups

<Steps>
1. **Tick My Activities** — In the sidebar, tick **My Activities**.
2. **Read them** — Your activities with a follow-up date show on their days, marked **Activity**. The mini calendar counts them in its dots too.
3. **Open one** — Press an activity to see its type and "Due …" date.
</Steps>

To tick off or change a follow-up, open the contact or lead it belongs to. See [Contacts and pipeline](../../contacts/).

<Aside type="tip">On a small screen, press **Mini calendar** above the calendar to reach the **Calendars** list and **My Activities**.</Aside>

## What you can see

- **Owners** see every meeting, private or not.
- **Staff and Field crew** see meetings visible to everyone in the organisation, and private meetings they organise or are invited to.

Rights to edit other people's meetings never widen this. See [Invite people and choose who sees a meeting](../invite-people-and-choose-who-sees-a-meeting/).
