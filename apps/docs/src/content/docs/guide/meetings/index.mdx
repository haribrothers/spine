Meetings is your team's calendar: site visits, customer calls and internal catch-ups, in day, week, month and agenda views. Link a meeting to a contact and it shows on their timeline. Invite colleagues, choose who can see it, and Spine reminds everyone before it starts.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can use meetings">**Meetings** is in the sidebar for Owners, Staff whose Team has **Open Meetings**, and Field crew. ## The main ideas

- **Views.** **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Agenda** (a list of what's coming up). The address in your browser keeps the view and date.
- **Organiser.** The person who created the meeting. "Own meetings" are the ones you organise.
- **Contact.** A meeting can be linked to a customer or vendor, or be internal with no contact.
- **Visibility.** A meeting is private (only you and the people you invite can see it) or visible to everyone in the organisation. Owners see every meeting.
- **Invitations and responses.** Invitees are told by email and in Spine, and answer **Accepted**, **Tentative** or **Declined**.
- **Recurring meetings.** A meeting can repeat daily, weekly or monthly. You can change one occurrence or the whole series.
- **Reminders.** Spine reminds the organiser and everyone who accepted shortly before a meeting starts.
- **My Activities.** Your follow-ups from contacts and leads can show on the calendar beside your meetings.
