The Jobs page lists every job with what's next on it and how far along it is. The cards at the top tell you what needs doing: work to bill, jobs going over budget and retention you're still owed.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Jobs**. The **Over or near budget** card and the **Budget used** column need **View job profitability**. Field crew see their assigned jobs in the field app instead.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open Jobs** — Choose **Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. **Press a card** — **Active** shows running jobs and their contract value. **Ready to bill** shows jobs with a completed milestone not yet invoiced, and that amount. **Over or near budget** shows jobs that have used 80% or more of their cost budget. Press a card again to clear it. **Retention held** shows the total held across your jobs.
3. **Narrow the list** — Use **Status** and **Contact** in the toolbar, or type in **Search jobs, numbers or sites** to search by number, name, contact or site address.
4. **Choose columns** — Press **Columns** to show or hide columns such as **Retention** and **Budget used**.
5. **Open a job** — Click a row to open the job's page.
</Steps>

## Reading a row

Each row shows:

- **Job** — the name, with the job number and site address under it.
- **Contact** and **Status**.
- **Next milestone** — its name and when it's due ("Due in 3 days", "Overdue 2 days"), or "All billed".
- **Progress** — two short bars: how complete the work is and how much of the contract is billed. A job billed over its contract shows the billed figure in the overdue colour with a warning icon, and "Billed over the contract" when you point at it.
- **End date** and **Contract**.

**Budget used** reads "78%", "Nearing · 85%" or "Over · 104%", or "—" for a job without a budget.

## Coming back to the same list

Your search, filters, card, sort and page are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a view. Press **Clear filters** to start again. The list shows when it was last updated. Press **Refresh list** to reload it, and it also reloads when you come back after 30 seconds.

If nothing matches, you see "No jobs match these filters". With no jobs at all, you see "No jobs yet" with **New job** (if you can create jobs) and **Go to quotes**.

<Aside type="tip">The dashboard can show jobs in progress, retention held and jobs ready to bill. Its links open this list or the right job.</Aside>
