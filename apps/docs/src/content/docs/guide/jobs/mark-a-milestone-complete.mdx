When a stage of the work is finished, mark its milestone complete. You can add photos and notes as evidence. The office is told straight away, and the milestone becomes ready to invoice.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners. Staff whose Team has **Define/edit own milestones** (for milestones on their own jobs) or **Edit others' milestones**. Field crew can always mark milestones complete on jobs they're assigned to, whoever set the milestones up.</Aside>

## On the web

<Steps>
1. **Open the job** — Go to **Jobs**, open the job and choose the **Milestones** tab.
2. **Start completing it** — Press **Mark complete** on a pending milestone. You can also press the job's main button, **Complete _milestone_**, which picks the next pending one.
3. **Add evidence (optional)** — The dialog "Mark "_milestone_" complete" asks for **Completion notes** (site conditions, what was done, anything worth recording) and **Photos**. Press **Add** to attach photos. Both are optional.
4. **Confirm** — Press **Mark complete**. A toast says the milestone is completed and ready to invoice.
</Steps>

The milestone now shows **Completed** with who completed it and when, and "not invoiced yet" until someone raises its invoice. Your completion notes show under it. The photos appear on the **Photos** tab, grouped under the milestone's name.

If a photo can't upload, you see "Could not upload one or more photos." Your notes stay, so you can try again.

## In the field app

Crew open the job from **Jobs** or **Today** on their phone, then tap **Mark complete** on the milestone. They can add **Photos of the finished work** and a **Note for the office (optional)**. With no signal, the completion is saved on the phone and sent when it's back online.

## What happens next

- Owners and office staff get a "Milestone completed" notification, by email and in the app.
- The job appears under **Ready to bill** in the Jobs list.
- The job's main button changes to **Invoice _milestone_** for people who can raise invoices. See [Raise a progress invoice from a milestone](../raise-a-progress-invoice/).

<Aside type="tip">On a job that's **Completed** or **Cancelled**, the main button no longer offers **Complete _milestone_**. You can still use **Mark complete** on the milestone's row.</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">There's no button to undo a completion. Ask the office before marking a stage complete if you're not sure the work is finished.</Aside>
