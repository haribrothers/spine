A job's status tells everyone where it stands: **Active**, **On hold**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**. Marking a job completed lets the office know the work is done, and it's when Spine offers to release the retention you've held.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners. Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own jobs** can change the status of jobs they created; **Edit others' jobs** covers everyone else's. Without the right, the status shows as a plain badge you can't open.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the job** — Go to **Jobs** and open the job.
2. **Open the status** — Press the status badge beside the job number.
3. **Choose a status** — Pick **Active**, **On hold**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**. The change saves at once and a toast says "Status changed".
4. **Confirm a cancellation** — For **Cancelled**, Spine asks "Cancel this job?" first. Press **Cancel the job** to go ahead, or **Keep the job**.
</Steps>

You can also change the status in **Edit job**.

## Closing a job

When the work is done:

1. Make sure every milestone is invoiced. The **Milestones** tab shows what's still **Completed** but not invoiced.
2. Set the status to **Completed**. The office gets a notification that the job is completed.
3. If retention is held on the job, the page's main button becomes **Release retention**. Release it when the defect period ends: see [Hold and release retention](../hold-and-release-retention/).

<Aside type="tip">Setting a job to **Completed** doesn't change its **Percent complete**. Set that yourself in **Edit job** if you track it.</Aside>

## What cancelling does

A cancelled job keeps its milestones, invoices and costs as they are. Nothing is deleted or reversed. You can set it back to **Active** later.

<Aside type="caution">There's no way to delete a job. Use **Cancelled** for work that won't go ahead.</Aside>

## The next-step button

Beside **Edit job** and **Raise variation**, one main button offers the next thing to do on the job:

- **Invoice _milestone_** when a milestone is completed but not invoiced.
- Otherwise **Complete _milestone_** for the next pending milestone, while the job isn't completed or cancelled.
- Otherwise **Release retention** when the job is completed with retention held.

**More** holds **Add photos** and **View history**, and **Release retention** when it isn't the main button.
