A job is the work behind an agreed price. It holds the contract value, the billing milestones you invoice as the work is done, the retention held back from each progress invoice, the variation orders that change the scope, site photos and notes, the crew working on it, and live costing from your books.

## The main ideas

- **Contract value.** What the customer agreed to pay. A job started from an accepted quote takes the quote's total. Afterwards it changes only when you apply an accepted variation order.
- **Job number.** Every job gets a permanent number, such as JOB-007, from the Job sequence in **Settings › Numbering**.
- **Status.** A job is **Active**, **On hold**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**.
- **Milestones.** Stages of the work, each a share of the contract or a fixed amount, with a due date. A milestone goes from **Pending** to **Completed** to **Invoiced**.
- **Progress invoices.** The invoice you raise from a completed milestone. Each issued one gets a progress claim number on the job.
- **Retention.** A percentage held back from each progress invoice. It sits in Retention Receivable, not in what the customer owes now, until you release it.
- **Variation orders.** A quote for a change to the job, linked to it. Once the customer accepts it, you apply it to add its total to the contract value.
- **Costing.** Cost, revenue and margin for the job, worked out from bills and approved expenses tagged to it and the invoices raised on it.
