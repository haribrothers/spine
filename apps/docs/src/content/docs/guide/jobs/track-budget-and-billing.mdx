A cost budget is what you expect a job to cost. Spine compares it with what the job has actually cost and warns you as it gets close. Alongside it, the **Billing** card shows how much of the contract you've claimed, and whether billing is keeping up with the work.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Setting the budget and percent complete: Owners, and Staff who can edit the job (**Create/edit own jobs** for their own jobs, **Edit others' jobs** for the rest). Seeing the **Budget** and **Billing** cards: Owners, and Staff whose Team has **View job profitability**.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the job** — Go to **Jobs**, open the job and press **Edit job**. On the **Costing** tab you can also press **Set a budget** or **Edit budget**.
2. **Enter the budget** — Under **Budget and progress**, type the **Cost budget**. Leave it blank if you don't budget the job.
3. **Enter percent complete** — Type your estimate of how much of the work is done in **Percent complete**, from 0 to 100.
4. **Save** — Press **Save**. The change is recorded in the job's history with who made it and when.
</Steps>

## The Budget card

On the **Costing** tab, the card shows **Cost budget**, **Spent** and **Remaining**, with a bar:

- Under 80%: "78% of budget used".
- From 80%: the bar turns to the warning colour with a warning icon, "Nearing budget — 85% used".
- Over 100%: "Over budget by 1,450.00" in the danger colour with its icon, and **Remaining** shows a negative amount.

The card reminds you of the limits: "Warning at 80% · over at 100%". A job without a budget shows "No budget set" with **Set a budget**. Spent is the same figure as **Cost**. The budget is a target only: nothing is posted to your books when it changes.

Jobs over budget also show on the **Over or near budget** card in the Jobs list, in its **Budget used** column, and on the dashboard's "Needs your attention".

## The Billing card

The card shows **Contract value**, **Billed to date** (with its percentage of the contract) and **Left to bill**, with a bar.

- Billed to date counts issued progress invoices on the job, including tax and before retention. A credit note against one of them lowers it.
- Advance invoices and retention-release invoices don't count. A deposit counts once it's offset on a progress invoice.
- If you've billed more than the contract, **Left to bill** reads "Billed over contract by _amount_". This often means a variation order hasn't been applied yet.

When you've set **Percent complete**, the card also shows **Work complete** and, with a gap of 10 points or more, a comparison such as "Billing is 15 points behind the work — check for a milestone to invoice." or "Billing is 10 points ahead of the work."

<Aside type="tip">Field crew can see a job's percent complete, but never its budget or billing.</Aside>
