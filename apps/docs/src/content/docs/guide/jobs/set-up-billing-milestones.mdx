Milestones split a job into stages you bill one at a time, such as "Foundation complete" or "Roof on". Each is a share of the contract or a fixed amount, with a due date. When a stage is done, you mark it complete and raise its progress invoice. Without milestones, you can still bill the job however you like.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners. Staff whose Team has **Define/edit own milestones** can set up milestones on jobs they created; **Edit others' milestones** covers jobs other people created. Field crew can't set up or change milestones, but they can mark them complete.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the job** — Go to **Jobs**, open the job and choose the **Milestones** tab. A job with none says "Set up how this job is billed".
2. **Open the editor** — Press **Add milestones**, or **Edit milestones** if the job already has some.
3. **Fill in each row** — Give it a **Name** (for example "Foundation complete"), choose a **Type** (**% of contract** or **Fixed amount**), enter the **Percent** or amount, and pick a **Due date**.
4. **Add more stages** — Press **Add milestone** for each extra row. To remove one, use the bin button on its row.
5. **Check the total** — While you edit, "Percent total" shows the sum of the percentage rows. It must read 100%.
6. **Save** — Press **Save milestones**. Each milestone shows its name, amount, due date and status **Pending**.
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="jobs-milestones-editor" alt="The milestones editor with rows for name, type, percent or amount and due date, and the running percent total" />

## Rules when you save

- Every milestone needs a name ("Every milestone needs a name.").
- You need at least one milestone ("Add at least one milestone.").
- Percentage milestones must total exactly 100%. If they don't, you see "Milestone percentages must total 100% (currently 80%)" and nothing saves.
- Fixed-amount milestones aren't part of the 100% check. You can mix both types on one job.

## Once something is invoiced

When at least one milestone is invoiced, its billing details are locked. You can't edit or remove it, only add new milestones alongside it.

## Reading the Milestones tab

The tab starts with "Billed _amount_ of _contract value_" and a bar, and how many milestones are done. Each milestone then shows, in order:

- A step marker for **Pending**, **Completed** or **Invoiced**.
- Its amount and share of the contract.
- When it's due ("Due 30 Oct · in 20 days"), or how overdue it is, in the overdue colour.
- Its action: **Mark complete**, **Raise invoice** or **View invoice**.

<Screenshot id="jobs-milestones" alt="A job's Milestones tab showing billed so far, a progress bar and each milestone with its status, amount, due date and action" />

<Aside type="tip">Percentage milestones follow the contract value. When you [apply a variation order](../apply-a-variation-order/), their amounts are worked out again.</Aside>
