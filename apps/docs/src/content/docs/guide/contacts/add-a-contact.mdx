Add a contact before you quote a customer or record a bill from a vendor. You need only a name and a type to start. You can add the people at a company and its addresses once it's saved.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own contacts**. Without it, **Add contact** isn't shown.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the drawer** — Go to **Contacts** and press **Add contact**. The **Add contact** drawer opens.
2. **Choose the type** — In **Type**, choose **Customer** (the default), **Vendor** or **Customer & vendor**.
3. **Say whether it's a company** — For a business, turn on **This is a company, not an individual** and fill in **Company name**. For a person, leave it off and fill in **Full name**.
4. **Add how to reach them** — Fill in **Email**, **Phone**, **Tax ID** and **Website** if you have them. Tax ID is useful for invoicing a business contact.
5. **Save it** — Press **Save contact**. The drawer closes, a "Contact created" toast shows, and the contact appears in the list.
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="contacts-add" alt="The Add contact drawer with Type, the company switch, Full name, Email, Phone, Tax ID and Website" />

## Customer, vendor or both

Choose **Customer & vendor** for a business you both sell to and buy from. It then appears in the customer picker on quotes and invoices, and in the vendor picker on vendor bills. When you filter the list by **Customer** or **Vendor**, these contacts are included in both.

You can change the type later. You can't change a **Customer & vendor** to **Customer** while it has vendor bills.

## For a GST business

If your business is set up for GST, the drawer also asks for **GST treatment** (registered, unregistered, consumer, overseas or special economic zone), the **GSTIN** and the **State**. For a registered business or an SEZ you see "Enter the GSTIN — it's needed for a registered business or SEZ." until you add one. When you enter a GSTIN, the state is filled from it.

## If something goes wrong

- If the name is empty, the name field shows an error and nothing is saved.
- If saving fails, you see "Could not create contact." and what you typed stays in the drawer, so you can try again.

## What happens next

Open the contact from the list to add the people you deal with and its addresses: see [Add contact persons and addresses](../add-contact-persons-and-addresses/). To bring in many contacts at once, see [Import contacts from a CSV file](../import-contacts-from-a-csv-file/).
