A statement lists a customer's invoices, payments and credits for a period, with a running balance and what's still due. Customers use it to check their records and pay everything in one go. You send it from the customer's contact page.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **View receivables**. Without it, **Send statement** isn't shown. Statements are for customers: a vendor-only contact has no **Send statement**.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the customer** — Go to **Contacts** and open the customer.
2. **Start the statement** — Press **Send statement**. The **Send a statement** dialog opens.
3. **Choose the period** — In **Period**, choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This year** or **Custom**. For **Custom**, pick the **From** and **To date**.
4. **Choose what to show** — In **Show**, choose **Open invoices only** or **All activity**.
5. **Check the summary** — The summary shows the **Opening balance**, **Invoiced**, **Paid and credited** and **Balance due** for the period.
6. **Check the email** — **To** starts with the contact's email. The **Subject** and **Message** start from your statement email template; change them if you like.
7. **Send it** — Press **Send statement**. The statement PDF is emailed, and a toast says "Statement sent to …".
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="contacts-statement" alt="The Send a statement dialog with Period, Show, the statement summary, To, Subject and Message" />

To get the PDF without emailing it, press **Download PDF** instead.

## What the PDF shows

- Your business, "STATEMENT", the period, and "Statement for" the customer with their address and the balance due.
- A table of **Date**, **Reference**, **Type**, **Charges**, **Payments** and **Balance**, starting with the opening balance.
- An aging strip of what's still owed: **Current**, **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days** and **90+ days**.
- A "How to pay" block, when you've set payment instructions.

With **Open invoices only**, paid invoices and their payments are left out, and so is the opening balance.

## Limits

- A customer with no invoices can't be sent a statement: you see "No invoices for this customer yet." and **Send statement** stays greyed out.
- The statement is worked out from your books each time. Nothing is stored except the record of the email.

<Aside type="tip">Emails need your server's email set up. If statements don't arrive, see [Email](../../../self-hosting/email/).</Aside>

For more on what customers owe, see [Payments](../../payments/).
