Contacts are the customers and vendors you do business with. Each contact has its own page with the people who work there, its addresses, a timeline, the activities and follow-ups you log, and its leads. The pipeline is a board of leads: the jobs you might win, by stage, with what each one is worth.

## The main ideas

- **Contact type.** A contact is a **Customer**, a **Vendor**, or a **Customer & vendor**. A customer appears when you pick who a quote or invoice is for; a vendor appears when you record a bill. A **Customer & vendor** appears in both, so a business that buys from you and sells to you is one record.
- **Company or individual.** A contact is either a company (with people at it) or a single person.
- **Contact persons and addresses.** The people you deal with at a company, and its billing, shipping, site and other addresses.
- **Timeline.** The contact's quotes, logged activities and meetings, newest first.
- **Activities and follow-ups.** A call, meeting, note or task you log against a contact or lead, with an optional date to follow up.
- **Leads.** A possible piece of work for a contact, with an estimated value, a source, an owner and a stage.
- **Stages.** The columns of the pipeline board. Open stages are leads still in play. **Won** and **Lost** stages close a lead.
- **Archive or delete.** Archiving hides a contact you no longer use but keeps its history. Deleting removes it for good, and only works for a contact with no financial documents.
