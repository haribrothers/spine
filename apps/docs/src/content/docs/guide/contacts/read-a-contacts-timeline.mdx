Before you call a customer, check where you stand with them. A contact's timeline lists what's happened with them, newest first, so you don't have to open each list in turn.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Contacts**. Staff see quotes only if their Team has **Open Quotes**, and never a private meeting they're not on.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the contact** — Go to **Contacts** and open it. The page opens on the **Timeline** tab.
2. **Read the entries** — Each entry shows what happened (for example "Quote QUO-0012 sent" or "Meeting: Site visit"), a short detail such as the quote's total, and how long ago it happened. Hover over the time to see the exact date.
</Steps>

## What's on the timeline

- **Quotes**: when each was created and sent, and whether it was accepted or rejected.
- **Activities** you've logged, and whether each task is done. See [Log an activity or follow-up](../log-an-activity-or-follow-up/).
- **Meetings** linked to the contact, at the time they take place. For a recurring meeting, a cancelled occurrence isn't listed, and a moved one shows its new time. See [Meetings](../../meetings/).

A contact with no history shows "Nothing yet" and "Quotes, logged activities, and scheduled meetings for this contact will show up here."

## The other tabs

- **Contact persons** and **Addresses**: see [Add contact persons and addresses](../add-contact-persons-and-addresses/).
- **Leads**: the contact's leads with their title, stage and value. See [Add a lead](../add-a-lead/).
- **Activities**: calls, meetings, notes and tasks for the contact.
- **Attachments**: files kept with the contact, such as a signed agreement. Press **Add files**, or drop files on the tab. PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files work, up to 10 MB each and 10 per record.

<Aside type="tip">The **Leads** tab shows only while the **Leads & meetings** module is on. With the module off, meetings aren't on the timeline either.</Aside>

## History is separate

The timeline shows what happened with the customer. To see who changed the contact's own details, press **View history** at the top of the page.
