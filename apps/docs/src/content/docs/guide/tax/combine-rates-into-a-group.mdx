Some taxes are charged in parts: CGST and SGST in India, or a state surcharge on top of a sales tax. A tax group puts those rates under one name. On a line, you pick the group once and Spine works out each part.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners only. Anyone who edits a document can then pick the group on a line.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Tax** — Scroll to **Tax groups**. If you have no rates yet, you see "Add at least one tax rate before creating a group." Add the rates first.
2. **Add a group** — Press **Add group**. The **Add tax group** dialog opens.
3. **Name it and pick the rates** — Enter a **Name** (for example "CGST + SGST") and choose the **Rates** to include.
4. **Save** — The group is listed with its parts, for example "Sales Tax (15%) + State Surcharge (2.5%)".

</Steps>

## How a group works out tax

Each rate in the group is applied to the line's amount. A rate marked **Compound** is applied to the amount plus the rates before it.

For a 1,000.00 line with a group of 15% and 2.5% compound:

- Sales tax: 15% of 1,000.00 = 150.00
- Surcharge: 2.5% of 1,150.00 = 28.75
- Tax 178.75, line total 1,178.75

The line's tax is rounded once, half-up. A line uses either one rate or one group, never both.

## GST groups in India

When your tax country is India, a group must be CGST with SGST (or UTGST) at the same rate, optionally with cess, or a single IGST rate. Any other mix shows "A GST group is CGST + SGST (or UTGST) at the same rate, optionally with cess."

## Edit, archive or restore a group

Use the group's menu to **Edit**, **Archive** or **Restore** it, the same as a rate. An archived group no longer appears in line pickers, and issued documents keep the tax they were issued with.

If saving fails you see "Could not save tax group." Try again.
