A tax rate is a name and a percentage that a line on a quote, invoice, bill, expense or catalogue item can use. Your rates live in Settings › Tax. Changing one later is safe: documents already issued keep the percentage they were issued with.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners only. Staff can pick rates on the lines they edit, but can't create or change them.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Tax** — Open your account menu, choose **Settings**, then **Tax**. The **Tax rates** list shows each rate as its name and percentage, for example "GST 10%".
2. **Add a rate** — Press **Add rate**. The **Add tax rate** dialog opens.
3. **Name it** — Enter a **Name** (for example "GST") and the **Rate (%)**. Rates are stored exactly, so 8.875 stays 8.875.
4. **Choose the tax type** — Pick a **Tax type**: **VAT**, **GST**, **Sales tax** or **Other**. For India, the choices are **CGST**, **SGST**, **UTGST**, **IGST** and **Cess**. Reports split tax by this type.
5. **Say what lines with this rate are** — Under **Lines with this rate are**, choose the supply type a line gets when it picks this rate, such as Taxable or Zero-rated.
6. **Tick Compound if it applies** — **Compound** taxes this rate on top of earlier rates in a group, not only on the base amount. Use it for a surcharge charged on tax.
7. **Save** — The rate joins the list.

</Steps>

## Edit, archive or restore a rate

Open the rate's menu (**Actions for** the rate) and choose:

- **Edit** to change its name, percentage or type. Issued documents keep the percentage they were issued with. Drafts and new lines use the new one.
- **Archive** to hide it from line pickers. It still shows on old documents, marked **Archived** in the list.
- **Restore** to bring an archived rate back.

<Aside type="caution">Archive a rate rather than reusing it for a different tax. Renaming "VAT 20%" to mean something else makes old reports harder to read.</Aside>

If saving fails you see "Could not save tax rate." Check the fields and try again.

A 0% rate is how you enter zero-rated sales. To say why a line has no tax, see [Mark a line as zero-rated, exempt or non-taxable](../mark-supplies-zero-rated-or-exempt/). To charge two rates together, [combine them into a tax group](../combine-rates-into-a-group/).
