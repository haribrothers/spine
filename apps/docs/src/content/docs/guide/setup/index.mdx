When the first owner creates an account on a new Spine, a short setup runs before anything else. It asks for the things every quote, invoice and report depends on: your business name and logo, the kind of work you do, your currency, financial year and tax, how documents are numbered, your accounts and your team. Nothing in it is permanent — every answer can be changed later in **Settings**.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. Staff and Field users never see the setup wizard or the Set up Spine page.</Aside>

## The main ideas

- **Four short stages.** Setup is grouped into **Business**, **Money**, **Documents** and **Books & team**, about six minutes in all. A preview beside the form shows the start of your first invoice as you type.
- **Skip anything.** Every step has **Skip to dashboard**. Spine works with sensible defaults, and the steps are all in Settings for later.
- **The Set up Spine page** lists everything a new business sets up — 14 cards from business details to the field app — and shows which are done. It's in your user menu.
- **Demo data** is a sample business you can load to look around before you enter your own. It's kept apart from your records, numbered from its own series and deleted in one step.
