A demo business fills Spine with about six months of realistic work — leads, quotes at every stage, jobs, invoices and payments, bills, expenses, meetings and a small team — so you can try every screen before you commit real data. It's kept apart from your own records and goes in one step when you're done.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. Loading and deleting demo data is Owner-only.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the demo picker** — On the setup welcome screen, press **Just show me around (use defaults)**. Later, open **Settings › Demo data** and press **Load demo data**. Either way, **Explore a demo business** opens.

2. **Choose a country** — **Country** offers United States (sales tax), India (GST), United Kingdom (VAT), Australia (GST), New Zealand (GST), United Arab Emirates (VAT) and Saudi Arabia (VAT). If you haven't set a tax country yet, Spine guesses it from your time zone.

3. **Choose an industry** — **Industry** offers Construction / Contracting, Interior design & fit-out, Trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), IT services & agency and Professional services. The field says which business you'll see, e.g. "You'll see Riverstone Builders."

4. **Press Load demo business** — Spine builds the business and opens the dashboard. If file storage isn't reachable, the demo still loads without its photos and the dialog says so; press **Continue**.

</Steps>

## What changes

- **Your settings follow the demo** when you load it from the welcome screen, or before you have records of your own: the tax country, currency, financial year and industry template become the demo's. Change them back in Settings any time.
- **Once you have your own records**, the country is fixed to your tax country ("Demo data uses your business's country: India (GST).") and only the industry can be picked. Loading then changes none of your settings.
- **Only one demo at a time.** With one loaded, **Settings › Demo data** names it — for example "Riverstone Builders · United States (sales tax) · Construction / Contracting" — and offers only **Delete all demo data**.

## Kept apart from your own records

- Demo documents have their own numbers: `DEMO-QUO-0001`, `DEMO-INV-0001`, `DEMO-JOB-001` and so on. Your first real invoice is still `INV-0001`.
- Anything you create for a demo customer, a demo vendor or on a demo job is demo too and gets a `DEMO-` number.
- The demo comes with demo people (Staff and Field) so you can see Users and Teams at work. Its invites can't be used, and no email is sent to them.

<Aside type="tip">When you're ready to start for real, [delete the demo data](../delete-demo-data/). Your reports and books then hold only your own business.</Aside>
