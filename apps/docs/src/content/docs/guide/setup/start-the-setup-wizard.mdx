Right after you create the first account on a new Spine, you land on the welcome screen. The first stage, **Business**, puts your name, logo and address on every quote, invoice and email from the first one, and picks a template that sets Spine up for your kind of work.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only the Owner who created the account. Staff and Field users never see the wizard.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Choose how to start** — The welcome screen says "Four short stages, about 6 minutes." Press **Set up my business** to begin. **Just show me around (use defaults)** loads a demo business instead ([Explore Spine with a demo business](../explore-with-demo-data/)), and **Skip to dashboard** skips setup ([Skip setup and come back later](../skip-setup/)).

2. **Enter your details** — On **Tell us about your business**, fill in **Business name** (required), **Address**, **Business email**, **Business phone** and **Industry**. Industry is free text, in your own words — the next step picks the template.

3. **Add your logo** — Click the logo square and pick an image up to 10 MB. When it's done the square shows "Logo uploaded". If it shows "Could not upload logo.", try again; the rest of the step still saves.

4. **Press Continue** — The step is saved and ticked in the rail on the left.

5. **Pick an industry template** — Choose one of **Construction / Contracting**, **Interior design & fit-out**, **Trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC)**, **IT services & agency**, **Professional services**, **General Services** or **Generic**. Each card says what it sets up. Press **Continue**.

</Steps>

## What the template does

A template only sets things up for you. It never takes anything away.

- **Construction / Contracting** shows milestones and retention on jobs by default. It and the next four templates add the cost categories and expense accounts that kind of business uses, such as "Plant & equipment hire" (5300) for construction.
- **General Services** and **Generic** start the milestones and retention sections collapsed on jobs; you can still open them on any job.
- **Generic** and **Professional services** switch off Jobs & field. The step says "Jobs are switched off — turn them on in Settings › Modules if you need them."

If an account code or category name you need already exists, Spine keeps yours and doesn't add a second one.

## Moving around the wizard

- The rail lists the four stages and their steps, and "About 4 min left" counts down as you go. On a narrow window it becomes a bar at the top.
- **Back** opens the previous step with what you saved.
- Under each form, a tip says why the step matters and where to change it later.
- From the Business stage on, a preview shows the start of your first invoice and updates as you type. Until you've typed a name it says "Your business".
- If you refresh part-way, setup picks up at the first step you haven't saved.

<Aside type="tip">Everything here can be changed later in **Settings › Business profile** and **Settings › Industry template**. See [Settings and data export](../../settings/).</Aside>
