When your company's Spine server is upgraded, it may need a newer version of the field app. Updating keeps your sign-in and anything waiting to send, so you can pick up where you left off.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone with the field app installed. During the beta, an Owner shares each new Android version with the crew.</Aside>

## Install a newer beta

<Steps>
1. **Get the new version** — Open the link your office sends for the new version and download it.
2. **Install it over the old one** — Press **Install**. You don't need to delete the old app first; the new one replaces it.
3. **Check the version** — Open **Profile**. The version line at the bottom shows the new number, with **Beta** during the beta, for example "Spine 1.0.0-beta.2 (14) · Beta".
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">Don't delete the app to update it. Deleting it removes anything still waiting to send, and you'll need to sign in again.</Aside>

## When the app says "Update Spine to keep going"

If your server needs a newer app than the one on your phone, the app stops and shows **Update Spine to keep going**: "Your company's Spine server needs a newer version of this app. It only takes a minute."

Anything waiting to send stays on the phone: "_n_ items waiting to send are kept on this phone and sent after you update." Sync shows them with "Update Spine to send them · they're kept on this phone".

The screen has **Open App Store** (iPhone) or **Open Google Play** (Android). During the beta the app isn't in the stores yet, so ask your office for the newer version and install it as above. Once you've updated, open the app; waiting items send on their own.

<Aside type="tip">Not sure which version you have? Your office can compare the version in **Profile** with the one they shared.</Aside>
