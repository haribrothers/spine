When you finish a stage of a job, mark its milestone complete from the phone. The office is told as soon as it reaches the server, so they can bill that stage. You can add photos of the finished work and a note. With no signal, the completion is saved on the phone and sent later.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Field members can always mark milestones complete on jobs they're on, whoever set the milestones up. Owners can too. Staff need **Define/edit own milestones** (on their own jobs) or **Edit others' milestones** from their Team. Needs the **Jobs & field** module.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the job** — From **Today** or **Jobs**, open the job.
2. **Start completing it** — Under **Milestones**, press **Mark complete** on the pending milestone. A sheet opens with the milestone, its due date and the job.
3. **Add photos (optional)** — Under **Photos of the finished work**, press **Add** to open the camera. Take the photos and press **Done** to bring them back to the sheet. To drop one, press **Remove photo** on it.
4. **Add a note (optional)** — In **Note for the office (optional)**, say what's done and anything they should know.
5. **Confirm** — Press **Mark complete**.
</Steps>

The sheet reminds you: "Your office is told straight away so they can bill this stage. No signal? It's saved and sent when you're back online."

## What happens next

- Online, the milestone shows **Completed** on the job and the office gets a notification.
- Offline, it shows **Completion waiting** until it sends. The sync pill on Today counts it. See [Work with no signal](../work-offline/).
- The office raises the invoice for that stage on the web. You never see milestone amounts or invoices in the app.

## If someone else got there first

If two people complete the same milestone, it's completed only once. The first completion to reach the server counts. Yours shows as sent in **Sync** with "Already completed by _name_", and nothing changes on the job.

<Aside type="caution">There's no way to undo a completion from the phone. If you marked the wrong milestone, tell your office.</Aside>
