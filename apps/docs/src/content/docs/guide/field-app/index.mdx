The field app is Spine on your crew's phones. It shows the jobs you're on with their addresses and contacts, and lets you take site photos, mark milestones complete, log expenses with a receipt photo, and show a quote to the customer for them to sign. Everything goes to your own company's Spine server, not anywhere else.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can use it">Anyone in your business can sign in to the field app with their Spine email and password. It's made for **Field** members, whose rights are fixed: they see only the jobs they're on the crew of and their own expenses. During the beta the app is for Android phones only; iPhone joins later.</Aside>

## The main ideas

- **Tabs.** The app has four tabs: **Today**, **Jobs**, **Expenses** and **Search**. On iPhone, Search is the search button in the tab bar. Your picture or initials at the top right of every tab opens **Profile**, and the bell on Today opens **Notifications**.
- **Your jobs only.** The app lists the jobs your office has put you on the crew for. If you're taken off a crew, the job leaves your list.
- **No money figures.** The app never shows invoices, payments, receivables, payables or reports. The only money you see is your own expenses and the quote you're showing a customer. A job's contract value and milestone amounts are never shown.
- **Works with no signal.** What the app last downloaded stays on the phone. Photos, expenses and milestone completions are saved on the phone and sent when you're back online. Showing a quote for the customer to accept or decline needs a connection.
- **Sync.** The pill at the top of Today says **All synced** or how many items are waiting. Tap it to see what's waiting, what was sent today and anything that needs fixing.
- **Modules.** When an Owner switches off **Jobs & field** in **Settings › Modules**, the Jobs tab, job cards and job figures are hidden and the app is for expenses. When **Leads & meetings** is off, your next meeting isn't shown.
- **Light and dark.** The app follows your phone's light or dark mode, or the one you choose in Profile.
