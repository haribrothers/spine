The field app works with your own company's Spine server, so signing in asks for three things: the server address, your email and your password. You stay signed in on the phone until you sign out, so you don't type passwords on site.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone with an active Spine account in your business. Use the email your office invited you with.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open Sign in** — Open the app. After the welcome screen (or if you signed out), you see **Sign in** and "Use the email your office invited you with."
2. **Enter the server** — In **Server**, type the link you open Spine with on a computer.
3. **Enter your details** — Fill in **Email** and **Password**.
4. **Sign in** — Press **Sign in**. You land on **Today**.
</Steps>

## If sign-in doesn't work

Each message shows under the field it's about, and what you typed stays.

- "Can't reach that server. Check the address — it's the link you open Spine with on a computer." The address is wrong or the server isn't answering. Check it against the link on a computer.
- "That email and password don't match. Check both, or reset your password." Check both, or reset your password.
- "You're offline. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data to sign in." Signing in needs a connection.
- "Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a minute, then try again."

## Forgot your password

Press **Forgot password?**. Your server's reset page opens in the phone's browser. Follow the emailed link, then sign in with the new password.

## If an Owner set your password

An Owner can set a new password for you if you're locked out. The next time you sign in with it, the app shows **Choose your own password** before anything else. Enter a **New password** and **Confirm password** — "At least 8 characters, not the one you were given." — and press **Save and continue**.

<Aside type="tip">You don't need to sign in each day. When you reopen the app days later, you go straight to Today, until you sign out or your session ends.</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">If your office removes your access, the app signs you out the next time it talks to the server and says "Your access to _business_ was removed."</Aside>
