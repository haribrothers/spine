Site photos record progress and back up your work. Photos you take in the field app are saved on the phone straight away, tagged to the job, and upload in the background. With no signal, they wait and send when you're back online. They then appear on the job's **Photos** tab on the web.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone signed in to the field app, on jobs they're on the crew of. Needs the **Jobs & field** module.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the camera** — On a job, press **Add photos** (the camera button at the top on iPhone, the round button on Android). Or press **Add photo** on Today.
2. **Allow the camera (first time only)** — The app explains "Allow camera access" and why. Press **Continue**, then allow it when your phone asks. **Not now** takes you back.
3. **Check the job** — The job shows at the top of the camera. Tap it to choose a different job.
4. **Take the photos** — Press the shutter for each photo. Use the flash button if you need it. The bottom shows "_n_ taken · saved on this phone".
5. **Review them** — Press **Done**. Add a **Caption** to each photo ("What does this photo show?"), check the **Job**, and choose a **Milestone (optional)**. **Retake** drops the photo shown and goes back to the camera; **Take more photos** adds more.
6. **Save** — Press **Save _n_ photos**.
</Steps>

"Photos upload in the background. You can keep working — check progress from the sync pill on Today." Until they've sent, the job shows them with a **Waiting** badge.

## Look at a photo

Tap any photo on a job, or on its **Photos** screen, to open it full screen. The top shows where you are ("3 of 12"); the bottom shows its caption, the milestone (or "Site photo") and who added it when.

- **Swipe** left or right to move between photos. With VoiceOver or TalkBack, use the **Next photo** and **Previous photo** actions.
- **Pinch** or **double-tap** to zoom, and drag to look around. Double-tap again to fit it back.
- **Swipe down** or press **✕** to close.

A photo still waiting to send shows **Waiting to send**. Offline, a photo the phone hasn't kept says "This photo will show when you're back online." There's no Save or Share: site photos stay out of your camera roll.

## Delete a photo

On a photo you're allowed to delete, press **Delete photo**, then **Delete photo** again in "Delete this photo?". It's removed from the job and deleted from storage, and the next photo shows.

- You can delete your own photos while the job is open and the photo's milestone isn't invoiced. Field crew can't delete other people's photos.
- **Deleting needs a connection.** Offline, **Delete photo** is dimmed with "Connect to delete this photo."
- **A photo that hasn't sent yet** has **Discard** instead: "It hasn't been sent yet. Discarding deletes it from this phone." Press **Discard** to confirm, or **Keep**.

## If you said no to the camera

The explainer tells you where to turn it on: on iPhone, **Settings › Spine › Camera**; on Android, **Settings › Apps › Spine › Permissions**. Once your phone won't ask again, **Continue** reads **Open Settings** and takes you there.

<Aside type="caution">Closing the camera before you save asks "Discard _n_ photos?" — "They haven't been saved to the job." Press **Keep taking** to go back, or **Discard** to lose them.</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">Photos go only to your company's Spine server, never anywhere else. They're made smaller before they're saved, so they upload quickly.</Aside>
