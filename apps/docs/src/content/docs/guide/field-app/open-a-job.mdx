The **Jobs** tab lists every job you're on, not only the ones due this week. Opening a job gives you what you need on site: the address, who to call, the milestones, the photos so far and any quote waiting for the customer.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone signed in to the field app, for the jobs they're on the crew of. Needs the **Jobs & field** module.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open Jobs** — Tap the **Jobs** tab. Under **Assigned to you** you see each job's name, customer, town and milestone status. Pull down to refresh.
2. **Open a job** — Tap it. The job shows its name, its number and customer (such as "JOB-007 · Alan Brooks"), its status, and the site address.
3. **Get there** — Press **Directions** to open your maps app at the site, or the call button to ring the job's contact.
4. **Check the milestones** — Under **Milestones**, a pending one shows its due date and **Mark complete**. A finished one shows **Completed**, and one you completed with no signal shows **Completion waiting**.
5. **See the photos** — Under **Photos**, press **See all** to open every photo on the job, grouped by day, newest first. Photos not sent yet carry a **Waiting** badge.
6. **Present a quote** — Under **Quotes to present**, a quote waiting for the customer shows **Present**.
</Steps>

To add photos, press **Add photos**: the camera button at the top on iPhone, or the round button at the bottom on Android. See [Take site photos for a job](../take-site-photos/).

## What you won't see

The job's contract value, milestone amounts and invoices are never shown in the app. Ask your office if you need a figure.

## If your job list is empty

If you're on no jobs, the tab says "No jobs assigned to you yet" and "When your office adds you to a job's crew, it shows up here with its address and contact." Press **Check again** after your office has added you.

<Aside type="caution">If a job says "This job isn't on your list", your office may have taken you off its crew. Pull down on **Jobs** to refresh.</Aside>

To find a job by customer or street, use the [Search](../search/) tab. The Jobs list has no search of its own.
