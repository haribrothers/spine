If the customer decides not to go ahead while you're showing them a quote, record it there. The quote is marked declined, with their reason if they give one, and the office is told straight away so nobody keeps chasing it.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Field members, on jobs they're on. Owners. Staff whose Team has **Accept a quote in person**. Needs a connection.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Present the quote** — On the job, under **Quotes to present**, press **Present**. See [Present a quote and take the customer's signature](../present-a-quote/).
2. **Decline** — Press **Decline** at the bottom.
3. **Check the details** — A sheet titled **Decline quote** says who is declining which quote for what total, and that your office is told straight away.
4. **Add a reason (optional)** — In **Reason (optional)**, note why, for example the price or the timing.
5. **Confirm** — Press **Decline quote**. **Cancel** goes back to the quote.
</Steps>

## What happens next

The quote is recorded as declined, with the reason as its comment, and the office gets a notification. The quote leaves **Quotes to present** on the job.

## With no signal

Declining can't be saved offline. The sheet says "Needs a connection — declining can't be saved offline." Move somewhere with signal and try again.

## If it doesn't save

You see "Couldn't record the decline" and "Nothing was saved. Try again."

<Aside type="caution">If the office has already changed the quote, or the customer answered it another way, the app says "This quote was already answered" — "Go back to the job to see what's changed."</Aside>

<Aside type="danger">A declined quote can't be accepted afterwards from the phone. If the customer changes their mind, your office can send them a revised quote.</Aside>
