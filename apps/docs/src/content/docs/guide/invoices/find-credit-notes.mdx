The Credit Notes list shows every correction you've made to issued invoices, and where each one's money went: off the invoice, applied to another, refunded, or still waiting as credit. Use it to see what customers can still use or be paid back.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Credit Notes**. Row actions show only when you have the right for them. Field users never see credit notes.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Credit Notes** — Go to **Billing › Credit Notes**.

2. **Read the cards** — **Credited this month** is the total of credit notes issued this month. **Credit to use** is the credit customers still hold from credit notes, and how many customers hold it. **Refunded this month** is credit-note credit paid back this month.

3. **Show credit still to use** — Press **Credit to use** to list only credit notes with credit left. Press it again to clear it.

4. **Filter** — Use **Where it went** (**Off the invoice**, **Credit left**, **Applied**, **Refunded**) and **Contact**.

5. **Search** — Type in **Search credit notes, invoices or reasons**. It matches the number, customer, invoice number, reason and line names.

6. **Open one** — Click its row to open its page.

</Steps>

## Reading a row

Each row shows the number with the reason under it, the customer, the invoice (a link), the date, the total, and **Where it went**: "Off INV-0016", "Applied $1,000.00", "Refunded $500.00", "$4,000.00 left", or a mix such as "Off INV-0016 · $4,000.00 left". There's no status badge; a credit note left unissued shows "Draft".

The row's menu offers **View**, **Apply credit** and **Refund** (while credit is left), **Email credit note** and **Download PDF**. Apply, refund and email open the credit note's page with that step's dialog. See [Use a credit note's credit](../use-a-credit-notes-credit/).

## What happened to a credit note

On its page, **Activity** lists, newest first: issued against the invoice (and by whom), emailed (or "Email failed"), credit applied to an invoice, refunded, and refund deleted, with who and when. **View history** shows every field change.

<Aside type="tip">
  Your search, filters and page are kept in the address, so Back and bookmarks return to the same list. **Clear filters** resets it. The refresh button (**Refresh list**) reloads it in place, and so does coming back after 30 seconds away. If there are none yet, the list says "No credit notes yet".
</Aside>

You can also find a credit note from the global search, or from the **Credit notes** section of the invoice it corrects.
