Contracts change. You can pause a schedule while a customer is on hold, change what it bills, or delete it when the contract ends. Invoices a schedule has already created are never changed by any of this.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Manage recurring invoices**, with the **Recurring documents** module switched on in **Settings › Modules**.
</Aside>

## Read the list

Go to **Invoices** and press **Recurring invoices**. Each schedule shows **Name**, **Customer**, **Repeats** (for example "Monthly · 1st"), **Next invoice**, **Amount**, **Status** (**Active**, **Paused** or **Finished**) and **When it runs** ("Issue and email" or "Create draft").

<Screenshot id="invoices-recurring-list" alt="The Recurring invoices list with each schedule's name, customer, repeats, next invoice, amount, status and when it runs" />

## Pause or resume

<Steps>

1. **Open the row's menu** — On the schedule's row, open its menu.

2. **Choose Pause** — A toast says **Schedule paused**: it won't create invoices until you resume it. Its next invoice shows "—".

3. **Choose Resume later** — Open the menu again and choose **Resume**. The toast gives the next invoice date.

</Steps>

When you resume, the next invoice is the next date still to come. Dates missed while it was paused aren't billed.

## Change the schedule or its lines

Choose **Edit** in the row's menu. **Edit recurring invoice** opens with everything you set. Change the lines, amounts or timing, then press **Save schedule**. Only invoices created from now on use the changes.

## Delete a schedule

<Steps>

1. **Choose Delete** — In the row's menu, choose **Delete**.

2. **Confirm** — **Delete this schedule?** says "Invoices it already created are kept." Press **Delete schedule**, or **Cancel**.

</Steps>

A toast says **Schedule deleted**.

<Aside type="danger">
  Deleting a schedule can't be undone. If you might need it again, pause it instead.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  To correct an invoice a schedule already issued, [issue a credit note](../issue-a-credit-note/) or [void it](../void-an-invoice/), as with any issued invoice.
</Aside>
