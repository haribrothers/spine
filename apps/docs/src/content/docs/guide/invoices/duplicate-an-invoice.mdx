When you bill the same work again, duplicate an earlier invoice instead of building it from scratch. Spine makes a new draft with the same customer and lines. The original isn't changed, whatever its status.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own invoices**. The copy is your own draft.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the invoice to copy** — Go to **Invoices** and open any invoice: a draft, issued, paid or void.

2. **Choose Duplicate as new draft** — Open **More** and choose **Duplicate as new draft**. From the list, choose **Duplicate** in the row's menu.

3. **Check the new draft** — The builder opens with the copy, and a toast says, for example, "Duplicated from INV-0012. Check the dates and lines, then save."

4. **Change what's different and save** — Update the lines or amounts for this job, then press **Save draft**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="invoices-detail" alt="An issued invoice's page with its status, customer and dates, the Total, Paid and Balance due strip, the lines table and the Activity panel" />

## What's copied

- The customer, currency, lines, notes and terms.
- The type. A progress or retention-release invoice becomes a standard invoice, because the copy isn't tied to a milestone.
- A line whose catalogue item has since been archived is copied as it was typed.

## What's new

- No number. It's a draft until you [issue it](../issue-an-invoice/).
- Today's issue date, and a due date from your payment terms.
- Its **Activity** says it was duplicated from the original.

<Aside type="tip">
  An opening-balance invoice (brought in from your old system) has no lines, so it can't be duplicated.
</Aside>
