A credit note corrects an issued invoice: something returned, not delivered or over-billed. It takes the tax back along with the amount, so what you owe the tax office drops with what the customer owes you. The invoice itself stays exactly as it was issued.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own credit notes** and **Issue a credit note**. These rights are separate from the invoice rights.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start the credit note** — Open the issued invoice, open **More** and choose **Issue credit note**. Or go to **Credit Notes**, press **New credit note**, search for the invoice and press **Continue**.

2. **Choose how to credit** — **Issue a credit note** offers **Credit lines** (the default) or **Credit an amount**.

3. **Credit lines** — Tick each line to credit and set how many, up to what's left (for example "3 of 5 left"). A line already credited in full shows **Fully credited** and can't be ticked.

4. **Or credit an amount** — Enter the **Amount to credit**, tax included. Spine spreads it over the invoice's lines by what's left to credit on each, and takes back each part's tax at that line's rate.

5. **Give a reason** — Enter the **Reason**, for example "Canopy reduced from 36 to 24 m² — 12 m² not built." The customer sees it on the credit note.

6. **Check the totals** — The dialog shows **Subtotal**, the tax by name (such as CGST and SGST, or VAT) and **Total**.

7. **Issue it** — Press **Issue credit note**.

</Steps>

## What happens next

- A toast says, for example, "CRN-0007 issued". It gets the next credit-note number and its PDF is saved.
- The invoice's balance drops, and the credit note is listed under **Credit notes** on the invoice, with a link to its own page.
- The tax comes off your tax account, and GST returns show it by component.

## When the credit is more than what's owed

You can credit an invoice that's part paid or fully paid. When the total is more than the balance, the dialog says how it splits, for example "$2,000.00 reduces what's owed on INV-0016; $4,000.00 becomes credit for" the customer. The extra is held as the customer's credit, to [apply to another invoice or refund](../use-a-credit-notes-credit/).

<Aside type="danger">
  A credit note can't be edited or deleted once it's issued. Check the lines and reason before you press **Issue credit note**.
</Aside>

## If Spine won't issue it

- "Line 2 has 3 left to credit." — you set a quantity above what's left.
- "You can credit up to … on this invoice." — the total is more than is left to credit.
- No **Issue credit note** on the invoice — everything on it has been credited already, or it's a deposit or opening-balance invoice.
