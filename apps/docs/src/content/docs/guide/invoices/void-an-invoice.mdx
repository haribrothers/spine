Void an invoice when it should never have been issued: the wrong customer, a duplicate, or work that was called off. Voiding keeps the invoice, its number and its lines, marks it **Void** with your reason, and posts a reversing entry so it no longer counts in your books.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Void an invoice**. Without it, **Void invoice** is hidden.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the invoice** — Go to **Invoices** and open the issued invoice.

2. **Choose Void invoice** — Open **More** and choose **Void invoice**. From the list, choose **Void** in the row's menu; the invoice opens with the same dialog.

3. **Give a reason** — **Void this invoice?** explains that it posts a reversing entry and can't be undone. Enter the **Reason**.

4. **Confirm** — Press **Void invoice**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="invoices-void" alt="The Void this invoice? dialog with the required Reason box and the Void invoice button" />

## What happens next

- A toast says **Invoice voided**.
- The invoice shows **Void**, with "Voided on" the date and your reason. Its **Activity** records who voided it.
- In the Invoices list it shows **Void**, and its balance shows "—".
- Nothing is deleted. The number stays used, so your sequence has no gaps.
- It's no longer counted as owed, reminded or included in your receivables.

<Aside type="danger">
  Voiding can't be undone. If the customer still owes part of the invoice, [issue a credit note](../issue-a-credit-note/) for the part that's wrong instead.
</Aside>

## When you can't void

- **No reason** — you'll see "A reason is required to void an invoice."
- **Payments are on it** — Spine refuses with "Cannot void an invoice with payments allocated against it". Issue a credit note instead. If the payment was put on this invoice by mistake, move it off first; see [Payments and receivables](../../payments/).
- **A draft** — a draft isn't issued, so there's nothing to void. [Delete it](../edit-or-delete-a-draft-invoice/) instead.
- **An opening balance** — invoices brought in from your old system can't be voided.
