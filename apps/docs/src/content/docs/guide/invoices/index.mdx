Invoices is where you bill customers for work. You build an invoice as a draft, issue it when it's ready, email it as a PDF and chase it until it's paid. When an issued invoice is wrong, you void it or issue a credit note against it. You never edit it. **Invoices** and **Credit Notes** sit under **Billing** in the sidebar.

## The main ideas

- **A draft can change; an issued invoice can't.** A draft has no number and isn't in your books. You can edit or delete it as often as you like.
- **Issuing locks the invoice.** It gets the next number in sequence, it's posted to your books, and its PDF is saved exactly as the customer gets it. After that it can only be voided or credited.
- **Four kinds of invoice.** A **Standard** invoice bills work. An **Advance** invoice asks for a deposit before work starts. A **Progress** invoice bills a job's milestone, and can hold back retention. A **Retention release** invoice collects that retention at the end.
- **Mistakes are corrected, not erased.** Void an invoice with a reason when it shouldn't exist. Issue a credit note when part or all of it should come off. Both keep the original and leave a record.
- **Credit belongs to the customer.** A credit note for more than is still owed becomes credit the customer can use on another invoice or be paid back.

## Who can do what

An Owner sets each Team's rights in **Settings › Team › Teams** (see [Settings](../settings/)). The invoice rights are **Create/edit own invoices**, **Edit others' invoices**, **Delete own invoices**, **Delete others' invoices**, **Issue an invoice**, **Void an invoice**, **Email an invoice**, **Send a payment reminder** and **Manage recurring invoices**. Credit notes have their own set, including **Issue a credit note**, which work separately from the invoice rights. So a Team can issue invoices without being able to void them, and someone can issue a colleague's draft without being able to edit it.

Recording payments, following up receivables and refunding payments have their own guide: see [Payments and receivables](../payments/).
