Use the invoice builder to bill work that didn't start as a quote, or anything you want to bill by hand. A new invoice saves as a draft: it has no number and nothing goes into your books until you issue it.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own invoices**. **Save and issue** also needs **Issue an invoice**.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start the invoice** — Go to **Invoices** and press **New invoice**.

2. **Choose the customer** — Pick the **Contact**. Check the **Currency**, and leave **Type** on **Standard** for an ordinary invoice.

3. **Set the dates** — Leave **Issue date** blank to use the day you issue it. **Due date** starts from your payment terms. Press **On receipt**, **+15 days**, **+30 days** or **+60 days** to change it; the one that matches shows pressed.

4. **Add the lines** — Press **Add item** and fill in **Item**, **Qty**, **Rate**, **Disc. %** and **Tax** for each line. Use **Add heading** to group lines and **Add text line** for a note between them. Drag a line, use its menu, or press Alt + ↑ or ↓ to move it.

5. **Add notes and terms** — Fill in **Notes** (the customer sees them on the invoice) and **Terms & conditions**.

6. **Save it** — Press **Save as draft**. To issue it straight away, press **Save and issue** instead.

</Steps>

## Totals and tax

The bar at the bottom of the builder stays in view as you scroll. It shows **Subtotal**, **Tax** and **Total**, and they update as you type. Tax is worked out on each line and rounded once per line. Tick **Price includes tax** on a line when the rate you entered already has the tax in it.

Press **Preview PDF** to see the invoice as it is on screen. The preview carries a faint "DRAFT — Preview only — not a valid invoice" watermark, and nothing is saved.

## Your payment terms

The due date comes from **Invoices due in**, under **Settings › Documents** (Owners only). It takes 0 to 365 days; 0 means due on receipt, and new businesses start at 30. If an invoice is issued without a due date, it gets one from this setting, so it can become overdue and be reminded.

<Aside type="caution">
  If you haven't picked a contact, **Save as draft** shows "Select a contact before saving." If you leave the builder with unsaved changes, Spine asks **Leave without saving?** first.
</Aside>

- From an old invoice, use **Duplicate**. See [Duplicate an invoice](../duplicate-an-invoice/).
