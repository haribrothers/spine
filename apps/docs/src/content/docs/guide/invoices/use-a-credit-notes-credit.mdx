When a credit note is for more than the invoice still owed, the extra becomes credit for the customer. From the credit note's page you can put that credit against their other invoices, pay it back to them, or email them the credit note.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Credit Notes**. Applying credit needs **Apply payment credit**, refunding needs **Refund customers** (off by default for Staff), and emailing needs **Email an invoice**.
</Aside>

## Read the credit note's page

Open a credit note from **Credit Notes**, or from the invoice it corrects. The header shows its number, the customer, "Against INV-0016", the issue date and the reason. A strip shows where the money went: **Credited** (with its tax), **Off INV-0016**, **Applied**, **Refunded** and **Credit left**. One main button offers the next step: **Apply credit** while credit is left, **Email credit note** if it hasn't been emailed, otherwise **Download PDF**.

## Apply the credit to other invoices

<Steps>

1. **Press Apply credit** — The customer's open invoices in the same currency are listed with what each owes.

2. **Set the amounts** — Enter how much goes on each invoice, up to its balance and the credit left. The dialog says how much credit stays to use later.

3. **Apply** — Press **Apply credit**.

</Steps>

A toast says **Credit applied**, and the strip updates. Nothing is paid out; it comes off what the customer owes. If they have no open invoices to apply it to, the dialog says so and suggests a refund.

## Refund the credit

<Steps>

1. **Press Refund** — **Refund** sits beside the main button while credit is left.

2. **Fill in the refund** — Check **Refund from** (this credit note), then enter the **Amount**, **Date**, **Paid from** (the account the money leaves), **Method** and, if you like, a **Reference**.

3. **Send a confirmation** — Tick **Send refund confirmation** to email the customer a receipt-style note.

4. **Record it** — Press **Record refund**.

</Steps>

A toast says, for example, "Refunded $500.00 to" the customer. Their credit drops by the same amount, and the refund shows on their statement.

<Aside type="caution">
  A refund that isn't reconciled can be deleted from the credit note's **Refunds** list. **Delete this refund?** explains that it's reversed in your books and the credit comes back. More on refunds is in [Payments and receivables](../../payments/).
</Aside>

## Email the credit note

Press **Email credit note** (the main button, or under **More**). Check the email, keep **Attach the credit note PDF** ticked and press **Send credit note**. A toast says **Credit note emailed**, and **Activity** records it.
