A recurring invoice is a schedule plus a set of lines. On each date, Spine creates the invoice from those lines. It can issue and email it for you, or leave a draft for you to check first.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Manage recurring invoices**. It needs the **Recurring documents** module switched on in **Settings › Modules**. Without the right, **Recurring invoices** isn't shown.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Recurring invoices** — Go to **Invoices** and press **Recurring invoices**.

2. **Start a schedule** — Press **+ New recurring invoice**.

3. **Name it and pick the customer** — Enter a **Name**, such as "Monthly maintenance — Hotel lobby", and choose the **Customer**.

4. **Set when it repeats** — Choose **Repeats** (**Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Every 3 months** or **Yearly**) and **On**: a weekday, a day of the month from the 1st to the 28th, or **Last day of the month**.

5. **Set the start and end** — Pick **Starts** (today or later). For **Ends**, choose **Never**, **On a date** or **After a number of invoices**.

6. **Set the payment terms** — Choose **Payment due**: **On receipt**, or 7, 14, 30 or 60 days after issue.

7. **Choose what happens** — Under **When it runs**, choose **Issue the invoice and email it to the customer** or **Create a draft for me to review**.

8. **Add the lines** — Press **Edit lines**, add the items, then press **Done**.

9. **Save** — Press **Save schedule**.

</Steps>

A toast says **Schedule saved**, with the date of the first invoice. The schedule is listed as **Active** with its next invoice date.

## What happens on each date

Runs happen at 6:00 AM in your organisation's time zone. Each invoice uses the schedule's lines with the tax rates of that day.

- **Issue and email** — the invoice is issued with the next number, posted to your books and emailed to the customer, as if you'd done it yourself.
- **Create a draft** — Owners get a notification, "Recurring invoice ready to review: …". Open the draft, check it and [issue it](../issue-an-invoice/).

A schedule never makes more than one invoice for the same date, even if the server was off and catches up. When the end you set is reached, the schedule shows **Finished**.

## If Spine won't save

- "Pick today or a later start date." — the start date is in the past.
- "Pick a customer." or "Pick a start date." — a required field is empty.
- "End after at least 1 invoice." — the number of invoices is 0.

To change, pause or delete a schedule, see [Pause, change or stop a recurring invoice](../pause-or-stop-a-recurring-invoice/).
