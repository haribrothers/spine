Spine tells you when something needs you: a customer accepts a quote, a payment arrives, a milestone is completed, an expense is approved or a meeting is about to start. Every notice appears under the bell. Depending on what it is and what you've turned on, it can also arrive as a browser notification on your device, an email, or in your daily digest.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone gets notifications about what they're allowed to see. Each person sets their own browser notifications, digest and reminder time in **Settings › Your account › Notifications**. Owners set the business's default reminder time.</Aside>

## The main ideas

- **The bell** shows how many notifications you haven't read and opens a panel of recent ones. Read state is the same on every device.
- **Only what you can see.** Notifications follow permissions. Field users never hear about money, and a private meeting only notifies its organiser and attendees.
- **Browser notifications** reach you even when Spine isn't the tab you're looking at. You turn them on per device.
- **The daily digest** is one email each morning with what needs you today and what happened yesterday.
- **Meeting reminders** arrive a set time before meetings you organise or have accepted.
- **Switched-off modules are quiet.** Nothing is sent about a module that's off in **Settings › Modules**.
