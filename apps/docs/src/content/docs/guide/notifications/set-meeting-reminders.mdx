Spine reminds you shortly before a meeting starts, by every channel you have — the bell, browser notifications and email. The business has a default lead time, and each person can choose their own.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone sets their own reminder. Only Owners set the business default.</Aside>

## Set your own reminder time

<Steps>

1. **Open your notification settings** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, then **Notifications** under **Your account**.

2. **Choose a lead time** — Under **Meeting reminders**, pick **5 min**, **15 min**, **30 min** or **60 min**, or **Business default** to follow the business's setting.

</Steps>

It saves at once. The toast says, for example, "You'll be reminded 30 minutes before."

## Set the business default

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, then **Meeting reminders** under **Sales & meetings**.

2. **Choose the default** — Pick **5 minutes before**, **15 minutes before**, **30 minutes before** or **60 minutes before**, and press **Save**. It starts at 15 minutes.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="notifications-business-reminders" alt="Settings, Meeting reminders: the business default lead time with 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes before" />

The default applies to everyone who hasn't chosen their own.

## Who gets a reminder

- **You get one** for meetings you organise, and meetings you've accepted or tentatively accepted.
- **You don't** for meetings you've declined.
- **Recurring meetings** remind you once for each occurrence. Cancelled occurrences send nothing.
- **Moved meetings** — if a meeting's time changes after you were reminded, you're reminded again before the new time.

<Aside type="tip">Field users get meeting reminders in the field app too.</Aside>

See [Meetings](../../meetings/) to schedule meetings and invite people.
