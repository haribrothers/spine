The bell is the one place every notification arrives, whatever else you've turned on. It shows how many you haven't read, so you don't have to rely on email to notice things.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone with a sign-in. You only see notifications meant for you.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Find the bell** — It's at the foot of the sidebar. On a phone-width browser, it's in the top bar. A count shows how many are unread — "9+" when there are more than nine.

2. **Open the panel** — Press the bell. **Notifications** opens from the right with "Recent activity across the org." and your notifications newest first. Each has a message, when it happened and a link. On a phone-width browser the panel fills the screen.

3. **Open one** — Press a notification to go to its record — the quote, job, expense or meeting. It's marked read.

4. **Mark everything read** — Press **Mark all read**. The count goes to zero and stays at zero after a reload.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="notifications-drawer" alt="The Notifications panel open from the bell, titled Recent activity across the org., with Mark all read and recent notifications, each with a message, time and link" />

## Things to know

- **Read everywhere.** Read one on your laptop and it's read on your other browsers and devices too.
- **New ones arrive by themselves.** The count goes up within about 20 seconds of a new notification, without reloading the page.
- **Nothing yet?** The panel says "No notifications yet" and "You'll see updates here as they happen."

<Aside type="tip">To hear about notifications when Spine isn't open, [turn on browser notifications](../turn-on-browser-notifications/). For a morning summary, use the [daily digest](../choose-your-daily-digest/).</Aside>
