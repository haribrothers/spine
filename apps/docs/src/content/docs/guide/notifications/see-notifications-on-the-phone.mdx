Field crew get their notifications in the Spine field app: what happened to their expenses, changes to their crews and their meetings. They share read state with the web, so something read on the phone is read everywhere.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Field users, in the field app. Field notifications never include anything about money they can't see.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Find the bell** — On the **Today** screen, the bell in the header shows how many are unread — "9+" when there are more than nine.

2. **Open your notifications** — Tap the bell. The list is split into **New** and **Earlier**. Each item has an icon, a message, a detail line and when it happened. Unread items carry a dot on iPhone, or a tinted row on Android.

3. **Open one** — Tap a notification to go to the expense, job or meeting. It's marked read.

4. **Mark them all read** — Tap **Read all**.

</Steps>

## What Field users are notified about

- **Expense decisions** — "Sarah Mendes approved your expense", or rejected, with the reason so you can fix it.
- **Crew changes** — being added to or removed from a job's crew.
- **Meetings** — invitations, changes and starting-soon reminders.

Nothing is sent about a module the business has switched off.

## When there's nothing new

The list says "You're all caught up" and "Expense decisions, crew changes and meeting invitations show up here."

<Aside type="tip">Field users can choose their own meeting reminder time and daily digest in **Settings › Your account › Notifications** on the web. See [Set your meeting reminders](../set-meeting-reminders/).</Aside>
