Every finished reconciliation is kept, so you can show your accountant how the bank and the books agree. If the latest one was wrong, you can undo it: its entries go back to unreconciled and nothing is deleted.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Reconcile accounts**.
</Aside>

## See past reconciliations

Open the account's register and choose **Reconciliations** under **More**. Each finished reconciliation shows its **Statement end date**, **Closing balance**, **Entries matched**, who finished it and when. The newest is marked **Latest**.

<Screenshot id="banking-reconciliations" alt="An account's Reconciliations list with statement end dates, closing balances, entries matched and the Latest one" />

## Open the reconciliation report

Press a row, or choose **View** in its menu. The report opens read-only with:

- the account, statement end date and closing balance, the opening and cleared balances, and who finished it and when;
- a summary that reads: statement closing balance + deposits not yet on the statement − payments not yet on the statement = balance in Spine on the end date;
- **Matched lines** with the entries they matched, the **Excluded** lines with your notes, and **What's outstanding** — entries in Spine up to the end date that weren't on the statement.

Choose **Download report** to save it as a PDF. It's made when you ask for it, in light colours, and named after the account and end date.

## Undo the latest reconciliation

<Steps>

1. **Find the latest one** — In **Reconciliations**, open the menu on the row marked **Latest** and choose **Undo**. You can also press **Undo** on its report page.

2. **Read the confirmation** — "Undo this reconciliation?" says its entries go back to unreconciled, nothing is deleted, and its statement lines go back to unmatched.

3. **Confirm** — Press **Undo reconciliation**. The toast reads **Reconciliation undone**.

</Steps>

## What happens next

The entries lose their **Reconciled** tick, and you can void or delete the documents behind them again. The statement lines are still imported; reconcile the account again when you're ready. See [Reconcile an account](../reconcile-an-account/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Only the latest can be undone">
  Older reconciliations don't offer **Undo** — Spine says "Undo the later reconciliations first." Undo them one at a time, newest first.
</Aside>
