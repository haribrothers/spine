Some money moves without an invoice, bill or expense behind it: you put your own money in, take drawings, receive or repay a loan, earn interest or pay bank charges. Record these as money in or money out so the account's balance is complete, without writing a journal.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Record transfers and other money in or out**.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the dialog** — On **Banking**, press **Money in** or **Money out**. From an account's row menu or **Record** in its register, that account is already chosen.

2. **Choose the account** — Pick **Into account** (money in) or **From account** (money out).

3. **Choose a category** — Use a shortcut — **Owner's capital**, **Loan received** or **Interest received** for money in; **Owner's drawings**, **Loan repayment** or **Bank charges** for money out — or search for any other account.

4. **Fill in the rest** — Enter the **Date** and **Amount**. For money out, choose a **Tax** rate if the amount includes tax. Add a **Contact** and a **Description** if they help.

5. **Record it** — Press **Record money in** or **Record money out**. Spine asks first, for example "Record money out?" with the amount, account, category, date and any tax, and says it's posted to your books.

6. **Confirm** — Press **Record**. The toast reads **Money in recorded** or **Money out recorded**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="banking-money-out" alt="The Money out dialog with From account, Date, Category shortcuts, Amount, Tax and Description" />

## What happens next

The entry shows in the register as "Money in — " or "Money out — " with its category. Open it to see its details or delete it.

<Aside type="tip" title="Tax on bank charges">
  If a bank charge includes tax, choose the rate under **Tax**. The dialog says how much tax is included; that part goes to Input Tax Receivable, so you can claim it.
</Aside>

## What you can't record here

Receivables, payables, tax, retention, customer advances and opening adjustments aren't offered as categories. Use a payment, bill or tax payment for these, so the right customer, vendor or tax return is updated. For money between your own accounts, use a [transfer](../move-money-between-accounts/).

## Delete an entry

Open the entry in the register and press **Delete entry**. Spine asks first, then posts a reversing entry today. Entries that are part of a finished reconciliation can't be deleted until it's undone.
