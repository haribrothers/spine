When you close a bank account or stop using a card, archive it in Spine. It stops appearing in the account pickers on payments, expenses and transfers, but its register, reconciliations and ledger history stay.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners only.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Empty the account first** — The balance must be zero. If money is left, [move it to another account](../move-money-between-accounts/) first.

2. **Choose Archive** — On **Banking**, open the account's row menu and choose **Archive**.

3. **Confirm** — "Archive …?" says the account leaves the pickers and keeps its history. Press **Archive account**, or **Go back** to keep it.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="banking-archive" alt="The Banking page's Archived tab, where archived accounts are listed and can be restored" />

The toast reads **Account archived** and the account moves to the **Archived** tab.

## When you can't archive an account

- **It still has money in it.** Spine says "Move the … out of this account before archiving it." and nothing changes.
- **It's the default account.** Spine says "Choose another default account first." Choose **Set as default** on another account, then archive this one.

## Restore an account

Open the **Archived** tab, open the account's row menu and choose **Restore**. The toast reads **Account restored** and the account is back in the pickers.

<Aside type="tip">
  You can still open an archived account's register to look back at what moved through it. Choose **Open register** in its row menu.
</Aside>
