Each account's register lists everything that moved through it, with a running balance — like your bank app, but from your books. Use it to compare Spine with the bank, find a payment, or see what still needs reconciling.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **View bank balances and registers**. Without it, Banking shows the no-access page.
</Aside>

## See all your accounts

**Banking** lists each account with its type, name (with **Default**), bank and last four digits, **Balance in Spine** ("owed" on a card), when it was last reconciled (or **Never reconciled**) and how many entries are unreconciled. The cards show **Bank and cash**, **Owed on cards**, **Not yet reconciled** and **Last reconciled**. Press **Not yet reconciled** to see only accounts with unreconciled entries. Filter by type (**All types**, **Bank**, **Cash**, **Card**) or search.

## Open and filter a register

<Steps>

1. **Open the account** — On **Banking**, press the account's name or choose **Open register** in its row menu. Pressing the unreconciled count opens it on unreconciled entries only.

2. **Read the cards** — **Balance in Spine** is today's balance. **Money in** and **Money out** cover the range (**Paid off** and **Spent** on a card). **Unreconciled** counts entries in the range; press it to show only those.

3. **Filter** — Choose a range (**This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Last 90 days**, **This year** or **Custom range**), a type (**All entries**, **Customer payments**, **Vendor payments**, **Expenses**, **Transfers**, **Money in and out**, **Journals and other**) and **Show** (**All** or **Unreconciled only**). Search matches what, contact or reference.

4. **Open an entry** — Press a row. A payment, bill or expense opens its own page. A transfer or money in or out entry opens a drawer with its details.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="banking-register" alt="An account's register with Balance in Spine, Money in, Money out and Unreconciled cards and the list of entries" />

Each row has the date, what it was, the contact, the reference, the amount in or out and the running balance. The running balance always starts from the true balance before your range. A reconciled entry shows **Reconciled** with a tick; a deleted one says **Deleted**.

## Export the register

Press **Export** and choose **CSV**, **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF**. The file has the rows on screen with the opening and closing balance.

## Delete a transfer or money in or out entry

In the entry's drawer, press **Delete entry** (or **Delete transfer**). Spine asks first and posts a reversing entry today; the entry stays in the register, marked deleted.

<Aside type="caution">
  Once an entry is part of a finished reconciliation, it can't be deleted. Undo that reconciliation first.
</Aside>

From the header, **Reconcile** starts reconciling, **Record** offers **Money in**, **Money out** and **Transfer** with this account chosen, and **More** has **Import statement**, **Reconciliations** and **Edit account**.
