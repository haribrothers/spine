To reconcile an account, Spine needs the bank's side of the story. Download a statement from your online banking as a CSV file and import it. Spine remembers how your bank's columns map, so next time it's one step.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Reconcile accounts**.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Export the statement from your bank** — In your online banking, download the account's statement as CSV.

2. **Open the import** — On **Banking**, open the account's row menu and choose **Import statement**. You can also find it under **More** in the account's register, or on the reconcile page. The **Import statement** dialog opens with the account's name.

3. **Choose the file** — Press **Choose file** and pick the CSV. Spine shows how many rows it has and the **First rows** of the file.

4. **Map the columns** — The first time, choose which column holds the **Date** (and its **Date format**), the **Description** and the **Reference**. Under **Amount columns**, choose **One amount column (money in positive)** or **Separate money in and money out columns**, and pick them. **Balance** is optional but helps later. Next time, the saved mapping is used and you can change it.

5. **Import** — Press **Import 58 lines** (the button shows your file's line count). Spine shows a summary, such as "Imported 58 lines · skipped 2 already imported · 0 rows couldn't be read". Press **Done**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="banking-import" alt="The Import statement dialog, ready for you to choose the bank's CSV file" />

## What happens next

The lines are ready to match on the reconcile page. See [Reconcile an account](../reconcile-an-account/). If the file had a balance column, Spine fills in the statement end date and closing balance for you when you start reconciling.

<Aside type="tip" title="Importing overlapping statements">
  Lines already imported for this account — the same date, amount and description — are skipped, so it's safe to import a statement that overlaps the last one.
</Aside>

## If the import has problems

- **Some rows couldn't be read.** Rows with an unreadable date or amount are listed under **Rows that couldn't be read** with the reason. The rest import.
- **The file is too big.** A file can have up to 5,000 lines (2 MB). Spine says "Split the statement into smaller files (up to 5,000 lines)."
- **It isn't a CSV.** Spine says "Export the statement from your bank as CSV, then import it here."
