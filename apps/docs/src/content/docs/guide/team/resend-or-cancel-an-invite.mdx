Invites wait on the **Invites** tab until the person accepts. From there you can send a lost email again, hand someone the link yourself, or cancel an invite sent by mistake.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the Invites tab** — Go to **Team › Users** and choose **Invites**. You can also press the **Pending invites** card, which counts how many have expired.

2. **Find the invite** — Each row shows the email, the role, the **Teams on joining**, who invited them and when ("Invited by *name* · 3 Oct"), and "Expires in 5 days", "Expires today" or "Expired". Search by email if the list is long.

3. **Open its menu** — Press the row's actions menu and choose one of:
   - **Copy invite link** — copies the link to paste into a message. The toast reminds you: only that email address can use it, signed in with that address.
   - **Resend** — sends the email again. It's the same link, and its time limit starts over.
   - **Cancel invite** — Spine asks first: "Cancel this invitation?" Press **Cancel invitation** to stop the link working, or **Keep invitation**.

</Steps>

## Expired invites

An invite that runs out shows "Expired". Choose **Resend** to send it again with a fresh time limit. The person can't use an expired, cancelled or already accepted invite; they see "This invitation can't be used" and are asked to get a new one.

<Aside type="tip">After you cancel an invite, you can invite the same email again at any time from **Invite people**.</Aside>

## If it doesn't work

If resending or cancelling fails, a message above the list says so — for example "Couldn't resend the invite to *email*." Try again, and check that email is set up in [Settings](../../settings/) if invites aren't arriving.
