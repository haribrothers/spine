A Team's permissions decide what its members can open and do. You change them as a draft: nothing is saved until you review the changes and confirm, so a mis-click never changes anyone's access.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. Managing users, Teams, settings and tax is never a Team permission — it's always Owner-only.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the Team's permissions** — Go to **Team › Teams**, open the Team and choose the **Permissions** tab.

2. **Open or close sections** — The **Sections** card at the top lists every section of Spine with a switch. Off closes the section: it leaves members' sidebar and its pages say they don't have access.

3. **Switch permissions** — Below, permissions are grouped (such as **Manage Quotes** or **Reports & Ledger**). Each group shows how many are on, for example "4 of 6", with a switch for the whole group. Expand a group to see each permission and its description. Use the search, **Expand all** and **Show changed only** to find your way.

4. **Check what members will open** — The **What members will open** panel shows each section as **Can work here**, **View only** or **Can't open**, and marks what your changes add or take away.

5. **Save** — Each switch you change is marked **Changed**, and a bar at the bottom counts them, with **Discard** and **Save changes**. Press **Save changes**. "Save 5 changes to *Team*?" lists what turns on and off — including permissions that turn on or off with them — and who gets this at their next request. Press **Save changes** again to confirm, or **Go back**. The toast says how many permissions changed.

</Steps>

## How permissions work together

- **Open comes first.** Each group starts with an Open permission, such as **Open Quotes**. Turning on an action, like **Create/edit own quotes**, turns its Open permission on too. Turning Open off turns its actions off. Spine tells you when this happens.
- **Expenses** has **See everyone's expenses** instead. With it off, members see and record only their own expenses.
- **Own and others'.** Many actions come in pairs, such as **Create/edit own quotes** and **Edit others' quotes**.
- **Closed doesn't block work.** With **Open Contacts** off, members can still pick a customer on an invoice; the name shows as plain text, not a link, and closed sections are left out of search.
- **Reports, Receivables, Payables and Banking** open with their own permissions in the list, not with a section switch.

<Aside type="caution">Leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first, for example "Discard 3 unsaved changes?" Press **Keep editing** to go back and save them, or **Discard changes** to leave.</Aside>

The [AI assistant](../../ai-assistant/) adds its own permission, **Use AI features**, when an Owner turns it on.
