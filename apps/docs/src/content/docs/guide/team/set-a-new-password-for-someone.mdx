When a Staff or Field member can't sign in and can't use the reset email — say, a crew member on site — you can set a new password for them. They're signed out everywhere, emailed that an Owner changed it, and choose their own the next time they sign in.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners, for active Staff and Field members. No one can set an Owner's password this way — not yours, another Owner's or the Primary Owner's.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open their menu** — Go to **Team › Users**, open the person's row menu and choose **Change password**.

2. **Use the suggested password, or type one** — "Change *name*'s password" suggests a strong password, shown so you can pass it on. Press **Generate another** for a different one, or type your own; the strength meter and checklist show whether it's good enough. Use **Copy password** to copy it.

3. **Read what happens** — The dialog lists it: they're signed out on every device, web and phone; Spine emails them that an owner changed their password, never the password itself; and you give them the new password yourself, in person or by phone.

4. **Change it** — Press **Change password**. The toast says their password changed.

</Steps>

## What they see next

When they sign in with the password you gave them — on the web or in the field app — Spine first asks them to **Choose your own password**. It must be at least 8 characters and not the one they were given. Until they press **Save and continue**, they can't do anything else in Spine.

<Aside type="caution">Their sessions end at once, so anything they hadn't saved on another device is lost. Tell them before you change it if you can.</Aside>

## Recorded in the audit log

The audit log records that the password was set and by whom — never the password. See [Use the audit log](../use-the-audit-log/).

<Aside type="tip">Owners change their own password in **Settings › Your account › Password & security**.</Aside>
