The dashboard is the first page you see when you sign in. It opens with what's waiting on you, then today's schedule, your key figures and a set of cards about money, sales and jobs. It adapts to who's looking: each person sees only what their role and Teams allow, so nobody gets locked or empty panels.

<Aside type="note" title="Who sees what">Owners see every card. Staff see the cards their Team's rights allow, such as **View dashboard summary** or **View receivables**, and cards that need no right. Field crew see their own jobs, expenses and what needs them, with no money figures. The dashboard never shows more than the full report behind a card would.</Aside>

## The main ideas

- **Needs your attention.** One row for each kind of thing waiting on you, such as overdue invoices or expenses to approve, each with the button that deals with it. Rows clear themselves once the work is done.
- **Today.** Your meetings, milestones coming due and follow-ups, in time order.
- **Key figures.** Cash position, Profit this month, Receivables and Overdue, each with an 8-week line and a comparison with last month. They stay at the top.
- **Cards.** Money in and out, Receivables aging, Payables, Accounts, Ready to bill, Tax this period, Top expenses, job cards and Recent activity. Each one links to its full report.
- **Accrual or cash.** Profit and the money chart can be shown on either basis.
- **Your layout.** You choose the order of the cards and which are hidden. Your layout follows you to every device.

While the dashboard loads, you see grey outlines of its cards in the places they'll fill, so nothing jumps as the figures arrive.
