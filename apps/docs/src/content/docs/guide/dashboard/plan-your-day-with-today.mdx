The **Today** card puts your day in one column beside **Needs your attention**: your meetings, milestones coming due and the follow-ups assigned to you, in time order. You can plan the day without opening the calendar or each job.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone sees their own Today card. Only Owners choose whether it shows each person's own meetings or every meeting in the business.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Read the list** — In time order: your meetings today, milestones due within 7 days on jobs you can see, follow-ups assigned to you that are due today or overdue, and your next meeting after today.
2. **Read each entry** — A time or "Due *day*", the title, and a line naming the kind and record, such as "Meeting · office" or "Milestone · Marina office fit-out".
3. **Open the calendar** — Press **Open calendar** to see the meetings calendar on today.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="dashboard-today" alt="The Today card listing a meeting, a milestone and a follow-up in time order, with Open calendar" />

With nothing on, it reads "Nothing scheduled today."

## Choose which meetings Today shows

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Upcoming meetings** — Open your account menu, choose **Settings**, then **Upcoming meetings**.
2. **Choose a scope** — **Only my meetings**: each person's dashboard shows only the meetings they organise. **Every org meeting**: everyone's dashboard shows every meeting in the business, which suits a small team that plans as one calendar.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="dashboard-meetings-scope" alt="Settings, Upcoming meetings, with the choices Only my meetings and Every org meeting" />

<Aside type="note">Field users always see only their own meetings, whatever this setting says.</Aside>

On a wide screen (1440 pixels or more), **Needs your attention**, **Today** and **Recent activity** sit in a column on the right that stays in view while the rest of the page scrolls. On a smaller screen they sit at the top.

For booking and managing meetings, see [Meetings](../../meetings/).
