A sent quote is never edited, so there's always a record of exactly what the customer was offered. To change the price, the scope or the date, you revise it. Spine makes a new draft version with the same lines, and the customer can only answer the newest version.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Revise a quote**. Sending the new version needs **Send a quote**.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the quote** — Go to **Quotes** and open the sent, accepted, declined or expired quote.

2. **Press Revise** — It's the main button on a declined or expired quote, beside it on a sent quote, and under **More** on an accepted one. You can also choose **Revise** from the row's **…** menu on the Quotes list.

3. **Confirm** — Spine asks **Revise this quote?** and explains that the new version is made as a draft. Press **Revise quote**.

4. **Make your changes** — The new version opens in the builder, with the same contact, title, lines, notes and terms. A toast says the quote was revised. Change what you need, including **Valid until**, and save it.

5. **Send the new version** — Open it and press **Send quote**, as for any draft.

</Steps>

## What happens to the earlier version

- It's kept as it was, and its page says **Superseded by** with a link to the new version.
- Its customer links stop working as soon as you revise. A customer who opens one sees **This quote link has expired**, with your contact details, until you send them the new version.
- The **Related** panel on each version lists every version (v1, v2 and so on) with its status chip, and "this one" beside the version you're looking at.
- The new version's **Activity** starts with **Revised from** a link to the earlier one.

<Aside type="caution">
Each version can be revised once. To change the quote again, revise its newest version. A draft can't be revised; edit it instead.
</Aside>

## Revising an expired quote

An expired quote's main button is **Revise**. Give the new version a fresh **Valid until** date and send it, and the customer gets a quote they can accept at today's prices.

If revising fails, you see "Could not revise this quote." Try again, or check whether someone has already revised it.
