When a new job is like one you've quoted before, duplicate that quote. You get a new draft with the same contact, title, lines, notes and terms, ready to change. It's a separate quote, not a new version of the old one.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own quotes**.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the quote to copy** — Go to **Quotes** and open any quote, in any status.

2. **Choose Duplicate as new draft** — Press **More** and choose **Duplicate as new draft**. On the Quotes list, choose **Duplicate** from the row's **…** menu.

3. **Change what you need** — The new draft opens. A toast says **Quote duplicated**. Change the contact, lines or anything else, and save.

4. **Send it** — When it's ready, press **Send quote**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="quotes-duplicate" alt="A quote's More menu open with Duplicate as new draft at the top" />

## What the copy gets

- The same contact, title, lines, notes and terms.
- No number until you send it, and today's date.
- A fresh **Valid until** date, from your usual validity.
- Prices and tax from the original quote's lines, not today's catalogue prices. If your prices have changed, update the lines.
- Its **Activity** starts with **Duplicated from** the original quote's number.

<Aside type="tip">
To reuse just some lines on future quotes, [save them as a bundle](../save-and-reuse-lines-as-a-bundle/) instead.
</Aside>

Duplicating is different from [revising](../revise-a-sent-quote/): a revision replaces the earlier version for the same customer, while a duplicate leaves the original as it is.
