You can delete a quote while it's still a draft and the customer has never seen it. Once a quote is sent it can't be deleted, so the record of what you offered is always kept.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Delete own quotes** (for drafts they created) or **Delete others' quotes** (for colleagues' drafts).</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the draft** — Go to **Quotes** and open the draft.

2. **Choose Delete draft** — Press **More** and choose **Delete draft**. On the Quotes list, you can choose **Delete** from the row's **…** menu instead.

3. **Confirm** — Spine asks **Delete this draft quote?** and says this permanently removes the draft. Press **Delete draft**.

4. **Check** — A toast says **Quote deleted**, and you're back on the Quotes list.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="quotes-delete" alt="A draft quote's More menu with Delete draft at the bottom" />

<Aside type="danger">
Deleting a draft can't be undone. Its lines, notes and terms are gone. If you might want them later, [duplicate the quote](../duplicate-a-quote/) or [save its lines as a bundle](../save-and-reuse-lines-as-a-bundle/) first.
</Aside>

## When there's no Delete

- **Sent, accepted, declined and expired quotes** have no delete. To replace a sent quote, [revise it](../revise-a-sent-quote/). An unanswered quote expires on its **Valid until** date.
- **You don't see Delete** on a draft when your Team doesn't give you the right to delete it, for example a colleague's draft when you only have **Delete own quotes**.
