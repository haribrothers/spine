A quote tells a customer what you'll do and what it costs. In Spine you build it line by line, send it by email with a secure link, and the customer reads it, accepts it or declines it on any device, without an account. When they accept, you turn it into a job or an invoice with one button, so nothing is typed twice.

Open **Quotes** in the sidebar, under **Sales**.

<Screenshot id="quotes-list" alt="The Quotes list with the Open, Expiring in 7 days, Won this month and Win rate cards above the table" />

## The main ideas

- **Draft.** A new quote is a draft. It has no number yet, and you can change it or delete it.
- **Sent.** Sending gives the quote its permanent number and emails it to the customer. A sent quote can't be edited or deleted, so what the customer saw is always kept.
- **Accepted or declined.** The customer answers from the link in the email, or on site on a field crew member's phone. Their comment, or their signature, is kept with the quote. The status chip reads **Accepted** or **Rejected** (the list's **Status** filter calls a rejected quote **Declined**).
- **Expired.** After its **Valid until** date, a sent quote shows **Expired**. Nothing changes in the quote itself; it's a signal to follow up or revise.
- **Versions.** To change a quote you've sent, you **Revise** it. That makes a new draft version with the same lines. The earlier version is kept, its page says **Superseded by** with a link to the newer one, and the customer can only answer the newest version.
- **Lines.** Each item line has a quantity, a rate, an optional discount % and a tax rate. Headings and text lines break a long quote into sections and add nothing to the totals.

## Who can work on quotes

Owners can do everything. Staff get rights from their Teams: **Open Quotes**, **Create/edit own quotes**, **Edit others' quotes**, **Delete own quotes**, **Delete others' quotes**, **Send a quote**, **Revise a quote** and **Accept a quote in person**. Sending and revising are separate from drafting, so a junior can draft quotes for someone else to send. Only Owners approve large discounts. Field users don't see Quotes on the web; they can present a quote on site in the field app. See [Choose who can draft, send and revise quotes](./choose-who-can-work-on-quotes/).

<Aside type="note">
Quotes are always on. Discount approval belongs to the **Approvals** module, and converting to a job needs the **Jobs & field** module (Settings › Modules).
</Aside>
