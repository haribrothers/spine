If you quote the same package of work again and again, save its lines as a bundle. Next time, pick the bundle and all its lines go in at once, in order, with their prices and tax.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Saving a bundle: Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own catalogue**. Adding a bundle to a quote: anyone who can build the quote.</Aside>

## Save a bundle from a quote

<Steps>

1. **Open the quote** — Go to **Quotes** and open a quote that has the lines you want. It can be a draft or a sent quote.

2. **Choose Save lines as a bundle…** — Press **More** and choose **Save lines as a bundle…**. You see this only when the quote has at least one item line.

3. **Name it** — Enter a **Bundle name**, such as "Bathroom refit — standard", and an optional **Description**. The dialog shows a summary of the lines it will save.

4. **Save** — Press **Save bundle**. A toast says **Bundle saved**, and the bundle is in the catalogue under **Bundles**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="quotes-more-menu" alt="A quote's More menu with Duplicate as new draft, Save lines as a bundle… and View history" />

If you press **Save bundle** without a name, you're asked to give the bundle a name.

## Add a bundle while building a quote

<Steps>

1. **Type in a line's Item field** — In the quote builder, start typing the bundle's name.

2. **Pick from Bundles** — Matching bundles are listed first under **Bundles**, each with its total and a summary such as "Adds 3 lines: …". Items follow under **Items**.

3. **Check the lines** — The bundle's lines go in at that position, in order. Each is an ordinary line you can change or remove.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- A bundle is a copy. Changing the quote you saved it from later doesn't change the bundle, and changing a bundle doesn't change quotes that already used it.
- Prices and tax come from the bundle, not today's catalogue prices.
- If an item in a bundle has since been archived, its line is still added, as a one-off line.
- Bundles appear in the item picker on quotes and invoices. Vendor bills show items only.
- To edit, archive or delete a bundle, go to the catalogue's **Bundles** tab. See [Catalogue](../../catalogue/).
