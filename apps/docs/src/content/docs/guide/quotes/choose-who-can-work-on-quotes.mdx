Quote rights come from Teams. You can let a junior draft quotes without letting them send anything on your behalf, or let them work on their own quotes without touching a colleague's.

import { Aside, Steps } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners only. Managing Teams is always Owner-only.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the Team** — Go to **Team** › **Teams** and open the Team whose rights you want to change.

2. **Find the quote rights** — They're grouped with Quotes. Type "quote" in **Search permissions** to see only them.

3. **Turn rights on or off** — Choose the rights from the list below. Turning on an action turns on **Open Quotes** with it.

4. **Save** — Press **Save changes** and confirm. Members get the new rights at their next request. A member has every right granted by any of their Teams.

</Steps>

## The quote rights

| Right | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Open Quotes** | See Quotes in the sidebar and read every quote. |
| **Create/edit own quotes** | Start new quotes, duplicate them, and edit drafts you created. |
| **Edit others' quotes** | Edit a draft a colleague created. |
| **Delete own quotes** | Delete a draft you created. |
| **Delete others' quotes** | Delete a colleague's draft. |
| **Send a quote** | Send a draft, email it again and copy the customer link. Sending can't be undone, so it's separate from editing. |
| **Revise a quote** | Make a new version of a sent quote. |
| **Approve quote above discount threshold** | Listed with the quote rights; on the quote's page, only Owners see **Approve discount**. |
| **Accept a quote in person** | Record a customer's on-site acceptance and signature. |

The seeded **General Staff** Team has every quote right except approving discounts.

## What people see

- Without **Create/edit own quotes** or **Edit others' quotes**, **New quote** is hidden.
- With **Create/edit own quotes** only, **Edit** is hidden on a colleague's draft.
- Without **Send a quote**, **Send quote** and **Save and send** are hidden, even on your own draft. With it, you can send a colleague's draft you can't edit.
- Without **Revise a quote**, **Revise** is hidden.

<Aside type="tip">
Spine checks these rights on the server too, so hiding a button is never the only protection.
</Aside>

Converting a quote needs the invoice or job rights: **Create/edit own invoices** or **Create/edit own jobs**. Field users never see Quotes on the web, whatever their Teams. See [Team](../../team/).
