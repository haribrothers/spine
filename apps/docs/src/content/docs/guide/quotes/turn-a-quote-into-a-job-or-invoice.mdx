Once a customer accepts a quote, you can turn it into a draft invoice with the same lines, or into a job with the quote total as its contract value. You can do both: for example, a job to track the work and an invoice for a deposit.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">To an invoice: Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own invoices**. To a job: Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own jobs**, with the **Jobs & field** module switched on (Settings › Modules).</Aside>

## Convert to an invoice

<Steps>

1. **Open the accepted quote** — Go to **Quotes** and open it, or use the row's **…** menu.

2. **Press Convert to invoice** — It's the main button on an accepted quote. Spine asks **Convert to an invoice?** and says a draft invoice is made from the quote's lines, and that nothing is issued or posted until you issue it.

3. **Confirm** — Press **Convert to invoice**. The draft invoice opens, for the same contact with the same lines, linked back to the quote.

4. **Check and issue it** — Change anything you need, then issue it from the invoice. See [Invoices](../../invoices/).

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="quotes-detail-accepted" alt="An accepted quote's page with Convert to invoice as the main button and Convert to job beside it" />

## Convert to a job

<Steps>

1. **Press Convert to job** — On the accepted quote, press **Convert to job**. Spine asks **Convert to a job?** and shows the contract value.

2. **Confirm** — Press **Convert to job**. The new job opens for the same contact, with the quote total as its contract value and a link to the quote. It's named after the quote's title, or its first item and the customer.

3. **Set up the job** — Add its milestones next. See [Jobs](../../jobs/).

</Steps>

## Converting the same quote twice

Each conversion makes a full, separate job or invoice for the quote's whole amount; the amount isn't split. By default Spine warns you: **This quote has already been converted before** lists the existing jobs or invoices, each as a link, with **Cancel** and **Convert to job anyway** (or **Convert to invoice anyway**).

<Aside type="caution">
Only convert again if you mean to bill or track this quote's work more than once.
</Aside>

An Owner chooses how this works in **Settings › Repeat quote conversion**:

- **Warn (recommended)** shows what already exists and asks before creating another.
- **Block** refuses a second job or invoice from the same quote.
- **Off** converts with no check at all.

<Screenshot id="quotes-settings-conversion" alt="Settings, Repeat quote conversion with the Warn, Block and Off choices" />

The quote's **Related** panel lists every job and invoice made from it.
