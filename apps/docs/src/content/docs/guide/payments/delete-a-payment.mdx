If you recorded a payment that never happened — the wrong customer, or the same money twice — you can delete it, as long as none of it is applied to an invoice or refunded. Spine posts a reversing entry, so your books go back to how they were.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Delete own payments** (for payments they recorded) or **Delete others' payments** (for payments a colleague recorded). Without the right one, **Delete payment** is hidden.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the payment** — Go to **Payments** and open it. If it was applied to invoices, first [move it off each invoice](../move-a-payment-off-the-wrong-invoice/) so nothing is applied.

2. **Choose Delete payment** — Open **More** next to the main button and choose **Delete payment**. It's also in the payment's row menu on the list, where it opens the payment's page with the confirmation.

3. **Confirm** — "Delete this payment?" says Spine posts a reversing journal entry and permanently removes the payment. Press **Delete payment** to go ahead.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="payments-delete" alt="A payment's page with the More menu open, where Delete payment appears while none of the payment is applied" />

## What happens next

The toast reads **Payment deleted**. The payment's journal entry is reversed and the payment no longer appears in the list. Owners aren't sent another "payment received" notice.

<Aside type="danger" title="This can't be undone">
  A deleted payment is gone. If the customer did pay, record the payment again.
</Aside>

## When you can't delete a payment

- **Delete payment** isn't offered while any of the payment is applied to an invoice or has been refunded. A customer payment that's been applied stays in your records; correct it by moving it off the invoice instead.
- A payment that's part of a finished bank reconciliation can't be deleted until that reconciliation is undone. See [Banking](../../banking/).
