When a customer holds credit you won't use — an overpayment, an unused deposit, or the extra on a credit note — you can pay it back. Recording the refund lowers their credit and takes the money out of the account you paid it from, so your bank balance stays right.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">
  Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Refund customers**. This right is off for General Staff unless an Owner turns it on, because money leaves the business. Without it, **Refund** is hidden.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start the refund** — Press **Refund** on a payment with credit left, on a credit note with credit remaining, or on the customer's contact page. The **Refund** dialog opens with the customer's name.

2. **Choose the credit** — Under **Refund from**, pick the payment or credit note the money comes from. It shows how much credit it holds.

3. **Fill in the details** — Enter the **Amount** (up to that credit), the **Date**, **Paid from** (the account the money left), the **Method** and an optional **Reference**.

4. **Tell the customer (optional)** — Tick **Send refund confirmation** to email them that you've refunded the amount.

5. **Record it** — Press **Record refund**. The toast reads, for example, **Refunded $250.00 to Maple Ridge Homes**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="payments-refund" alt="The Refund dialog with Refund from, Amount, Date, Paid from and Method" />

## What happens next

- The customer's credit drops by the amount refunded. A payment whose credit is all paid back reads **Refunded**.
- The money shows leaving the **Paid from** account in its register.
- The refund appears under **Refunds** on the payment's page and in its **Activity**.
- The customer's statement shows the refund as a line of its own.

## Delete a refund

If you recorded a refund by mistake, open the payment and press **Delete refund** next to it under **Refunds**. Spine asks first: "Delete this refund?" Confirm, and the refund is reversed in your books and the amount goes back into the customer's credit. The refund stays in the list, marked **Deleted**.

<Aside type="caution">
  A refund that's part of a finished bank reconciliation can't be deleted. Undo that reconciliation first. See [Banking](../../banking/).
</Aside>

If the dialog says the customer has no credit to refund, there's nothing to pay back yet.
