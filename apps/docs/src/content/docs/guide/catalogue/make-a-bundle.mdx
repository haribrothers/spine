If you often quote the same set of items together, such as the labour, materials and disposal for a bathroom refit, save them as a bundle. On a quote or invoice, picking the bundle adds all its lines at once.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Create/edit own catalogue**. Editing, archiving or deleting a bundle someone else made also needs **Edit others' catalogue** or **Archive/delete others' catalogue**.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Tick the items** — Go to **Catalogue** and tick the items that belong in the bundle.
2. **Start the bundle** — In the bar, press **Make a bundle**.
3. **Name it** — The **Make a bundle** dialog opens. Fill in **Name**, such as "Bathroom refit", and, if you like, **Description (optional)** to say what's included for your team.
4. **Set the quantities** — Each item starts at a quantity of 1. Change the **Quantity** of any item. The dialog shows the **Total before tax**.
5. **Save** — Press **Save bundle**. A "Bundle saved" toast shows, and the bundle is listed on the **Bundles** tab.
</Steps>

Prices and tax come from each item's defaults. You can change them on the document after you add the bundle.

## If you can't save it

- All the items must be in one currency. Otherwise you see "Choose items in one currency." and nothing is saved.
- Each item needs a quantity above 0.
- A bundle holds a limited number of items; Spine tells you if you've ticked too many.

## The Bundles tab

Open the **Bundles** tab to see each bundle with its lines and total. Search with **Search bundles**, or filter by status. From a bundle's menu:

- **Edit** changes its **Bundle name** and **Description**. Its lines stay as they were saved.
- **Archive** hides it from the item picker; **Restore** brings it back.
- **Delete** removes it for good, after Spine asks "Delete "…"?". Quotes and invoices that used it keep their lines.

<Screenshot id="catalogue-bundles" alt="The Bundles tab of the Catalogue listing each bundle with its lines, total and status" />

With no bundles yet, the tab says "No bundles yet" with **Choose items**, which takes you to the **Items** tab.

<Aside type="tip">You can also save a quote's lines as a bundle from the quote's **More** menu. See [Quotes](../../quotes/).</Aside>
