A catalogue grows quickly. Search and filters find an item in seconds, and the cards at the top show which items still need a tax rate or an income account, so your documents and books come out right.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Open Catalogue**. Everyone who can open the catalogue sees every item, whoever added it.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. **Open the catalogue** — Go to **Catalogue**. The **Items** tab is open, with its count beside it.
2. **Read the cards** — **Total items** and **Categories** count your active items. **Missing tax rate** counts taxable items with no default tax. **No income account** counts items whose sales post to 4100 Sales — Services.
3. **Search** — Type in **Search items**.
4. **Filter** — Choose a **Status** (**Active** or **Archived**) or a **Category**. Press **Price** to set a **Min** and **Max** default price and press **Apply**; the button then shows the range.
5. **Choose columns** — Press **Columns** to show or hide columns in the table.
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="catalogue-missing-tax" alt="The Catalogue filtered by the pressed Missing tax rate card, showing only items with no default tax" />

## Fix missing setup

<Steps>
1. **Press a card** — Press **Missing tax rate** or **No income account**. The table shows just those items, and the card says "Showing these · press again to clear".
2. **Fix the items** — Edit them one by one, or tick them all and use **Set tax** or **Set income account**. See [Change many items at once](../change-many-items-at-once/).
3. **Clear it** — Press the card again. **Total items** clears every filter.
</Steps>

Exempt and non-taxable items never count as missing a tax rate. For a GST business, zero-rated items don't count either.

## Reading the table

- The **Tax** column names the default tax, such as "GST 18%". A zero-rated, exempt or non-taxable item says so. A taxable item with no tax says **No tax** with a warning icon.
- An item's description shows as a muted line under its name. Hover over it to read it in full.
- Archived items, shown when you filter by **Archived**, carry an **Archived** badge.
- For a GST business, an **HSN/SAC** column shows each item's code, or "—" when it has none.

## Come back to the same list

The address in your browser keeps your search, filters, setup card, sort and page (for example `/catalog?category=Labour&setup=tax`), so Back and bookmarks bring you to the same list. **Clear filters** clears them. Press **Refresh list** to see colleagues' changes; the list also refreshes when you come back to the tab after 30 seconds or more away.

When nothing matches, you see "No items match these filters" with **Clear filters**.
