The catalogue holds the goods and services you quote, invoice and buy: labour by the hour, materials by the each or the square foot, a fixed-price call-out. Each item has a unit, a default price and tax, and the accounts it posts to. Pick an item on a quote or invoice line and its details fill in for you.

## The main ideas

- **Item.** A good or service with a name, description, unit, category, default price and currency.
- **Default tax.** The tax rate or group a new line for this item starts with. You can change it on each line.
- **Supply type.** Whether the item is **Taxable**, **Zero-rated**, **Exempt** or **Non-taxable (outside GST/VAT)**. Only taxable items need a tax rate.
- **Income and expense accounts.** Where sales of the item post in your books, and where purchases of it post. An item with no income account posts its sales to 4100 Sales — Services.
- **A copy on every document.** A quote or invoice copies the item's details onto the line. Changing or deleting the item later never changes documents you've already made.
- **Bundle.** A group of items you sell together, added to a quote or invoice in one pick.
- **Archive or delete.** Archiving hides an item from pickers but keeps it. Deleting removes it for good; documents keep their copy.
