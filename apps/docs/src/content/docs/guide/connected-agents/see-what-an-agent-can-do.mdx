A connected agent works as you, and can only read and draft. Ask in your own words; the agent chooses which of Spine's tools to use.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Anyone with a connected agent. What it can do depends on your role, your Teams' permissions and the modules that are on. Report tools need the matching report permission, such as **View receivables**.</Aside>

## Find and read records

- **Search** — like Spine's own search: contacts by name, phone, tax ID or person; documents by number or amount; leads, jobs and catalogue items.
- **Lists** — of any kind of record you can open, filtered by status, contact, job, dates or overdue. Newest first, 25 at a time and at most 100.
- **One record** — by its number or link, as its page shows it: lines, tax, totals, balance, status, dates and its Spine link.

Anything you can't open in Spine, the agent can't either. If your Teams can't open invoices, it's told "You don't have permission to open invoices."

## Ask about money and the business

With the matching permission, the agent can read **receivables** (who owes you, by age), **payables**, **profit and loss** and the **balance sheet** for a period, **job profitability**, **bank balances** and the **dashboard summary**. The figures are exactly the report's in Spine, for the same period and basis.

## Start work as drafts

If you allowed drafts when you connected, and the Owner allows them, the agent can:

- **Create a lead**, with the enquiry as its first note, adding the contact if it's new.
- **Draft a quote** — catalogue items take their price and tax unless a price is given, and Spine works out the totals. The agent gives you the link to open it, check it and send it.
- **Draft a vendor bill** — never recorded, so nothing reaches the books.
- **Add a note** to a contact, lead or job you can change. Field users can add notes to their assigned jobs.

Each draft is yours, and its Activity names the agent.

<Aside type="caution">Agents can't issue, send, record, approve, void, match, delete or change an existing record. There's no tool for it. You finish every draft in Spine, with that screen's own confirm.</Aside>

## Ready-made tasks

Agents that show ready-made prompts offer **Chase overdue invoices** (it suggests what to send; you send it from Spine), **Summarise a job** and **This week in the business**, where your permissions allow them.

## Field users

A Field user's agent reads only contacts, the catalogue, their assigned jobs without money, and their own expenses. It never gets a money figure or a report.

## Limits

One agent can create up to 30 drafts an hour ("Too many drafts from this agent. Try again later.") and make up to 120 requests a minute. A repeated request never creates a second draft.
