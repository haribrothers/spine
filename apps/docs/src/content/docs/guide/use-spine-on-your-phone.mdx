Open your Spine address in your phone's browser and sign in as you would on a computer. On a screen narrower than a tablet, Spine lays itself out like a phone app. Your data, roles and settings are the same as on the computer. Field members doing site work can also use the [field app](../field-app/).

## Tabs along the bottom

| Tab | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The dashboard |
| **Sales** | Quotes, contacts, the pipeline, meetings and the catalogue |
| **Jobs** | Jobs |
| **Money** | Invoices, payments, receivables, credit notes, bills, expenses, payables, banking and reports |
| **More** | The whole menu, your account, Settings, Help and Sign out |

- **Sales** and **Money** open the page you last used there. The area's other pages are in a row at the top: swipe it sideways to reach them all.
- A tab you have no pages in isn't shown. A Field member has no **Money** tab; their own expenses are under **More**.
- **More** opens the whole menu in a sheet. Tap a page to go there, or close the sheet with ✕ or by swiping it down.

## The bar at the top

The bar at the top shows the page's name, search (🔍) and your notifications. On a record, such as an invoice or a job, it also has a back arrow (‹). The arrow takes you back to where you came from, with the list scrolled and filtered as you left it. If you opened the record from a link, it takes you to its list.

## Lists

At the top of a list its totals are chips in a row: swipe it sideways, and tap a chip to show just those records. Under them are the search and **Filters**, which opens every filter and the sort; each filter that's on shows as a chip, and × removes it. The list's main action, such as **New invoice**, is the round button above the tabs; it shrinks to + while you scroll. Its other actions, such as **Recurring invoices** or **Refresh**, are under ⋯ at the top.

Each list shows one row per record: its number or name and status, who it's for, and the amount on the right. Tap anywhere on a row to open it, or tap its ⋯ for its actions. After 25 rows, **Load more** shows the next 25.

To act on several at once, such as sending reminders for overdue invoices, tap **Select** at the top, tick the rows and use the bar along the bottom. **Done** ends it.

The pipeline shows one stage at a time: tap a stage's chip to see its leads, and use a lead's ⋯ to move it.

## Building a document

New quotes, invoices and bills fill the screen without the tabs. The tabs also step aside while you're typing in a field, so the keyboard has room.

## From a tablet up

At tablet width and wider, Spine shows the sidebar on the left, as on a computer.
