Open your Spine address in your phone's browser and sign in as you would on a computer. On a screen narrower than a tablet, Spine lays itself out like a phone app. Your data, roles and settings are the same as on the computer. Field members doing site work can also use the [field app](../field-app/).

## Tabs along the bottom

| Tab | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The dashboard |
| **Sales** | Quotes, contacts, the pipeline, meetings and the catalogue |
| **Jobs** | Jobs |
| **Money** | Invoices, payments, receivables, credit notes, bills, expenses, payables, banking and reports |
| **More** | The whole menu, your account, Settings, Help and Sign out |

- **Sales** and **Money** open the page you last used there. The area's other pages are in a row at the top: swipe it sideways to reach them all.
- A tab you have no pages in isn't shown. A Field member has no **Money** tab; their own expenses are under **More**.
- **More** opens the whole menu in a sheet. Tap a page to go there, or close the sheet with ✕ or by swiping it down.

## The bar at the top

The bar at the top shows the page's name, search (🔍) and your notifications. On a record, such as an invoice or a job, it also has a back arrow (‹). The arrow takes you back to where you came from, with the list scrolled and filtered as you left it. If you opened the record from a link, it takes you to its list.

## The dashboard

Home starts with a greeting and the date, then **Needs your attention**: each thing waiting for you is a row with its button on the right, such as **Send reminder**, **Review** or **Approve**. When there are more than three, **Show all** shows the rest. Your key figures follow, two to a row, then Today and your other cards one under another. To switch the figures between Accrual and Cash, tap ⋯ at the top. The layout you choose with **Edit layout** on a computer applies on your phone too, in one column.

## Lists

At the top of a list its totals are chips in a row: swipe it sideways, and tap a chip to show just those records. Under them are the search and **Filters**, which opens every filter; each filter that's on shows as a chip, and × removes it. The list's main action, such as **New invoice**, is the round button above the tabs; it shrinks to + while you scroll. Its other actions, such as **Recurring invoices** or **Refresh**, are under ⋯ at the top.

Each list shows one row per record: its number or name and status, who it's for, and the amount on the right. Tap anywhere on a row to open it, or tap its ⋯ for its actions. After 25 rows, **Load more** shows the next 25.

To act on several at once, such as sending reminders for overdue invoices, tap **Select** at the top, tick the rows and use the bar along the bottom. **Done** ends it.

The pipeline shows one stage at a time: tap a stage's chip to see its leads, and use a lead's ⋯ to move it.

## A record

Opening a record, such as an invoice or a job, shows its number and status at the top, then the amount that matters (the balance due on an invoice, or its total), then who it's for. The back arrow at the top takes you back to the list.

The next step for the record, such as **Send reminder**, **Issue invoice** or **Record payment**, is the wide button at the bottom, above the tabs. Everything else, such as **Download PDF**, **Email invoice**, **Duplicate** or **Void**, is under ⋯ at the top. Voiding or deleting still asks you first.

A record's figures, such as a job's contract, billed and margin, are two to a row. Its tabs are one row: swipe it sideways to see the rest. A document's lines are cards with the item, "4 × $650.00", its tax and the amount, and the totals are under them as on the PDF. A job's milestones are rows; tap a milestone's ⋯ to complete it, invoice it or see its history.

## The calendar

Meetings opens on the month: a dot under each day that has meetings, in their calendars' colours, and the meetings of the day you've chosen listed underneath. Tap a day to see its meetings, and swipe the month sideways (or use the arrows) to go to the next or previous one; **Today** brings you back. Tap the month's name to jump to another month.

**Day** shows a strip of the week's days over the day's timeline, with a line at the current time. Swipe the strip to move a week, or the timeline to move a day. **Agenda** lists what's coming up.

Tap a meeting to see it in a sheet from the bottom, and the round **+** to book a new one on the day you've chosen. The filter button at the top opens the small calendar and the list of whose calendars are shown.

## Reports

The Reports page lists every report in its group; tap one to open it. At the top of a report, one line says what it covers, such as "Jan 1 – Nov 18, 2026 · Accrual · No comparison". Tap it to change the dates, basis or comparison in a sheet, then **Show report**. **Export** is under ⋯ at the top, and the related reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Cash Flow) are tabs you can swipe.

The totals are chips you can swipe sideways, and a chart is the width of the screen with its key under it. Profit & Loss, the Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and the Tax Summary read as a list: each section, every account with its amount, the subtotals in bold and the total last. Tap an account to see the entries behind it. If you compare with another period, its amount is under each one in grey.

Lists such as sales by customer show one row per customer or invoice. Reports that are really grids, such as the Trial Balance or GSTR-1, keep their columns: the first column stays put while you swipe the rest sideways.

## Building a document

New quotes, invoices, bills and vendor credits fill the screen without the tabs, with **Cancel** at the top left and every field one under another. If you've changed something, **Cancel** (or going back) asks before your changes are lost.

Each line is a card with the item, "4 × $650.00", its tax and the amount. Tap **Add item** to add one: the item (search the catalogue), description, quantity, unit price, discount and tax are all in one sheet, and **Done** keeps it (**Cancel** in the sheet keeps nothing). Tap a line to change it in the same sheet, where **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete** are too.

The bar along the bottom shows the total; tap it to see the subtotal and tax. Next to it is the main button, such as **Save and send** or **Save and issue**, and ⋯ has **Save as draft** and **Preview PDF**. While you're typing, the bar and the tabs step aside so the keyboard and the field you're in have room.

A credit note, and a recurring invoice or bill, open full screen too, with each line as a card.

## From a tablet up

At tablet width and wider, Spine shows the sidebar on the left, as on a computer.
