Spine keeps your books for you. Every invoice, payment, bill and expense posts to the right accounts automatically — "you never write journal entries by hand". The chart of accounts is the list of those accounts. Opening balances are what each account held on the day you started using Spine, so your Balance Sheet is right from the first day.

## The main ideas

- **Accounts are grouped** under Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and Expenses, each with a code — for example 1100 Accounts Receivable or 6000 Rent.
- **System accounts** are the ones Spine posts to itself, such as Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, tax accounts, Retention Receivable and Opening Balance Adjustments. They're marked **System** and can't be renamed.
- **Your own accounts** can be renamed, and you can add more — say a "Software Subscriptions" expense account.
- **Closing a period** stops anything new being posted with a date inside it, so filed figures stay filed.
- **The cutover date** is the day you start using Spine. Opening balances are your balances on the day before it, taken from your old software's trial balance. Unpaid invoices and bills come in one by one, so you can chase and pay them in Spine.
- **Opening Balance Adjustments** (3900) holds any difference while you're entering figures. When it's zero, your opening balances match your old books.
