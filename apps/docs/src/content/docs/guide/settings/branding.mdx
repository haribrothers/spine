Branding sets two things: the accent colour everyone sees in Spine, and the colour and footer printed on the documents and emails your customers get. Your logo isn't here — it's in **Business profile**.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Branding** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Branding** under **Business**.

2. **Pick an app theme** — Under **App theme**, choose **Spine Blue** (marked **Default**), **Harbour Teal**, **Plum** or **Graphite**. The theme changes buttons, links, the active item in the sidebar and focus rings, in light and dark mode.

3. **Set the document colour** — Under **Document colour**, pick a colour or type it as `#` and six hex digits, for example `#2954E5`. A check underneath tells you whether it's readable, such as "✓ Readable on white (6.0:1, passes AA)".

4. **Write a document footer** — **Document footer** is printed on every quote, invoice, credit note and receipt, and in emails. Up to 300 characters.

5. **Check the preview and press Save** — The **Preview** shows a sample invoice with your colour and footer. A "Saved" message appears when it's done.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Each person keeps their own light or dark mode.** "Each person still chooses light or dark mode from their user menu." The theme sits on top of that choice.
- **Status colours never change.** Paid, due soon, overdue and draft keep their usual colours with every theme, so they always mean the same thing.
- **The Spine logo stays blue.** Themes change the app's accent colour only.
- **Too light a colour can't be saved.** Documents are printed on white, so a colour under 4.5:1 shows "Too light to read on white … Pick a darker colour — documents need at least 4.5:1."
- **Issued documents keep theirs.** "Issued documents keep the colour and footer they were issued with."

<Aside type="tip">**Reset to Spine defaults** brings back Spine Blue and the default document colour. Press **Save** to keep it.</Aside>
