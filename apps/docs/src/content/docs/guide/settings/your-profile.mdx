Your profile is "How you appear to your team and on what you send. Only you can change these." Everyone has it, whatever their role.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone — Owners, Staff and Field users — for their own account. Settings opens on **Profile** for Staff and Field users.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Profile** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Profile** under **Your account**.

2. **Change your photo** — Press **Upload photo** (or **Change photo**) and choose a PNG or JPEG up to 10 MB. Crop it, then confirm. "Photo updated." Your photo shows in the sidebar, on records you change and on the team list.

3. **Change your name** — Under **Name**, type your full name as your team knows you, and press **Save**. "Profile updated. Your team sees your new name."

4. **Change your sign-in email** — Press **Change email**. In **Change your sign-in email**, enter the **New email** and your **Current password**, then press **Change email**.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Removing your photo** asks "Remove your photo?" first. Your initials show instead, everywhere your photo was.
- **Your sign-in email** is "Where Spine sends your sign-in links, invites and digests." After you change it, you sign in with the new address, and Spine tells your old address it changed.
- **On the field app,** the phone has its own Profile screen; this pane is the web app's.

<Aside type="tip">Light or dark mode is in your user menu, not in Settings.</Aside>
