Payment instructions are "Printed under the totals on every invoice and in invoice emails, so customers know how to pay." Enter them once and every invoice you issue carries them. In India you can add your UPI ID and a QR code too.

import Screenshot from "../../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Payment instructions** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Payment instructions** under **Money & tax**.

2. **Write how customers can pay** — In **How customers can pay**, enter your bank name, account number, routing or sort code, or whatever your customers need. Up to 600 characters. Leave it empty to hide the block.

3. **Add UPI (India only)** — When your currency is INR, enter your **UPI ID** (for example `riverstone@okhdfc`) and **Payee name**. Tick **Show a UPI QR code on invoices** to print a code for each invoice's balance due.

4. **Press Save** — A "Saved" message appears.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-payment-instructions" alt="Settings, Payment instructions: the How customers can pay box, its 600-character limit and Save" />

## Good to know

- **Only new invoices change.** "Issued invoices keep the instructions they were issued with — changing this only affects invoices issued from now on."
- **A QR code doesn't record the payment.** "Payments still need recording — or find them when you reconcile."
- **The UPI fields only show for INR.** Other currencies see the instructions box only.

For how the instructions look on an invoice, see [Invoices and credit notes](../../invoices/).
