**Settings** holds your own account and, for Owners, everything about how the business runs: its profile and branding, money and tax, documents and emails, sales and meetings, and your data. Open it from your user menu (your name in the sidebar) and choose **Settings**.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone sees **Your account**. Only Owners see the business's settings — managing settings is Owner-only, always. Staff and Field users see only Your account, and the business settings can't be changed from their sign-in.</Aside>

## Finding your way around

- **Groups.** The list is grouped: **Your account**, **Business**, **Money & tax**, **Documents & emails**, **Sales & meetings**, **Data** and **Team**. The Team group's **Users**, **Teams**, **Audit log** and **Email log** close Settings and open that page.
- **Search settings.** Type what you're after — "prefix", "logo", "signature", "SMTP". Spine finds panes by what's inside them and shows the matching phrase ("Matches: invoice prefix"). Press Enter to open the first result. If nothing matches you see "No matching settings." with **Clear search**.
- **Come back to the same place.** The address keeps the open pane, so refreshing or sharing the link opens it again.
- **Unsaved changes.** A pane you've changed but not saved shows a dot. Leaving it asks "Discard unsaved changes to …?" with **Keep editing** and **Discard**.
- **On a phone,** Settings fills the screen and shows the list first; **Back to settings** returns to it.
- **Switched-off modules** take their panes with them, so you only see settings for what you use.
