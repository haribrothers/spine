Pipeline stages are "The columns on your pipeline board. Rename, reorder or add stages. Won and Lost always stay last so reports can tell a closed lead from an open one." Changing them changes the board for everyone.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. The pane only shows while the **Leads & meetings** module is on (**Settings › Modules**).</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Pipeline stages** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Pipeline stages** under **Sales & meetings**. Each row shows the stage's name, its kind (**Open**, **Won** or **Lost**) and how many leads are in it.

2. **Rename a stage** — Type a new name in its row, for example change "Proposal" to "Quote sent". Its leads stay in it.

3. **Reorder** — Drag a stage by its handle to a new position, or use the up and down arrows on its row ("Move Proposal up").

4. **Add a stage** — Press **+ Add stage**, name it, and move it where it belongs. You can have up to 12 stages.

5. **Delete a stage** — Press the delete button on its row. If it still has leads, "Delete "…"?" asks where they go: choose a stage under "Move 3 leads to:" and press **Delete stage**. "Its leads aren't deleted — they move to the stage you choose when you save the stages."

6. **Press Save** — A "Saved" message appears and the board shows your stages.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-pipeline-stages" alt="Settings, Pipeline stages: a row per stage with its kind and lead count, + Add stage and Save" />

## Good to know

- **Won and Lost stay last.** "Won and Lost can be renamed but not moved or deleted."
- **Up to 12 stages.** With 12, **+ Add stage** is turned off.
- **Nothing changes until you save.** A deleted stage and its leads' move happen when you press **Save**.

For working leads on the board, see [Contacts and pipeline](../../contacts/).
