Your business profile is what customers see at the top of every quote, invoice, credit note and receipt, on the public quote page and in your emails. Keep it current here — Spine takes it from this pane each time it makes a new document.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Business profile** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Business profile** under **Business**. It shows your current details.

2. **Change your details** — Edit **Business name** (required), **Address**, **Business email** ("Shown to customers who need to reach you."), **Business phone** and **Industry** (what your business does, in your own words).

3. **Change your logo** — Click the logo square and pick an image up to 10 MB. When it's uploaded the square shows "Logo uploaded". A square logo looks sharpest.

4. **Press Save** — A "Saved" message appears.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-business-profile" alt="Settings, Business profile: business name, logo square, address, business email, phone and industry, with Save" />

## What changes, and what doesn't

- **New documents** — PDFs made from now on, and the public quote page, show the new name, address and logo.
- **Issued documents keep theirs.** An issued quote, invoice or receipt snapshots your details when it's issued, so its PDF keeps the logo and address it was issued with.
- **Your organisation's name** in Spine changes with the business name.

## If something goes wrong

- **Business name is empty** — saving is refused and the field shows an error. Enter a name and save again.
- **"Could not upload logo."** — the logo didn't upload, often because file storage isn't reachable. Try again; the other details still save without it.

<Aside type="tip">The colour and footer on your documents are in **Settings › Branding** — see [Choose your theme and document colour](../branding/). Tax registration numbers such as a GSTIN are part of your tax settings; see [Tax](../../tax/).</Aside>
