Spine uses one currency for every quote, invoice, bill and report. The locale decides how dates and numbers are written — ₹1,25,000.00 in India, $125,000.00 in the US. Both were set during setup; you can change them here.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Currency & locale** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Currency & locale** under **Business**. It says "Used for every document and report."

2. **Choose the currency** — Pick from **Common currencies** (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, INR, AED, SGD, NZD, ZAR) or type a **Currency** code. It's a three-letter ISO 4217 code, such as USD, EUR or INR.

3. **Choose the locale** — Pick from **Common locales** (for example en-US, en-GB or en-IN) or type a **Locale**. It "Controls date and number formatting."

4. **Press Save** — A "Saved" message appears. New documents and reports use the new settings.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-currency" alt="Settings, Currency & locale: the currency and locale fields with their common choices, and Save" />

## Good to know

- **One currency only.** Spine doesn't do multi-currency. Pick the currency you invoice and keep your books in.
- **Issued documents keep their currency.** An issued quote or invoice keeps the amounts and currency it was issued with.
- **The locale is about how things look.** It doesn't change any amount, tax or date — only how they're written on screen and on documents.

<Aside type="caution">Set your currency before you issue your first documents. Changing it later doesn't convert any amounts already in your books.</Aside>

Your financial year is a separate setting: see [Set your financial year](../financial-year/).
