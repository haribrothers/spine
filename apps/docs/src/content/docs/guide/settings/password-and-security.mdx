**Password & security** lets you "Change your password and see where your account is signed in." If you've lost a phone or signed in on someone else's computer, sign that device out here.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone, for their own account.</Aside>

## Change your password

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Password & security** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Password & security** under **Your account**.

2. **Enter your passwords** — Under **Change password**, fill in **Current password**, **New password** and **Confirm new password**. Use at least 8 characters, and one you don't use anywhere else.

3. **Choose whether to sign out elsewhere** — Tick **Sign out of my other devices** to end every other session.

4. **Press Change password** — "Password changed." Use it the next time you sign in.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="settings-password-security" alt="Settings, Password & security: Change password and the list of devices you're signed in on" />

If your current password is wrong, you see "Couldn't change your password. Check your current password and try again."

## Sign out a device

**Where you're signed in** lists each device with when it was last active; yours is marked **This device**.

- **One device** — press **Sign out** on its row. Spine asks "Sign out …?" first; it'll need to sign in again.
- **Every other device** — press **Sign out of other devices**. Spine asks first and you stay signed in here.

<Aside type="tip">Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email.</Aside>
