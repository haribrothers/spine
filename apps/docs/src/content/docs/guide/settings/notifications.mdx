The Notifications pane covers "How Spine tells you about what needs you. These are yours; the business's defaults are named where they apply." Each setting here changes only your own notifications.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Everyone, for their own account.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Notifications** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Notifications** under **Your account**. Changes save as you make them.

2. **Browser notifications** — Press **Turn on** to get a notification on this device when something needs you, even when Spine isn't the tab you're looking at. Your browser asks for permission. **Turn off** stops it on this device only.

3. **Daily digest** — Turn on **Email me a summary each morning**: "What needs you today and what happened yesterday. Sent only on days with something to report." Choose **Send at** and **Days** (every day, or Monday to Friday).

4. **Meeting reminders** — Choose how long before a meeting you're attending we remind you: **Business default** (the Owner's setting, for example "Business default · 15 min"), **5 min**, **15 min**, **30 min** or **60 min**.

</Steps>

## If something doesn't work

- **"Blocked by the browser"** — you've blocked notifications for Spine. Re-enable them in your browser's site settings, then press **Turn on**.
- **"Browser notifications aren't configured on this Spine instance."** — whoever runs Spine hasn't set them up on the server yet.
- **"This browser doesn't support push notifications."** — try another browser.

Times are in your organisation's time zone. For the notification bell and what you're notified about, see [Notifications](../../notifications/).
