With automatic reminders on, "Spine emails customers about unpaid invoices on a schedule, so you don't have to chase." You choose when reminders go, relative to each invoice's due date.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open Settings › Automatic reminders** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, and pick **Automatic reminders** under **Documents & emails**.

2. **Turn on Send reminders automatically** — It's off by default and applies to every issued invoice with a balance due.

3. **Choose the schedule** — Under **Schedule**, tick any of **3 days before the due date**, **On the due date**, **7 days after the due date**, **14 days after the due date** and **Every 14 days after that, up to 3 more times**.

4. **Choose the time** — **Send at** sets the time of day, in your organisation's time zone.

5. **Preview the email** — **Preview email** shows the reminder for your most overdue invoice, or a sample if none is overdue.

6. **Press Save** — A "Saved" message appears. With reminders on and nothing ticked, you see "Pick at least one reminder."

</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Never more than one a day.** A customer gets at most one reminder a day for an invoice, whether it was sent automatically or by hand.
- **Paid invoices stop.** Nothing is sent once an invoice is paid, voided or credited in full.
- **Pause one customer.** You can turn reminders off for one customer on their contact page.
- **The words are yours.** Reminders use the **Payment reminder** email — see [Write the emails customers get](../emails/).

For sending a reminder by hand, see [Invoices and credit notes](../../invoices/).
