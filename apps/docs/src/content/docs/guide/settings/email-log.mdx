The email log lists "Every email Spine sends — to customers and your team — and what happened to it. Kept for 180 days." Use it to check that a customer got their invoice, or to find out why they didn't.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. Staff and Field users don't see the link, and get the no-access page at its address.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the email log** — In **Settings**, pick **Email log** under **Team**, or press **Email log** in **Settings › Emails**. From a quote's or invoice's "Email failed" message, **See it in the email log** opens it showing only that record's emails.

2. **Read the cards** — **Waiting** (queued or retrying), **Failed in the last 7 days** and **Sent today**. Press **Waiting** or **Failed in the last 7 days** to show only those emails; press it again to clear.

3. **Find an email** — Search the recipient, subject or record number, or filter by **Status**, **Kind** and **Show emails from** (last 7, 30, 90 or 180 days).

4. **Read the row** — Each email shows **When**, **To**, **Subject**, its kind, the **Record** it's about (a link), the **Status** — Queued, Retrying, Sending, Sent, Failed or Cancelled — the **Attempts** ("2 of 4") and, if it failed, the last error.

</Steps>

## Retry or cancel an email

Open an email's row menu.

- **Retry now** — for a failed or retrying quote, invoice, reminder or notification email. "Retry this email?" says who it goes to; **Retry email** queues it again with its attempts starting over.
- **Open** the record — for emails that were sent straight away (credit notes, receipts, refunds, statements, invitations, password resets, tests, digests, data exports). Send it again from the record.
- **Cancel** — for an email that's still waiting. "Cancel this email?" says it won't be sent; **Cancel email** stops it and the row shows who cancelled it.

An email that's sending or already sent can't be cancelled.

<Aside type="caution">If email isn't set up, the log warns "Email isn't set up" with how many are waiting. Those emails aren't tried and use no attempts; they go out once email is set up and Spine restarts. If emails keep failing, the warning reads "Emails are failing" with the last error. See [Email](../../../self-hosting/email/).</Aside>
