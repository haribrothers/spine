The AI assistant helps fill in forms. It reads a vendor's bill or a receipt into the bill or expense form, drafts the email that goes with a quote or invoice, drafts quote lines from a customer's enquiry and suggests bank matches while you reconcile. It never saves, sends or posts anything by itself: you check what it filled in and press the normal button.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners switch AI on and set it up, in **Settings › AI assistant**. Then Owners, Staff whose Team has **Use AI features**, and Field users reading their own receipts can use it. It's off on a new Spine.</Aside>

## The main ideas

- **Off means off.** With AI switched off, no AI button, suggestion or prompt appears anywhere, and Spine sends nothing to any AI service.
- **Your provider, your choice.** Use a model on your own network, so nothing leaves it, or a cloud service such as Anthropic Claude with your business's own API key.
- **You see what's sent.** Each feature lists exactly what it sends. Bank account numbers, tax IDs and GSTINs, UPI IDs, passwords and keys are never sent.
- **Marked until you change it.** Everything AI fills in says **Suggested by AI** until you edit it. AI only fills empty fields; it offers **Replace my entries** for the rest.
- **Forms work without it.** If the provider is down, slow or the budget is used up, you fill the form in by hand as usual.
- **A budget you set.** Cap the tokens used each month so a cloud bill never surprises you.
- **Recorded.** Every AI request is in the audit log, without the text of the request or reply.

## When AI doesn't answer

If the provider doesn't answer within 30 seconds, the control says "AI didn't answer. Fill this in by hand or try again." with **Try again**. What you've typed stays, and you can save the form by hand. A request you cancel fills nothing. If AI returns a value Spine can't use, that field stays empty and the rest fills in.
