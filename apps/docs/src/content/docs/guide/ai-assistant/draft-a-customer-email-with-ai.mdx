When you send a document, the email starts from your template. With AI on, you can ask for a draft in better words. AI writes the words; Spine fills in every detail — the customer's name, the number, amounts and dates — so a draft can never carry a wrong figure. Nothing is sent until you press **Send**.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Use AI features** and the right to send that email. AI and **Draft customer emails** must be on in **Settings › AI assistant**, and email must be set up.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the send dialog** — Send a quote, invoice, payment reminder, statement, payment receipt or credit note as usual. Subject and Message start from your template. The details Spine filled in are highlighted on screen.

2. **Press Draft with AI** — It sits between **To** and **Subject**. While it works, the button shows "Drafting…".

3. **Read the draft** — It appears above your email as **AI's draft** — "check it, then use it. Your email below doesn't change until you do." — with its subject and message.

4. **Use it, or not** — Press **Use this draft** to put its words into Subject and Message, **Try again** for another draft, or **Discard**.

5. **Check and send** — Fields from the draft are tagged **Drafted by AI** until you change them. "Using AI's draft — check it before sending." has **Undo** to put back what was there. Edit anything you like, then press **Send**.

</Steps>

## Choose the tone of a reminder or statement

For a payment reminder or a statement — from an invoice or from Receivables — choose a **Tone** beside **Draft with AI**: **Friendly** (to start with), **Formal** or **Firm**. Choosing another tone on a draft drafts again in it. The amounts, dates and invoice numbers stay exactly as in Spine; the balance is always Spine's.

<Aside type="tip">A firm reminder never threatens or adds fees, interest, legal action or deadlines your template doesn't have.</Aside>

## What to know

- The email is sent exactly as it is in the dialog and appears in the email log like any other.
- If a draft names a detail Spine doesn't have, or a number that isn't the document's, it's refused with "AI didn't answer. Fill this in by hand or try again."
- **Draft with AI** isn't offered for remittance advice or other emails, or when email isn't set up.
