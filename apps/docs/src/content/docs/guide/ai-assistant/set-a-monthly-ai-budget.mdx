A monthly budget caps how much AI your business uses, so a cloud provider's bill never surprises you. AI pauses for everyone when the month's tokens are used up, and starts again on the first of the next month.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the AI settings** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, then **AI assistant**, and find **Monthly budget**.

2. **Enter the budget** — Type a whole number of tokens in **Tokens a month**. Leave it empty for no limit.

3. **Save** — Press **Save**. The toast says "Monthly budget saved" and when AI will pause, or that AI has no monthly limit.

</Steps>

## See this month's usage

Under **This month**, the pane shows the tokens used — against your budget, with the percentage in words — and how many requests were made. **Top features** and **Top people** show where most of it went. Before anyone uses AI, it says "No AI requests yet this month."

<Aside type="tip">Tokens are how AI providers measure and charge for use. Reading a long bill uses more than drafting a short email. Watch the usage for a month or two before you settle on a budget.</Aside>

## When the budget runs low

- **At 80%** — Owners get a notification: "AI has used 80% of this month's budget." It's sent once a month.
- **At 100%** — AI controls stay on screen but are disabled, with "AI is paused until 1 December — this month's budget is used up." Forms work as they do without AI. Owners are notified once.

To start AI again before the month ends, raise the budget and save.

## Too many requests

One person can make up to 30 AI requests in 10 minutes. After that, they see "Too many AI requests. Try again in a few minutes."

## Budget changes are recorded

Every change to the budget is in the audit log, under the AI settings. See [Check how AI is used](../check-how-ai-is-used/).
