When you reconcile a bank account, Spine's rules suggest matches for most lines. For the odd ones they can't settle, AI can suggest which entry a line matches, or what to create for it, with its reason. You still match or create each one yourself.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Owners, and Staff whose Team has **Use AI features** and **Reconcile accounts**. AI and **Suggest bank matches** must be on in **Settings › AI assistant**.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the reconciliation** — Start or continue reconciling the account in **Banking**.

2. **Press Suggest with AI** — Above the statement panel, "Suggest with AI (4 lines)" counts the lines the rules couldn't settle. AI only runs when you press it, so a long statement doesn't use your budget unasked.

3. **Look at the Suggested tab** — AI's suggestions appear marked **AI suggests**, with the reason — for example "same amount; description mentions Maple Ridge".

4. **Match or skip each one** — On a line, "AI suggests: *entry* · *contact*" has **Match** and **Not this one**. When you select the line, AI's entry comes first in the entries panel, before the same amount and nearest dates.

5. **Create what's missing** — "AI suggests creating: *category* · *contact*" has **Create** and **Exclude**. **Create** opens the form with the suggested kind, category and contact filled in, marked **Suggested by AI**. Check it and save.

</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Match all suggested** only matches the rules' suggestions. AI suggestions aren't included — check and match each one.
- **Ignoring a suggestion** matches or creates nothing.
- A line matched from AI's suggestion reads "Matched · suggested by AI".
- **Suggest again** replaces the suggestions with new ones. AI looks at up to 30 lines at a time.
- AI is sent the lines' dates, amounts and descriptions, with account numbers and IDs removed.

<Aside type="tip">AI suggestions are kept on the reconciliation, so you can come back to them later. See [Banking](../../banking/) for the rest of reconciling.</Aside>
