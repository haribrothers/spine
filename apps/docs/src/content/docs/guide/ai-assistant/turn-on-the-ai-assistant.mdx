AI is off until an Owner connects a provider and switches it on. You choose where AI requests go: a model running on your own network, or a cloud service paid for with your business's own key.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can do this">Only Owners. Staff and Field don't see **AI assistant** in Settings.</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the AI settings** — Open your user menu, choose **Settings**, then **AI assistant** under **Business**. Searching settings for "AI", "provider" or "model" finds it too.

2. **Choose a provider** — Under **Provider**, pick:
   - **Local or OpenAI-compatible** — a model on your own network (such as Ollama or vLLM) or any OpenAI-compatible service. Enter the **Server address**, for example `http://192.168.1.20:11434/v1`, and an **API key (optional)** if your server asks for one.
   - **Anthropic Claude** — enter your business's **API key**.

3. **Choose the model** — **Model** lists the models the provider reports. You can type one it doesn't list.

4. **Test the connection** — Press **Test connection**. It shows "Connected · *model* · reads images: yes" (or no). If it can't connect, it says why and asks you to check the address and key.

5. **Save** — Press **Save**. The toast says "AI provider saved".

6. **Switch AI on** — Use the switch at the top of the pane. For a provider outside your own network, "Turn on AI?" says business data will be sent to that host when people use AI features, and lists what each feature sends. Press **Turn on AI**. The toast says "AI assistant is on".

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="ai-assistant-settings" alt="Settings, AI assistant: the on/off switch and the Provider card with Local or OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic Claude" />

If you switch AI on before saving a provider, Spine says "Connect a provider first." and the switch stays off.

## About the API key

The key is stored encrypted. It's never shown again, written to logs or included in data exports. After saving, it shows only its last four characters, with **Replace key**.

<Aside type="caution">A model that can't read images can still read PDFs with text, but not photos or scans. The pane notes this under **Read bills and receipts**.</Aside>

## Switch AI off

Use the same switch. "Turn off AI?" says AI buttons disappear for everyone and nothing already saved changes. Press **Turn off AI**.

The **AI assistant** row in **Settings › Modules** shows the same setting — "Off" or "On" with the provider's host — and **Set up in AI assistant** opens this pane.

## Changing the provider later

Changing the provider, model or key is recorded in the audit log, without the key. If you move AI to a cloud host while it's on, Spine asks "Send AI requests here?" first; press **Use this provider**.
