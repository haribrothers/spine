## Set up your business

After you create your account, onboarding asks for your business details, country and tax, currency, financial year and document numbering. Skip anything you're not sure of: **Set up Spine** (from the dashboard's checklist, or your account menu) shows what's left, and each card opens the right setting.

:::tip[Explore first]
Not ready to enter your own data? Load a demo business from onboarding, or later in **Settings › Data › Load demo data**, and delete it when you're done. Your own records are never touched.
:::

## Issue your first invoice

1. **Contacts › Add contact.** Add a customer with their email address.
2. **Invoices › New invoice.** Pick the customer, add a line (from your catalogue, or typed in), and **Save draft**.
3. **Issue invoice.** Spine asks first, because an issued invoice can't be edited: it gets its permanent number and posts to your books.
4. **Email** sends it to the customer as a PDF. When they pay, **Record payment** on the invoice.

## Then

- **Invite your team:** Team › Users › **Invite people**. Office staff get rights through Teams; field crew use the phone app.
- **Set up email and backups** on the server: see [Email](../../self-hosting/email/) and [Backups](../../self-hosting/backups/).
- Read the [user guide](../../guide/) for each part of Spine.
