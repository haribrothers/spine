The sixth beta of Spine 1.0. The web app now has a layout made for phones, so on a phone it works like a phone app instead of a squeezed desktop. On a tablet or computer nothing changes.

## Before you upgrade

- Nothing to change. `./upgrade.sh --version 1.0.0-beta.6` backs up first, as always. On a Docker panel, set `SPINE_VERSION=1.0.0-beta.6` (or paste this release's `docker-compose.panels.yml`) and redeploy; on Coolify, paste this release's `docker-compose.coolify.yml`.

## New: Spine on your phone

- **Getting around:** tabs along the bottom (Home, Sales, Jobs, Money, More) and a top bar with the page's title, a back arrow, search and notifications. **More** opens the whole menu.
- **Lists:** one row per record, with its number, status, who it's for and the amount. The totals are chips you can swipe, filters are in a **Filters** sheet, the main action (such as **New invoice**) is a round button above the tabs, and **Select** lets you act on several at once.
- **Records:** the number, status and the amount that matters at the top, the next step as a wide button at the bottom, and everything else under ⋯.
- **Quotes and invoices:** build them full screen, edit each line in a sheet, and see the total in a bar at the bottom.
- **Calendar:** a month with a dot on each busy day and that day's meetings underneath, a week strip over the day, and swiping between them.
- **Reports and the dashboard:** reports read as lists you can tap into, and the dashboard puts what needs you first.
- **Search:** fills the screen, with room to type and the areas to search as pills.
- **Home screen:** add Spine to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app, without the browser around it. On an iPhone you sign in once inside it.

The guide's new page, **Use Spine on your phone**, covers all of it.

## Fixed

- **The dashboard's greeting** no longer shows an error when the server's time zone differs from yours (common with Docker, which runs in UTC). It now follows your device's clock.
