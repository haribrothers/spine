# 21 · AI assistant

An optional assistant that helps fill in forms across Spine: it reads a vendor's bill or a receipt into the bill or expense form, drafts customer emails, drafts quote lines from an enquiry and suggests bank matches. It's off until an Owner switches it on and connects a provider — a model on the business's own network or a cloud service with the business's own key. AI only ever pre-fills what a person has open; nothing is saved, sent or posted until the person presses the normal button ([D-162](../prd/12-decisions.md)–[D-173](../prd/12-decisions.md)). Off means no AI anywhere. The bigger AI features follow in v1.1 ([16-roadmap](../prd/16-roadmap.md)).

**Screens:** `SettingsAi` (P1), `TeamPrivilegesBooks` (P1), `OrgAuditLog` (P2), `VendorBillBuilderAi` (P1), `ExpenseDialogAi` (P1), `MobExpenseNewAi` (P1), `MobExpenseNewAiAndroid` (P1), `EmailDraftAi` (P1), `QuoteBuilderAi` (P1), `ReconcileAiSuggestions` (P1)
**Depends on:** 16 Settings (Modules, D-154), 15 Team (capabilities), 10 Purchases (bills, expenses), 06 Quotes, 08 Invoicing and 09 Payments (emails), 20 Banking (reconciliation), 17 Mobile (receipt capture), 18 Ops (files, audit).

## User stories

### US-AI-01 · Switch the AI assistant on or off
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** one switch for all AI in Spine, **so that** I decide whether my business uses AI at all.

Decision: D-162, D-164

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** Settings › AI, **when** it opens on a new install, **then** "AI assistant" is off and the pane reads "AI helps fill in forms — reading bills and receipts, drafting emails and quote lines, suggesting bank matches. It never saves, sends or posts anything by itself.".
- **Given** I switch it on, **when** no provider is connected, **then** I see "Connect a provider first." and the switch stays off.
- **Given** a cloud provider (any address outside the local network), **when** I switch AI on, **then** I confirm "Business data will be sent to <provider host> when people use AI features. What each feature sends is listed below." with "Turn on AI"; the confirmation is recorded in the audit log.
- **Given** AI is off, **when** anyone uses Spine, **then** no AI button, suggestion or prompt appears anywhere, AI routes answer 403 `MODULE_OFF`, and the server makes no request to the provider (network test).
- **Given** a Staff user, **when** they call the settings route, **then** the API returns 403.

**Implementation notes**
- AI is the fifth module in `packages/shared/src/modules.ts` (D-154); `settings.ai.enabled`. API: `GET/PUT /org/ai` (roles: owner).

### US-AI-02 · Connect a local or cloud AI provider
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** to use a model on my own network or a cloud service with my own key, **so that** I choose where my data goes and who I pay.

Decision: D-163

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "Provider", **when** I open it, **then** it offers "Local or OpenAI-compatible" (asks for "Server address", e.g. `http://192.168.1.20:11434/v1`, "Model" and an optional "API key") and "Anthropic Claude" (asks for "API key" and "Model").
- **Given** I press "Test connection", **when** the provider answers, **then** I see "Connected · <model> · reads images: yes/no"; when it doesn't, "Couldn't reach <address>: <reason>. Check the address and key, then try again.".
- **Given** a saved key, **when** the pane reopens, **then** the key shows as "••••1a2b" with "Replace key"; it's never returned by the API, written to logs or included in data exports.
- **Given** the model can't read images, **when** the pane shows, **then** "Read bills and receipts" notes "Needs a model that can read images. PDFs with text still work.".

**Implementation notes**
- `AiDriver` with `none`, `openai-compatible`, `anthropic` drivers in `apps/api/src/modules/ai/drivers/`. API: `POST /org/ai/test` (roles: owner). Key encrypted with an `AUTH_SECRET`-derived key; secrets rule in [07-security](../prd/07-security.md).

### US-AI-03 · Choose which AI features are on
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** a switch per AI feature and to see what each sends, **so that** I allow only what I'm comfortable with.

Decision: D-162, D-165

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** AI is on, **when** the pane shows, **then** it lists "Read bills and receipts", "Draft customer emails", "Draft quote lines" and "Suggest bank matches", each with a switch (on by default) and "What's sent".
- **Given** "What's sent" on a feature, **when** I open it, **then** it lists exactly what that feature sends (e.g. for emails: "the email template, the document's number, amounts and dates, and the customer's name") and states "Never sent: bank account numbers, tax IDs and GSTINs, UPI IDs, passwords or keys.".
- **Given** a feature switched off, **when** anyone works in its screen, **then** its AI control isn't shown and its route answers 403 `{ error: "AI_FEATURE_OFF" }`.

**Implementation notes**
- `settings.ai.features` `{ readDocuments, draftEmails, draftQuoteLines, bankSuggestions }`. The redaction step (D-165) lives in `apps/api/src/modules/ai/redact.ts` with unit tests per pattern.

### US-AI-04 · Set a monthly AI budget and see usage
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** to cap how much AI we use each month, **so that** a cloud bill never surprises me.

Decision: D-168

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "Monthly budget", **when** I enter a number of tokens (or leave it empty for no limit), **then** usage this month shows as tokens used, requests and a bar against the budget, with the top three features and people.
- **Given** usage reaches 80%, **when** the next request completes, **then** Owners get "AI has used 80% of this month's budget." once that month.
- **Given** usage reaches 100%, **when** anyone uses an AI control, **then** it's disabled with "AI is paused until <first of next month> — this month's budget is used up." and forms work as without AI.
- **Given** a person sends more than 30 AI requests in 10 minutes, **when** they try again, **then** they see "Too many AI requests. Try again in a few minutes." (429 `AI_RATE_LIMIT`).

**Implementation notes**
- API: `GET /org/ai/usage?month=` (roles: owner). Data: `settings.ai.monthlyTokenBudget`; usage summed from `ai_requests`. Events `ai_budget_warning`, `ai_budget_reached` (Owners).

### US-AI-05 · Let a Team use AI
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `TeamPrivilegesBooks`
**As** an owner, **I want** AI as a Team right, **so that** only the people I choose use it.

Decision: D-167

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a Team's privileges, **when** AI is on, **then** an "AI assistant" group lists "Use AI features" (`ai.use`), off for General Staff by default.
- **Given** a Staff member without `ai.use`, **when** they open a bill, expense, quote, email or reconciliation, **then** no AI control shows and the AI routes answer 403.
- **Given** a Field user, **when** they photograph a receipt on the phone with AI on, **then** they can use "Read receipt" on their own expense only; every other AI route answers 403.
- **Given** a person using AI, **when** AI reads data for a suggestion (e.g. candidate catalogue items), **then** it reads through the same services and permission checks as that person.

**Implementation notes**
- Capability `ai.use` in `CAPABILITIES`, `CAPABILITY_LABELS`, `DEFAULT_TEAM_GRANTS` (✗) with migration; `ai.read_own_receipt` in `FIELD_OWN_CAPABILITIES`. The group is hidden while the AI module is off (US-SET-24).

### US-AI-06 · See every AI request in the audit log
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `OrgAuditLog`
**As** an owner, **I want** a record of who used AI on what, **so that** I can check how it's used.

Decision: D-169

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the organisation audit log, **when** I filter by "AI", **then** each request shows who, when, the feature, the record it was for, the provider and model, tokens and whether it succeeded.
- **Given** an AI request, **when** it's logged, **then** the prompt and the reply aren't stored.
- **Given** a record saved after AI filled it in, **when** I open its history, **then** it shows "Filled in with AI by <name>".

**Implementation notes**
- Data: `ai_requests` (new); `ai_assisted` on `vendor_bills`, `expenses`, `quotes`; audit entity type `ai_request`.

### US-AI-07 · Keep working when AI is unavailable
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `VendorBillBuilderAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** forms to work when the AI provider is down, **so that** AI never blocks my work.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the provider doesn't answer within 30 seconds, **when** I use an AI control, **then** I see "AI didn't answer. Fill this in by hand or try again." and the form keeps what I entered.
- **Given** the provider is down, **when** I save the form by hand, **then** it saves normally.
- **Given** a request is running, **when** I keep typing in the form, **then** nothing I typed is overwritten; AI only fills empty fields and offers "Replace my entries" for the rest.

**Implementation notes**
- API errors: 503 `AI_UNAVAILABLE`, 504 on timeout; no retries from the server for interactive requests.

### US-AI-08 · Fill in a vendor bill from the vendor's PDF or photo
**Feature:** F-AI-02 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `VendorBillBuilderAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** Spine to read the vendor's bill into the form, **so that** I check it instead of typing it.

Decision: D-166, D-170

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "New vendor bill" with AI on, **when** I drop the vendor's PDF or photo on "Read from document", **then** it's attached to the bill (US-OPS-33) and within a few seconds the vendor, bill number, date, due date, currency and lines (description, quantity, unit price, tax) are filled in, each marked "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** the vendor name matches a contact, **when** it fills in, **then** that vendor is selected; otherwise "New vendor: <name>" is offered and nothing is created until I confirm.
- **Given** lines, **when** they fill in, **then** the expense account comes from that vendor's last bill or the matched catalogue item, the tax rate is matched to one of my rates by percentage, and the totals are computed by Spine.
- **Given** the document's total differs from Spine's computed total, **when** it fills in, **then** I see "The document says <amount>; the lines add up to <amount>. Check the lines before saving.".
- **Given** I press "Save as draft", **when** it saves, **then** the bill saves normally with `ai_assisted` set; nothing was saved before.
- **Given** an image and a model that can't read images, **when** I drop it, **then** I see the message from D-170 and the file is still attached.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/read-document { fileId, target: "vendor_bill" }` → `DocumentSuggestion` (`ai.use`, feature `readDocuments`). Matching vendor, rates and accounts happens in the API, not the model. Fixture tests with sample bills.

### US-AI-09 · Fill in an expense from a receipt
**Feature:** F-AI-02 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `ExpenseDialogAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** a receipt photo to fill in the expense, **so that** expenses take seconds.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "New expense" with AI on, **when** I add the receipt and press "Read receipt", **then** date, amount, tax, the vendor or merchant and a suggested category are filled in, marked "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** a suggested category, **when** it's chosen, **then** it's one of my expense categories (by the merchant's past expenses, else by name); otherwise the field stays empty.
- **Given** I submit, **when** it saves, **then** the expense saves normally with `ai_assisted`, and approval works as before.

**Implementation notes**
- Same route with `target: "expense"`.

### US-AI-10 · Fill in my expense from a receipt on the phone
**Feature:** F-AI-02 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `MobExpenseNewAi`, `MobExpenseNewAiAndroid`
**As** field crew, **I want** my receipt photo to fill in my expense, **so that** I don't type on site.

Decision: D-167

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** I photograph a receipt on the New expense screen while online and AI is on, **when** I tap "Read receipt", **then** amount, date, tax and the merchant are filled in, marked "Suggested by AI", and I can change them before submitting.
- **Given** I'm offline, **when** I take the photo, **then** "Read receipt" is shown disabled with "Needs a connection — fill it in, or read it later." and the expense queues as today (US-MOB-13).
- **Given** a queued expense I filled by hand, **when** it syncs, **then** AI changes nothing.

**Implementation notes**
- Mobile calls `POST /ai/read-document { fileId, target: "expense" }` after uploading the photo; Field passes only for their own draft expense (`ai.read_own_receipt`). Native controls follow [native-notes](../design/native-notes.md).

### US-AI-11 · Draft a quote or invoice email with AI
**Feature:** F-AI-03 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `EmailDraftAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** AI to write the email that goes with a quote or invoice, **so that** it reads well without effort.

Decision: D-171

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the send dialog for a quote or invoice, **when** I press "Draft with AI", **then** the subject and message are rewritten from the email template and the document's facts, and the result shows "Drafted by AI — check it before sending.".
- **Given** a draft, **when** I don't like it, **then** "Try again" gives another and "Undo" restores the template text.
- **Given** the draft, **when** I press "Send", **then** it sends exactly what's in the dialog; nothing is sent by AI.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/draft-email { type: quote|invoice|reminder|statement|receipt|credit_note, documentId, tone? }` → `{ subject, body }` (`ai.use`, feature `draftEmails`; the caller must be allowed to send that document).

### US-AI-12 · Draft a payment reminder or statement email in a chosen tone
**Feature:** F-AI-03 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `EmailDraftAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** reminders in the right tone, **so that** I can be firm with late payers and gentle with good customers.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a reminder or statement email, **when** I choose "Friendly", "Formal" or "Firm" and press "Draft with AI", **then** the message changes tone and keeps the amounts, dates and invoice numbers exactly as in Spine.
- **Given** a draft, **when** it mentions an amount, **then** the amount is the balance from Spine (placeholders are filled by Spine after drafting, not by the model).

**Implementation notes**
- Placeholders stay in the model's output and are filled by the template engine (D-153).

### US-AI-13 · Draft quote lines from an enquiry
**Feature:** F-AI-04 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `QuoteBuilderAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** to paste an enquiry and get draft lines, **so that** I start a quote in minutes.

Decision: D-172

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the quote builder with AI on, **when** I open "Draft lines with AI", paste an enquiry or describe the work and press "Draft lines", **then** proposed lines appear in a review list with quantity, the matched catalogue item or bundle, its price and "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** a proposed line with no catalogue match, **when** it shows, **then** its price is empty and marked "Add a price".
- **Given** the review list, **when** I tick lines and press "Add to quote", **then** only those lines are added to the builder; nothing else changes and the quote isn't saved until I save it.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/draft-quote-lines { text, currency }` → `{ lines: [{ catalogItemId?, bundleId?, name, description, quantity }] }`; candidates from trigram search (D-095), max 50, read with the caller's permissions.

### US-AI-14 · Get AI suggestions while reconciling
**Feature:** F-AI-05 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 9 · **Screens:** `ReconcileAiSuggestions`
**As** a bookkeeper, **I want** AI to suggest matches and categories the rules can't, **so that** reconciling the odd lines is quick.

Decision: D-173

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a reconciliation with AI on, **when** a statement line has several possible entries, **then** the most likely one is shown first with "AI suggests" and the reason (e.g. "same amount; description mentions Maple Ridge").
- **Given** an unmatched line, **when** AI has a suggestion, **then** "Create" opens prefilled with the suggested kind, category and contact, marked "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** a suggestion, **when** I ignore it, **then** nothing is matched or created; only "Match" or saving the create drawer does that.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/bank-suggestions { reconciliationId }` → `[{ statementLineId, rankedJournalLineIds, create?: { kind, accountId, contactId, reason } }]` (`ai.use` + `banking.reconcile`, feature `bankSuggestions`). Sends date, amount and redacted description only.
