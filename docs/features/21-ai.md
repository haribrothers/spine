# 21 · AI assistant

An optional assistant that helps fill in forms across Spine: it reads a vendor's bill or a receipt into the bill or expense form, drafts customer emails, drafts quote lines from an enquiry and suggests bank matches. It's off until an Owner switches it on and connects a provider — a model on the business's own network or a cloud service with the business's own key. AI only ever pre-fills what a person has open; nothing is saved, sent or posted until the person presses the normal button ([D-162](../prd/12-decisions.md)–[D-173](../prd/12-decisions.md)). Off means no AI anywhere. The bigger AI features follow in v1.1 ([16-roadmap](../prd/16-roadmap.md)).

The stories were fitted to the screens as the refinements before Phase 10 left them — grouped Settings, Team permissions as a draft, the bill builder page, the expense form, the shared send dialog, the compact quote builder, the reconcile tabs and each record's activity — without changing what AI does ([D-242](../prd/12-decisions.md)).

**Screens:** `SettingsAi` (P1), `SettingsAiDark` (P1), `SettingsAiTurnOnConfirm` (P1), `SettingsModulesAi` (P1), `TeamPagePermissionsAi` (P1), `AuditLogAi` (P2), `BillActivityAi` (P2), `VendorBillBuilderAi` (P1), `VendorBillBuilderAiDark` (P1), `VendorBillBuilderAiError` (P1), `ExpenseFormAi` (P1), `MobExpenseNewAi` (P1), `MobExpenseNewAiAndroid` (P1), `SendEmailDialogAi` (P1), `QuoteBuilderAi` (P1), `QuoteBuilderAiDark` (P1), `ReconcileAiSuggestions` (P1)
**Depends on:** 16 Settings (grouped Settings, Modules — D-154, D-236), 15 Team (permissions draft, sections — D-234, D-235), 10 Purchases (bill builder, expense form), 06 Quotes (compact builder), 08 Invoicing and 09 Payments (the send dialog, Receivables), 20 Banking (reconcile), 17 Mobile (receipt capture), 18 Ops (files, audit, activity).

## Shared rules

These hold for every AI control below, so the stories don't repeat them:

- **Marked until changed.** Every field or row AI fills shows "Suggested by AI" (a Sparkles icon and the words — never colour alone) until the person changes it; the marker then goes.
- **Empty fields only.** AI fills only fields that are empty; when it has a value for a field the person already filled, it offers "Replace my entries (<n>)" instead of overwriting.
- **Working state.** While a request runs, its button shows a spinner and "Reading…" or "Drafting…" with "Cancel"; the rest of the form stays usable. Reduced motion shows the words without the spinner.
- **Hidden, not disabled, when not allowed.** With AI off, the feature off, or the person without the right, the control isn't rendered. Only a paused budget (US-AI-04) or no connection on the phone (US-AI-10) shows it disabled, with why.
- **Nothing saved by AI.** The form's own buttons save, record, submit or send — with their own confirms (D-218) and toasts (D-223); leaving a page with AI-filled values that aren't saved asks "Leave without saving?" like any other edit.
- **Menu actions** that start AI carry the Sparkles icon from `lib/menu-icons.ts` (D-222). Every AI screen works in light and dark mode.

## User stories

### US-AI-01 · Switch the AI assistant on or off
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`, `SettingsAiDark`, `SettingsAiTurnOnConfirm`, `SettingsModulesAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** one switch for all AI in Spine, **so that** I decide whether my business uses AI at all.

Decision: D-162, D-164, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** an Owner opens Settings, **when** the list shows, **then** "AI assistant" sits in the Business group after Modules (US-SET-31), and searching settings for "AI", "provider", "model", "budget" or "receipts" finds it (US-SET-34). Staff and Field don't see it.
- **Given** Settings › AI assistant on a new install, **when** it opens, **then** the switch is off and the pane reads "AI helps fill in forms — reading bills and receipts, drafting emails and quote lines, suggesting bank matches. It never saves, sends or posts anything by itself.".
- **Given** Settings › Modules, **when** it shows, **then** "AI assistant" is its fifth module, with its state ("Off" or "On · <provider host>") and "Set up in AI assistant", which opens that pane; it's the same setting as the pane's switch, not a second one.
- **Given** no provider is connected, **when** I switch AI on, **then** I see "Connect a provider first." and the switch stays off.
- **Given** a provider outside the local network, **when** I switch AI on, **then** "Turn on AI?" asks first: "Business data will be sent to <provider host> when people use AI features. What each feature sends is listed below." with "Turn on AI"; confirming saves it, records the acknowledgement in the audit log and the toast says "AI assistant is on".
- **Given** AI is on, **when** I switch it off, **then** "Turn off AI?" asks first ("AI buttons disappear for everyone. Nothing already saved changes.") and the toast says "AI assistant is off".
- **Given** provider or budget edits I haven't saved, **when** I move to another pane or close Settings, **then** it asks before discarding them (US-SET-33); the pane works in the large dialog and on a phone with "Back to settings" (US-SET-32).
- **Given** AI is off, **when** anyone uses Spine, **then** no AI button, suggestion or prompt appears anywhere, AI routes answer 403 `MODULE_OFF`, and the server makes no request to the provider (network test).
- **Given** a Staff user, **when** they call the settings routes, **then** the API returns 403.

**Implementation notes**
- AI is the fifth module in `packages/shared/src/modules.ts` (D-154); `settings.ai.enabled`. Its Modules row links to the pane rather than switching there, because turning AI on needs a provider and, for a cloud one, the acknowledgement. API: `GET/PUT /org/ai` (roles: owner). The pane registers in `SETTINGS_SECTIONS` (group `business`) with its keywords.

### US-AI-02 · Connect a local or cloud AI provider
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** to use a model on my own network or a cloud service with my own key, **so that** I choose where my data goes and who I pay.

Decision: D-163

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "Provider", **when** I open it, **then** it offers "Local or OpenAI-compatible" (asks for "Server address", e.g. `http://192.168.1.20:11434/v1`, "Model" and an optional "API key") and "Anthropic Claude" (asks for "API key" and "Model").
- **Given** an address and key, **when** I open "Model", **then** it lists the models the provider reports, and I can type one it doesn't list.
- **Given** I press "Test connection", **when** the provider answers, **then** I see "Connected · <model> · reads images: yes/no"; when it doesn't, "Couldn't reach <address>: <reason>. Check the address and key, then try again.".
- **Given** a saved key, **when** the pane reopens, **then** the key shows as "••••1a2b" with "Replace key"; it's never returned by the API, written to logs or included in data exports.
- **Given** the model can't read images, **when** the pane shows, **then** "Read bills and receipts" notes "Needs a model that can read images. PDFs with text still work.".
- **Given** I change the provider, model or key, **when** I save, **then** the change is in the audit log (without the key) and the toast says "AI provider saved".

**Implementation notes**
- `AiDriver` with `openai-compatible` and `anthropic` drivers in `apps/api/src/modules/ai/drivers/` (`none` has no driver), over `fetch` through `AiHttp` (D-243). API: `POST /org/ai/test` and `POST /org/ai/models` (roles: owner). Key sealed in `settings.ai_provider_key` with an `AUTH_SECRET`-derived key; secrets rule in [07-security](../prd/07-security.md).

### US-AI-03 · Choose which AI features are on
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** a switch per AI feature and to see what each sends, **so that** I allow only what I'm comfortable with.

Decision: D-162, D-165

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** AI is on, **when** the pane shows, **then** it lists "Read bills and receipts" (new vendor bill, the expense form, the Field app), "Draft customer emails" (the send dialog), "Draft quote lines" (the quote builder) and "Suggest bank matches" (reconcile), each with a switch (on by default) and "What's sent".
- **Given** "What's sent" on a feature, **when** I open it, **then** it lists exactly what that feature sends (e.g. for emails: "the email template, the document's number, amounts and dates, and the customer's name") and states "Never sent: bank account numbers, tax IDs and GSTINs, UPI IDs, passwords or keys.".
- **Given** a feature switched off, **when** anyone works in its screen, **then** its AI control isn't shown and its route answers 403 `{ error: "AI_FEATURE_OFF" }`.

**Implementation notes**
- `settings.ai.features` `{ readDocuments, draftEmails, draftQuoteLines, bankSuggestions }`. The redaction step (D-165) lives in `apps/api/src/modules/ai/redact.ts` with unit tests per pattern. Every feature calls the provider through `AiRequestService.run`, which refuses a switched-off feature before anything is sent (D-244).

### US-AI-04 · Set a monthly AI budget and see usage
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SettingsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** to cap how much AI we use each month, **so that** a cloud bill never surprises me.

Decision: D-168

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** "Monthly budget", **when** I enter a number of tokens (or leave it empty for no limit), **then** usage this month shows as tokens used, requests and a bar against the budget (with the percentage in words, not colour alone), with the top three features and people.
- **Given** usage reaches 80%, **when** the next request completes, **then** Owners get "AI has used 80% of this month's budget." once that month.
- **Given** usage reaches 100%, **when** anyone sees an AI control, **then** it's disabled with "AI is paused until <first of next month> — this month's budget is used up." and forms work as without AI; Owners are notified once.
- **Given** a person sends more than 30 AI requests in 10 minutes, **when** they try again, **then** they see "Too many AI requests. Try again in a few minutes." (429 `AI_RATE_LIMIT`).

**Implementation notes**
- API: `GET /org/ai/usage?month=` (roles: owner); `GET /ai/status` has `pausedUntil`. Data: `settings.ai.monthlyTokenBudget`; usage summed from `ai_requests` in the organisation's time zone. Events `ai_budget_warning`, `ai_budget_reached` (Owners, once a month each, `settings.ai_budget_notices`).

### US-AI-05 · Let a Team use AI
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `TeamPagePermissionsAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** AI as a Team permission, **so that** only the people I choose use it.

Decision: D-167, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a Team's Permissions tab while AI is on, **when** it shows, **then** an "AI assistant" group lists "Use AI features" (`ai.use`), off for General Staff by default; switching it changes the draft like any other permission ("Changed", the bar, "Save <n> changes to <Team>?" naming it — US-TEAM-28), and the Team's history records it (US-TEAM-29).
- **Given** the Sections card and "What members will open", **when** they show, **then** AI isn't listed — it's a permission, not a section; using AI on a record also needs that section open and the right to change the record (e.g. reading a bill needs Open Vendor Bills and the right to create bills).
- **Given** a Staff member without `ai.use`, **when** they open a bill, expense, quote, send dialog or reconciliation, **then** no AI control shows and the AI routes answer 403.
- **Given** a Field user, **when** they photograph a receipt on the phone with AI on, **then** they can use "Read receipt" on their own expense only; every other AI route answers 403.
- **Given** a person using AI, **when** AI reads data for a suggestion (e.g. candidate catalogue items), **then** it reads through the same services and permission checks as that person.
- **Given** AI is switched off, **when** a Team's permissions show, **then** the group is hidden and its grants are kept for when it's back (US-SET-24, US-SET-25).

**Implementation notes**
- Capability `ai.use` in `CAPABILITIES`, `CAPABILITY_LABELS` (group "AI assistant"), `DEFAULT_TEAM_GRANTS` (✗) with migration `0127`. Listed in the AI module's `capabilities` so the module map hides it. Field's own-receipt right is a fixed rule in `AiRequestService` (`fieldOwnReceipt`), not a capability (D-245).

### US-AI-06 · See every AI request in the audit log
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `AuditLogAi`, `BillActivityAi`
**As** an owner, **I want** a record of who used AI on what, **so that** I can check how it's used.

Decision: D-169, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** Team › Audit log, **when** I choose the record type "AI request", **then** each row shows when, who, the action ("Read a document", "Drafted an email", "Drafted quote lines", "Suggested bank matches"), the record it was for (opens it) and, in Details, the provider and model, tokens and whether it succeeded.
- **Given** an AI request, **when** it's logged, **then** the prompt and the reply aren't stored.
- **Given** a vendor bill, expense or quote saved after AI filled it in, **when** I open its Activity, **then** the entry reads "Created as a draft · filled in with AI" (or "Edited · filled in with AI", "Submitted for approval · filled in with AI"), with who and when under it like every entry.
- **Given** AI settings change (on, off, provider, features, budget), **when** I look in the audit log, **then** each change is there under Settings, without the key.

**Implementation notes**
- Data: `ai_requests` (new); `ai_assisted` on `vendor_bills`, `expenses`, `quotes`; audit entity type `ai_request`. The `created`, `edited` and `submitted` activity entries of bills, expenses and quotes gain `aiAssisted`.

### US-AI-07 · Keep working when AI is unavailable
**Feature:** F-AI-01 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `VendorBillBuilderAiError`
**As** office staff, **I want** forms to work when the AI provider is down, **so that** AI never blocks my work.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the provider doesn't answer within 30 seconds, **when** I use an AI control, **then** an inline message by the control reads "AI didn't answer. Fill this in by hand or try again." with "Try again", and the form keeps what I entered.
- **Given** the provider is down, **when** I save the form by hand, **then** it saves normally.
- **Given** a request is running, **when** I keep typing in the form or press "Cancel", **then** nothing I typed is overwritten, and a cancelled request fills nothing.
- **Given** AI answers with something Spine can't use (a field that fails validation), **when** it fills in, **then** that field stays empty and the rest fills.

**Implementation notes**
- API errors: 503 `AI_UNAVAILABLE`, 504 on timeout; no retries from the server for interactive requests. Every AI reply is checked against a fixed schema before it reaches the form (R-23).

### US-AI-08 · Fill in a vendor bill from the vendor's PDF or photo
**Feature:** F-AI-02 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `VendorBillBuilderAi`, `VendorBillBuilderAiDark`
**As** office staff, **I want** Spine to read the vendor's bill into the bill page, **so that** I check it instead of typing it.

Decision: D-166, D-170, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** New vendor bill with AI on, **when** I press "Read from document" in the header card and choose or drop the vendor's PDF or photo, **then** it's attached to the bill (US-OPS-33) and the header card — Vendor, Bill number, Bill date, Due date, Currency and, for a GST business, Place of supply — and the line rows (Item, Qty, Rate, Tax, Account, with the description and HSN/SAC under each) fill in, each marked "Suggested by AI". A PDF or image already in Attachments offers "Read" too.
- **Given** the vendor name matches a contact, **when** it fills in, **then** that vendor is selected; otherwise "New vendor: <name>" is offered and nothing is created until I confirm (and only with the right to add contacts).
- **Given** lines, **when** they fill in, **then** each is matched to a catalogue item where one fits; the account comes from that vendor's last bill or the matched item, the tax rate is matched to one of my rates by percentage, and the totals in the bar are computed by Spine.
- **Given** the document's total differs from Spine's computed total, **when** it fills in, **then** I see "The document says <amount>; the lines add up to <amount>. Check the lines before saving.".
- **Given** the document has no due date, **when** the vendor is chosen, **then** the due date comes from that vendor's last bill as it does by hand, saying so (US-PUR-35).
- **Given** the bill number read matches one of this vendor's bills, **when** it fills in, **then** the duplicate warning shows with that bill's date, amount and link before anything is saved (US-PUR-35).
- **Given** I press "Save as draft" or "Save and record" (which asks "Record this bill?"), **when** it saves, **then** the bill saves normally with `ai_assisted` set; nothing was saved before.
- **Given** a draft bill's edit page, **when** I read a document, **then** only empty fields fill, with "Replace my entries (<n>)" for the rest.
- **Given** an image and a model that can't read images, **when** I read it, **then** I see the message from D-170 and the file is still attached.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/read-document { fileId, target: "vendor_bill" }` → `DocumentSuggestion` (`ai.use`, feature `readDocuments`, plus the bill create right). Matching the vendor, items, rates and accounts happens in the API, not the model. Fixture tests with sample bills.

### US-AI-09 · Fill in an expense from a receipt
**Feature:** F-AI-02 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `ExpenseFormAi`
**As** anyone who submits expenses, **I want** a receipt to fill in the expense, **so that** expenses take seconds.

Decision: D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the expense form with AI on and a receipt added (the first file, US-PUR-44), **when** I press "Read receipt" on its preview, **then** Date, Amount, Currency, Tax rate and Tax amount, Category and Description (the merchant and what was bought, e.g. "Bunnings — timber screws") fill in, marked "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** a suggested category, **when** it's chosen, **then** it's one of my categories — from this merchant's past expenses, else by the category's name and "What it's for" (US-PUR-48); otherwise Category stays empty.
- **Given** the receipt shows tax, **when** it fills in, **then** its tax amount is used and the rate matched by percentage; when it shows none, the category's default rate applies and the tax is worked out as included, "Worked out from <rate>" (US-PUR-44, US-PUR-48).
- **Given** I already submitted an expense with the same amount on the same day, **when** the read amount and date fill in, **then** the duplicate warning shows (US-PUR-44).
- **Given** the form is in Labour mode, **when** it shows, **then** "Read receipt" isn't offered.
- **Given** I press "Submit expense" or "Submit and add another", **when** it saves, **then** the expense saves normally with `ai_assisted` and approval works as before; the fresh form starts with nothing from AI.
- **Given** a rejected expense I'm fixing (US-PUR-45), **when** I read its receipt, **then** only empty fields fill, with "Replace my entries (<n>)" for the rest.

**Implementation notes**
- Same route with `target: "expense"`; the person needs the right to submit the expense (own expenses for Staff without "See everyone's expenses").

### US-AI-11 · Draft a customer email with AI
**Feature:** F-AI-03 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SendEmailDialogAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** AI to write the email that goes with a quote, invoice, receipt or credit note, **so that** it reads well without effort.

Decision: D-171, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the send dialog for a quote, invoice, reminder, statement, payment receipt or credit note, **when** AI and "Draft customer emails" are on and I may send it, **then** "Draft with AI" shows above the message.
- **Given** I press "Draft with AI", **when** the draft arrives, **then** the subject and message are rewritten from the email template and the document's facts, and the dialog shows "Drafted by AI — check it before sending.".
- **Given** a draft, **when** I don't like it, **then** "Try again" gives another and "Undo" restores the template text.
- **Given** the draft, **when** it keeps placeholders such as the balance, **then** Spine fills them when sending, and a placeholder Spine doesn't know is caught by the dialog's own check before it can send.
- **Given** the draft, **when** I press Send, **then** it sends exactly what's in the dialog and appears in the email log like any other email; nothing is sent by AI.
- **Given** email isn't set up (US-SET-29), **when** sending isn't offered, **then** neither is "Draft with AI". Remittance advice and other emails don't offer it in 1.0.

**Implementation notes**
- One change to the shared `SendEmailDialog`, used by quotes, invoices, credit notes, payments and Receivables. API: `POST /ai/draft-email { type: quote|invoice|reminder|statement|receipt|credit_note, documentId, tone? }` → `{ subject, body }` (`ai.use`, feature `draftEmails`; the caller must be allowed to send that document).

### US-AI-12 · Draft a payment reminder or statement email in a chosen tone
**Feature:** F-AI-03 · **Priority:** P2 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `SendEmailDialogAi`
**As** office staff, **I want** reminders in the right tone, **so that** I can be firm with late payers and gentle with good customers.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a reminder or statement email — from an invoice or from Receivables (US-PAY-28) — **when** I choose "Friendly", "Formal" or "Firm" and press "Draft with AI", **then** the message changes tone and keeps the amounts, dates and invoice numbers exactly as in Spine.
- **Given** a draft, **when** it mentions an amount, **then** the amount is the balance from Spine (placeholders are filled by Spine after drafting, not by the model).

**Implementation notes**
- The tone is a three-way toggle group beside "Draft with AI", shown only for reminders and statements. Placeholders stay in the model's output and are filled by the template engine (D-153).

### US-AI-13 · Draft quote lines from an enquiry
**Feature:** F-AI-04 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `QuoteBuilderAi`, `QuoteBuilderAiDark`
**As** office staff, **I want** to paste an enquiry and get draft lines, **so that** I start a quote in minutes.

Decision: D-172, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** the quote builder (new or draft) with AI on, **when** I press "Draft lines with AI" beside "Add item", **then** a side panel opens where I paste an enquiry or describe the work and press "Draft lines".
- **Given** the proposed lines, **when** they show, **then** each has its name, quantity, the matched catalogue item or bundle with its price, and "Suggested by AI"; a line with no catalogue match has no price and reads "Add a price".
- **Given** I tick lines and press "Add to quote", **when** they're added, **then** they arrive as compact rows after the last line (US-QUO-34), each description in the row under it and a bundle as its lines, marked "Suggested by AI"; nothing else changes, and the quote isn't saved until I save it.
- **Given** the panel, **when** I close it without adding, **then** the quote is unchanged.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/draft-quote-lines { text, currency }` → `{ lines: [{ catalogItemId?, bundleId?, name, description, quantity }] }`; candidates from trigram search (D-095), max 50, read with the caller's permissions. Needs `ai.use` and the right to create or edit the quote.

### US-AI-14 · Get AI suggestions while reconciling
**Feature:** F-AI-05 · **Priority:** P1 · **Phase:** 10 · **Screens:** `ReconcileAiSuggestions`
**As** a bookkeeper, **I want** AI to suggest matches and categories the rules can't, **so that** reconciling the odd lines is quick.

Decision: D-173, D-242

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Given** a reconciliation with AI on and lines the rules couldn't settle, **when** I press "Suggest with AI (<n> lines)" above the statement panel, **then** AI suggestions appear in the Suggested tab marked "AI suggests" with the reason (e.g. "same amount; description mentions Maple Ridge"), counted in the tab.
- **Given** I select a line with an AI suggestion, **when** the entries panel shows, **then** the suggested entry comes first, marked "AI suggests", before "Same amount" and the nearest dates (US-BANK-16).
- **Given** "Match all suggested (<n>)", **when** it shows, **then** it counts and matches only the rules' suggestions; with AI suggestions waiting, the Suggested tab says "AI suggestions aren't in Match all — check and match each one.".
- **Given** an unmatched line, **when** AI has a suggestion, **then** "Create" opens prefilled with the suggested kind, category and contact, marked "Suggested by AI".
- **Given** a suggestion, **when** I ignore it, **then** nothing is matched or created; only "Match" or saving the create drawer does that, and a match made from an AI suggestion is saved as suggested by AI.

**Implementation notes**
- API: `POST /ai/bank-suggestions { reconciliationId }` → `[{ statementLineId, rankedJournalLineIds, reason, create?: { kind, accountId, contactId, reason } }]` (`ai.use` + `banking.reconcile`, feature `bankSuggestions`), for the lines the matcher (D-158) left unsettled. Sends date, amount and redacted description only. Suggestions run when asked, not on opening the page, so a long statement doesn't spend the budget unasked. `bank_statement_line_matches.suggested_by = 'ai'`.
