**New: a repeat-quote-conversion safeguard (user-reported, post-Phase-3).** Neither `InvoicesService.convertFromQuote` nor `JobsService.createFromQuote` has ever had a one-shot restriction — a deliberate second conversion is a real use case (staged billing, splitting one quote across two jobs) — but with no total anywhere cross-checking a job's/invoice's amount against the source quote, an ACCIDENTAL repeat click silently doubles the billed/tracked amount.
